High-Protein Chickpea & Tofu Flour Scramble with Spinach and Sun-Dried Tomatoes

🥜 34g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 15 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 450 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 60 g Chickpea flour
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 200 g Spinach
  • 60 g Sun-dried tomatoes (oil-packed, drained)
  • 30 ml Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (Kala Namak)
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 60 ml Water
Instructions
1. Press the extra-firm tofu for at least 30 minutes to remove excess water, then crumble it into a bowl. 2. In a small bowl, whisk together chickpea flour, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, turmeric, black salt, and black pepper with 60ml of water until a smooth batter forms. 3. Heat the olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tofu and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly golden. 4. Pour the chickpea flour mixture over the tofu and stir well to coat. Cook for another 3-5 minutes, stirring, until the mixture starts to set and coats the tofu. 5. Add the spinach and chopped sun-dried tomatoes to the skillet. Cook for 3-5 minutes more, or until the spinach has wilted and the scramble is heated through. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Chili-Lime Tofu & Black Bean Quinoa Bowl with Roasted Corn, Bell Peppers, and Toasted Pumpkin Seeds

🥜 48.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Quinoa, uncooked
  • 450 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
  • 400 g Black beans, canned, rinsed and drained
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 1.5 cup Corn, frozen or fresh
  • 4 tbsp Pumpkin seeds, raw
  • 2 tbsp Lime juice
  • 30 ml Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 cup Water (for quinoa)
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Cook quinoa according to package directions; typically, combine 1 cup quinoa with 2 cups water, bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes until water is absorbed. 2. While quinoa cooks, dice the red bell pepper. On a baking sheet, toss the cubed tofu, bell pepper, and corn with 1 tablespoon olive oil, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Roast for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway, until vegetables are tender and tofu is lightly browned. 3. In a small dry skillet over medium heat, toast the pumpkin seeds for 2-3 minutes, stirring constantly, until lightly golden and fragrant. 4. In a large bowl, combine the cooked quinoa, roasted tofu and vegetables, rinsed black beans, and lime juice. Stir well. 5. Divide the mixture into two serving bowls and top each with toasted pumpkin seeds. Serve warm.

High-Protein Lentil & Seitan Shepherd's Pie with Sweet Potato Topping

🥜 72g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 50 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Seitan, sliced or cubed
  • 1 cup Brown or green lentils, dry
  • 2 whole Sweet potatoes, medium
  • 360 ml Vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1.5 cup Frozen mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn)
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 30 ml Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 0.5 tsp Dried rosemary
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 cup Water (for lentils)
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Wash and peel the sweet potatoes, then cut into chunks. Boil them in salted water for 15-20 minutes until very tender. Drain, mash with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper until smooth. Set aside. 2. While sweet potatoes cook, rinse the dry lentils. In a medium saucepan, combine the rinsed lentils with 2 cups of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes until tender. Drain any excess liquid. 3. In a large skillet or Dutch oven, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium heat. Add the seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes until lightly browned. 4. Stir in the cooked lentils, tomato paste, vegetable broth, frozen mixed vegetables, garlic powder, dried thyme, dried rosemary, salt, and black pepper. Bring to a simmer and cook for 5-7 minutes, allowing the sauce to thicken slightly. Stir in the nutritional yeast. 5. Transfer the seitan and lentil mixture to a small oven-safe casserole dish (approximately 8x8 inch). Evenly spread the mashed sweet potato topping over the lentil mixture. 6. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the topping is lightly golden and the filling is bubbly. Let cool for a few minutes before serving.

High-Protein Adzuki Bean & Quinoa Porridge with Savory Tofu Crumbles

🥜 44g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Quinoa, dry
  • 1.5 cup Water
  • 1.5 cup Cooked Adzuki Beans (canned, rinsed)
  • 300 g Extra-firm Tofu, pressed
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 2 cup Fresh Spinach
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Rinse quinoa thoroughly under cold water. Combine quinoa and 1.5 cups water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Let it sit covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 2. While quinoa cooks, crumble the pressed tofu into small pieces using your hands or a fork. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the crumbled tofu, tamari, smoked paprika, and garlic powder. 3. Cook the tofu for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it's golden brown and slightly crispy. Add the fresh spinach to the skillet and cook until wilted, about 2-3 minutes. Season with salt and black pepper. 4. In two bowls, divide the cooked quinoa. Top each bowl with the cooked adzuki beans and the savory tofu crumbles with spinach. Sprinkle with nutritional yeast before serving.

High-Protein Peanut Tofu & Edamame Noodle Bowl

🥜 48.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Extra-firm Tofu, pressed and cubed
  • 2 cup Shelled Edamame (frozen or fresh)
  • 150 g Whole Wheat Spaghetti or Soba Noodles
  • 4 tbsp Creamy Peanut Butter
  • 3 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 2 tbsp Rice Vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Maple Syrup
  • 1 tbsp Fresh Ginger, grated
  • 1 whole Red Bell Pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup Carrots, julienned or shredded
  • 3 cup Fresh Spinach
  • 1 tbsp Sesame Seeds, for garnish
  • 0.5 whole Lime, for serving
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
Instructions
1. Cook noodles according to package directions. Drain and rinse with cold water to prevent sticking, then set aside. If using frozen edamame, cook according to package directions (usually boil for 3-5 minutes), drain, and set aside. 2. In a small bowl, whisk together peanut butter, tamari, rice vinegar, maple syrup, and grated ginger until smooth to make the peanut sauce. Add a tablespoon or two of water if the sauce is too thick. 3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add cubed tofu and cook for 5-7 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden and slightly crispy. Remove tofu from skillet and set aside. 4. Add sliced red bell pepper and julienned carrots to the same skillet. Sauté for 3-5 minutes until slightly tender-crisp. Add the fresh spinach and cook until just wilted, about 1-2 minutes. 5. Return the cooked tofu and edamame to the skillet with the vegetables. Add the cooked noodles and pour the peanut sauce over everything. Toss gently to combine and coat all ingredients evenly. Cook for another 2-3 minutes to heat through. 6. Divide the noodle bowl mixture between two serving bowls. Garnish with sesame seeds and a squeeze of fresh lime juice before serving.

Hearty Seitan & Chickpea Stew with Herbed Barley

🥜 64g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Seitan, cubed
  • 2 cup Cooked Chickpeas (canned, rinsed)
  • 1 cup Pearl Barley, dry
  • 4 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 400 g Diced Tomatoes, canned
  • 2 whole Carrots, peeled and diced
  • 2 whole Celery Stalks, diced
  • 4 cup Fresh Spinach
  • 1 tsp Dried Thyme
  • 1 whole Bay Leaf
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped (for garnish)
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Rinse pearl barley under cold water. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add diced carrots and celery, sautéing for 5-7 minutes until slightly softened. 2. Add the cubed seitan to the pot and cook for another 3-5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned. 3. Pour in the vegetable broth and diced tomatoes (undrained). Add the rinsed pearl barley, dried thyme, and bay leaf. Bring the stew to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 30-35 minutes, or until the barley is tender. 4. Stir in the cooked chickpeas and fresh spinach. Cook for another 3-5 minutes, or until the spinach has wilted and the chickpeas are heated through. Remove the bay leaf. 5. Season the stew with salt and black pepper to taste. Ladle the hearty stew into two bowls. Garnish generously with fresh chopped parsley before serving.

High-Protein Savory Polenta Bowl with Crumbled Tempeh and Roasted Mushrooms

🥜 45g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 340 g Extra-firm tempeh
  • 250 g Cremini mushrooms
  • 160 g Quick-cooking polenta
  • 960 ml Vegetable broth
  • 15 g Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 30 ml Soy sauce (or tamari)
  • 30 ml Olive oil
  • 60 g Fresh spinach
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Slice the mushrooms thinly and crumble the tempeh into small pieces. In a large bowl, toss the mushrooms and tempeh with 1 tbsp (15ml) of olive oil, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tbsp (15ml) soy sauce, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Spread them in a single layer on a baking sheet and roast for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway, until golden and slightly crispy. While the tempeh and mushrooms roast, prepare the polenta: Bring 960ml vegetable broth to a boil in a medium saucepan. Slowly whisk in the 160g quick-cooking polenta, reducing heat to low. Continue to whisk occasionally for 5-7 minutes until the polenta is creamy and thickened. Stir in the remaining 1 tbsp (15ml) soy sauce, 15g nutritional yeast, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Once cooked, gently fold in the fresh spinach until wilted. Divide the creamy polenta between two bowls and top generously with the roasted tempeh and mushrooms.

High-Protein Spiced Tofu & White Bean Power Bowl with Quinoa and Roasted Broccolini

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 400 g Canned cannellini beans
  • 185 g Quinoa
  • 200 g Broccolini
  • 45 ml Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 whole Lemon
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
  • 480 ml Water or vegetable broth
Instructions
Press the tofu for at least 20 minutes to remove excess water, then cut it into 1-inch cubes. Rinse the cannellini beans thoroughly and drain. Cook the quinoa according to package directions: rinse 185g quinoa, combine with 480ml water or vegetable broth in a saucepan, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes until liquid is absorbed. Fluff with a fork. While quinoa cooks, preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Trim the broccolini. In a large bowl, toss the tofu cubes with 1.5 tbsp (22.5ml) olive oil, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp chili powder, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp black pepper. In a separate bowl, toss the broccolini with the remaining 1.5 tbsp (22.5ml) olive oil, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Spread tofu and broccolini on separate baking sheets. Roast for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, until tofu is golden and broccolini is tender-crisp. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa, roasted tofu, roasted broccolini, and drained cannellini beans evenly between two bowls. Squeeze fresh lemon juice over each bowl and garnish with chopped fresh cilantro if desired.

High-Protein Seitan & Lentil Bolognese with Whole Wheat Pasta

🥜 50g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Seitan
  • 100 g Red lentils
  • 150 g Whole wheat spaghetti
  • 794 g Crushed tomatoes
  • 30 g Tomato paste
  • 240 ml Vegetable broth
  • 2 whole Carrots
  • 2 whole Celery stalks
  • 2 whole Garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 tsp Dried basil
  • 0.5 tsp Red pepper flakes
  • 30 ml Olive oil
  • 0.2 cup Fresh parsley
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Finely dice the carrots and celery. Mince the garlic cloves. Dice or crumble the seitan into small, ground-meat-like pieces. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat 30ml olive oil over medium heat. Add the diced carrots and celery and sauté for 5-7 minutes until softened. Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant. Stir in the 30g tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes, stirring constantly, until it darkens slightly. Add the crumbled seitan, 100g red lentils (rinsed), 794g crushed tomatoes, 240ml vegetable broth, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp dried basil, 0.5 tsp red pepper flakes, 0.75 tsp salt, and 0.5 tsp black pepper. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and let it cook for 25-30 minutes, or until the lentils are tender and the sauce has thickened, stirring occasionally. While the bolognese simmers, cook the 150g whole wheat spaghetti according to package instructions until al dente. Drain the pasta. Serve the bolognese generously over the whole wheat spaghetti, garnished with fresh chopped parsley.

High-Protein Savory Oat & White Bean Hash with Spinach and Sun-Dried Tomatoes

🥜 22g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Rolled oats
  • 2 cup Vegetable broth
  • 1 cup Cooked cannellini beans
  • 4 cup Fresh spinach
  • 4 tbsp Sun-dried tomatoes (oil-packed, drained and chopped)
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 2 tsp Dried mixed herbs (e.g., oregano, basil, thyme)
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Pumpkin seeds
Instructions
1. In a medium saucepan, combine the rolled oats and vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 10-12 minutes, or until the oats are tender and most of the liquid is absorbed. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking. 2. While the oats are cooking, heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the cooked cannellini beans and sauté for 5 minutes until slightly browned. 3. Add the chopped sun-dried tomatoes, spinach, dried mixed herbs, salt, and black pepper to the skillet. Cook, stirring frequently, until the spinach has wilted, about 3-5 minutes. 4. Once the oats are cooked, stir in the nutritional yeast. Add the cooked oats to the skillet with the bean and spinach mixture. Stir well to combine all ingredients into a savory hash. 5. Divide the hash evenly between two bowls and sprinkle each serving with pumpkin seeds before serving warm.

High-Protein Chickpea & Seitan Green Curry with Brown Rice

🥜 36g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Cooked brown rice
  • 200 g Seitan (pre-cooked, sliced or cubed)
  • 1 cup Cooked chickpeas
  • 1 cup Bell peppers (any color, sliced)
  • 1 cup Green beans (trimmed)
  • 0.5 cup Carrots (thinly sliced)
  • 1 cup Full-fat coconut milk
  • 4 tbsp Vegan green curry paste
  • 2 tsp Lime juice
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro (chopped, for garnish)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil

High-Protein Tamari Roasted Almonds & Roasted Chickpeas with Seaweed Flakes

David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 80 g Whole raw almonds
  • 1.5 cup Cooked chickpeas (drained and rinsed)
  • 2 tbsp Tamari
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 2 tsp Dried seaweed flakes (nori or dulse)
Instructions

High-Protein Moroccan-Spiced Seitan & Lentil Couscous Bowl with Roasted Bell Peppers

🥜 41g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Seitan, diced
  • 0.5 cup Brown or Green Lentils, dry
  • 1.5 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 0.5 cup Whole Wheat Couscous, dry
  • 1 whole Red Bell Pepper
  • 2 whole Carrots, peeled and sliced
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 2 tsp Moroccan Spice Blend
  • 1 tbsp Tomato Paste
  • 2 tbsp Chopped Fresh Parsley
  • 2 whole Lemon Wedges
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 1 cup Vegetable Broth (for couscous)
Instructions
1. Cook the lentils: Rinse the dry lentils thoroughly. In a small saucepan, combine the rinsed lentils with 1.5 cups vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the lentils are tender. Drain any excess liquid and set aside. 2. Roast vegetables: Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Slice the red bell pepper and carrots. In a bowl, toss the sliced bell pepper and carrots with 1 tbsp olive oil, 0.5 tsp Moroccan spice blend, 0.25 tsp salt, and black pepper. Spread them in a single layer on a baking sheet and roast for 20-25 minutes until tender and slightly caramelized. 3. Cook couscous: While vegetables roast, bring 1 cup vegetable broth to a boil in a separate small saucepan. Prepare seitan: Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it is browned on all sides. 5.
