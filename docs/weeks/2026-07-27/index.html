High-Protein Savory Tofu & Chickpea Scramble with Toasted Buckwheat

🥜 34g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 120 g Canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 80 g Uncooked buckwheat groats
  • 200 g Fresh spinach
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper, diced
  • 3 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (Kala Namak)
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Rinse buckwheat groats thoroughly. Combine 80g buckwheat with 160ml water in a small saucepan, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Let stand for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 2. While buckwheat cooks, press the tofu for at least 15 minutes to remove excess water. Crumble the pressed tofu into small pieces using your hands or a fork. Drain and rinse the canned chickpeas. 3. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the diced bell pepper and sauté for 5-7 minutes until softened. 4. Add the crumbled tofu and drained chickpeas to the skillet. Sprinkle with turmeric powder, black salt, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Sauté for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tofu begins to lightly brown and dry out. 5. Stir in the nutritional yeast and fresh spinach. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, or until the spinach has wilted completely. 6. Divide the cooked buckwheat evenly between two bowls. Top generously with the tofu and chickpea scramble. Serve hot.

High-Protein Lentil & Quinoa Power Bowl with Roasted Zucchini and Lemon-Herb Dressing

🥜 28g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 150 g Dry green lentils
  • 100 g Dry quinoa
  • 2 whole Zucchini, chopped into 1-inch pieces
  • 200 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp Hemp seeds
  • 1.5 tbsp Olive oil (for roasting)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil (for dressing)
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 tsp Dried basil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Toss the chopped zucchini and cherry tomatoes with 1.5 tbsp olive oil, salt, and black pepper. Spread them in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until vegetables are tender and slightly caramelized. 2. While vegetables roast, cook the lentils. Rinse 150g dry green lentils thoroughly. Combine with 450ml water in a saucepan, bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 20-25 minutes until tender. Drain any excess water. 3. Rinse 100g dry quinoa thoroughly. Combine with 200ml water in a separate saucepan, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Let stand for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 4. Prepare the dressing by whisking together 1 tbsp olive oil, lemon juice, dried oregano, and dried basil in a small bowl. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper. 5. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked lentils and quinoa evenly between two large bowls. Add the roasted zucchini and cherry tomatoes. Drizzle generously with the lemon-herb dressing. Sprinkle each bowl with 1 tbsp hemp seeds and chopped fresh parsley before serving.

High-Protein Seitan & Black Bean Skillet with Bell Peppers and Cumin-Lime Rice

🥜 51g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 250 g Seitan, thinly sliced or cubed
  • 400 g Canned black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 160 g Dry brown rice
  • 2 whole Bell peppers (red and yellow), sliced
  • 400 g Canned diced tomatoes
  • 120 ml Vegetable broth
  • 2 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 whole Lime, whole
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. First, cook the cumin-lime brown rice. Rinse 160g dry brown rice thoroughly. Combine with 320ml water in a medium saucepan. Add 1 tsp ground cumin and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 35-40 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Remove from heat, stir in the juice of half a lime, and fluff with a fork. Keep covered. 2. While the rice cooks, heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet or non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Add the sliced bell peppers and sauté for 5-7 minutes until they are slightly softened and begin to char. 3. Add the sliced or cubed seitan to the skillet with the bell peppers. Cook for another 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the seitan is lightly browned. 4. Stir in the drained and rinsed black beans, canned diced tomatoes, vegetable broth, remaining 1 tsp cumin, smoked paprika, chili powder, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer, then reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and cook for 10-15 minutes to allow the flavors to meld. 5. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed. Squeeze the juice from the remaining half lime over the skillet contents. 6. Serve the seitan and black bean skillet mixture hot, over generous portions of the cumin-lime brown rice. Garnish with chopped fresh cilantro.

High-Protein Roasted Chickpeas & Walnuts with Smoked Paprika

🥜 24g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Canned chickpeas, drained, rinsed, and thoroughly dried
  • 100 g Walnut halves
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Ground cumin
  • 0.2 tsp Cayenne pepper (optional)
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Ensure the chickpeas are very dry; pat them thoroughly with a clean kitchen towel or paper towels. This is crucial for crispiness. 2. In a medium bowl, combine the dried chickpeas and walnut halves. Drizzle with 1 tbsp olive oil and toss to coat evenly. 3. Add the smoked paprika, ground cumin, cayenne pepper (if using), salt, and black pepper to the bowl. Toss again until the chickpeas and walnuts are well coated with the seasonings. 4. Spread the seasoned chickpeas and walnuts in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. Ensure they are not overcrowded, as this can prevent them from crisping up. 5. Roast for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the chickpeas are golden brown and crispy, and the walnuts are lightly toasted. Keep a close eye on them during the last few minutes to prevent burning. 6. Remove from the oven and let them cool on the baking sheet for a few minutes; they will crisp up further as they cool. Divide into two equal portions and serve as a satisfying high-protein snack.

High-Protein Savory Steel-Cut Oats with Crumbled Tempeh and Spinach

🥜 29g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 100 g Steel-cut oats
  • 400 ml Water or vegetable broth
  • 200 g Tempeh
  • 200 g Fresh spinach
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 3 tbsp Tamari or soy sauce
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tsp Olive oil
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
Instructions
1. In a medium saucepan, combine steel-cut oats, 400ml water or vegetable broth, and 0.25 tsp salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until oats are tender and liquid is absorbed. Stir occasionally. 2. While oats are cooking, crumble the tempeh into small pieces. Heat 2 tsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tempeh and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring frequently, until lightly browned and crispy. 3. Add smoked paprika, garlic powder, and 0.25 tsp black pepper to the tempeh, stirring to coat. Cook for another 1 minute until fragrant. 4. Stir in the fresh spinach and tamari. Cook until the spinach has wilted, about 2-3 minutes. 5. Once the oats are cooked, divide them between two bowls. Top each bowl with the tempeh and spinach mixture, then sprinkle with 2 tbsp nutritional yeast per bowl. Serve immediately.

🥜 25g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 140 g Farro (dry)
  • 300 g Cannellini beans (canned, drained & rinsed)
  • 200 g Carrots
  • 200 g Zucchini
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil (for roasting)
  • 1 tsp Dried rosemary
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 3 tbsp Tahini
  • 3 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 4 tbsp Water (for dressing)
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder (for dressing)
  • 0.2 tsp Salt (for dressing)
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Wash and chop carrots and zucchini into bite-sized pieces. In a large bowl, toss the chopped vegetables with 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp dried rosemary, 1 tsp dried thyme, 0.5 tsp salt, and 0.25 tsp black pepper. Spread them in a single layer on a baking sheet. High-Protein Seitan & Chickpea Stir-fry with Brown Rice and Bok Choy

🥜 41g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 100 g Brown rice (dry)
  • 300 g Seitan
  • 200 g Canned chickpeas (drained & rinsed)
  • 300 g Bok choy
  • 200 g Red bell pepper
  • 2 tsp Fresh ginger (grated)
  • 4 tbsp Tamari or soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 2 tsp Toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Cornstarch
  • 4 tbsp Water (for sauce)
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil (for stir-frying)
Instructions
1. Cook brown rice according to package instructions. Typically, combine 100g dry brown rice with 2 cups of water in a saucepan, bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 40-45 minutes until water is absorbed and rice is tender. 2. While rice cooks, prepare the stir-fry. Slice seitan into thin strips or bite-sized pieces. Chop bok choy, separating the white stems from the green leaves. Core and slice the red bell pepper into thin strips. Grate the fresh ginger. 3. In a small bowl, whisk together 4 tbsp tamari, 2 tbsp rice vinegar, 2 tsp toasted sesame oil, 1 tbsp cornstarch, and 4 tbsp water to create the stir-fry sauce. 4. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes until lightly browned and slightly crispy. Add the grated ginger and cook for 1 minute until fragrant. 5. Add the red bell pepper strips and the white stems of the bok choy to the skillet. Stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until vegetables start to soften. Stir in the drained chickpeas and the green leaves of the bok choy. Cook for another 2-3 minutes until the leaves are wilted. 6. Pour the prepared stir-fry sauce into the skillet. Stir continuously for 1-2 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats all the ingredients. 7. Divide the cooked brown rice into two bowls and top with the seitan and chickpea stir-fry. Serve hot.

High-Protein Savory Chickpea & Kale Pancakes with Roasted Bell Peppers and Tahini Drizzle

🥜 26g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 120 g Chickpea Flour
  • 180 ml Water
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 1 tsp Baking Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.2 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 cup Kale, chopped
  • 1 whole Bell Pepper (any color), sliced
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 2 tbsp Tahini
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 1 tbsp Water (for drizzle)
  • 1 tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped (for garnish)
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss the sliced bell pepper with 1 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes until tender and slightly charred. 2. While bell peppers are roasting, prepare the pancake batter. In a large bowl, whisk together chickpea flour, 180ml water, nutritional yeast, baking powder, garlic powder, turmeric, 0.5 tsp salt, and 0.25 tsp black pepper until smooth. 3. Fold in the chopped kale into the batter. Let the batter rest for 5-10 minutes. 4. Heat 0.5 tbsp olive oil in a non-stick pan or griddle over medium heat. Pour about 1/2 cup of batter per pancake, forming 2-3 pancakes at a time. Cook for 3-4 minutes per side, until golden brown and cooked through. Repeat with remaining batter, adding more oil as needed. You should get 4-6 pancakes total for 2 servings. 5. Prepare the tahini drizzle: In a small bowl, whisk together tahini, lemon juice, and 1 tbsp water until smooth and pourable. Add more water if needed to reach desired consistency. 6. To serve, divide the cooked pancakes between two plates. Top with roasted bell peppers and a generous drizzle of the tahini sauce. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley.

High-Protein Tofu & Quinoa Power Bowl with Roasted Sweet Potatoes and Peanut Dressing

🥜 32g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm Tofu
  • 100 g Quinoa, uncooked
  • 200 ml Water (for quinoa)
  • 2 whole Sweet Potato, medium
  • 2 cup Broccoli florets
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 2 tbsp Soy Sauce (tamari for gluten-free)
  • 1 tbsp Rice Vinegar
  • 1 tsp Maple Syrup
  • 3 tbsp Peanut Butter, natural
  • 1 tbsp Lime Juice
  • 1 tsp Ginger, grated
  • 3 tbsp Water (for dressing)
  • 0.5 tsp Chili flakes (optional)
  • 2 tbsp Cilantro, fresh, chopped (for garnish)
  • 1 tsp Sesame Seeds (for garnish)
Instructions
1. Press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15 minutes to remove excess water. Then cut into 1-inch cubes. 2. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Peel and dice sweet potatoes into 1-inch cubes. Toss sweet potatoes and broccoli florets with 1 tbsp olive oil, a pinch of salt and pepper. Spread on a baking sheet. 3. In a separate bowl, toss the tofu cubes with 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp soy sauce, and 1 tsp maple syrup. Spread on a separate baking sheet or alongside vegetables if there's enough space. 4. Roast the vegetables and tofu for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway, until sweet potatoes are tender, broccoli is slightly charred, and tofu is golden brown and firm. 5. While roasting, cook the quinoa. Rinse quinoa thoroughly under cold water. Combine quinoa and 200ml water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 6. Prepare the peanut dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together peanut butter, 1 tbsp soy sauce, rice vinegar, lime juice, grated ginger, chili flakes (if using), and 3-4 tbsp water until smooth and creamy. Adjust water to reach desired pouring consistency. 7. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two large serving bowls. Top each with roasted sweet potatoes, broccoli, and tofu. Drizzle generously with the peanut dressing. Garnish with fresh cilantro and sesame seeds.

High-Protein Seitan & Kidney Bean Ragu with Polenta

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 250 g Seitan, store-bought, drained and chopped
  • 1.5 cup Kidney Beans, canned, rinsed and drained
  • 400 g Crushed Tomatoes, canned
  • 1 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 1 cup Carrots, diced
  • 0.5 cup Celery, diced
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp Dried Oregano
  • 1 tsp Dried Basil
  • 0.5 tsp Red Pepper Flakes (optional)
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional Yeast (for polenta)
  • 3 cup Water (for polenta)
  • 1 cup Fine Cornmeal (Polenta)
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped (for garnish)
Instructions
1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add diced carrots and celery and sauté for 5-7 minutes until softened. Add minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant. 2. Stir in the chopped seitan and cook for 3-5 minutes until lightly browned. 3. Add the rinsed kidney beans, crushed tomatoes, vegetable broth, dried oregano, dried basil, red pepper flakes (if using), 0.5 tsp salt, and 0.25 tsp black pepper. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and let the ragu cook for 20-25 minutes, allowing flavors to meld and vegetables to become tender. Stir occasionally. 4. While the ragu simmers, prepare the polenta. In a separate medium saucepan, bring 3 cups of water to a boil. Slowly whisk in the fine cornmeal (polenta) in a thin stream to prevent lumps. 5. Reduce heat to low and continue to whisk frequently for 5-7 minutes, until the polenta thickens to a creamy consistency. Stir in the nutritional yeast, a pinch of salt, and pepper. 6. Taste the ragu and polenta, adjusting seasonings as needed. 7. To serve, spoon a generous portion of creamy polenta into each of two bowls. Ladle the hearty seitan and kidney bean ragu over the polenta. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley.

High-Protein Hearty Lentil & Sunflower Seed Loaf with Roasted Parsnips and Sautéed Kale

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 cup Brown or green lentils, cooked
  • 1 cup Rolled oats
  • 0.5 cup Sunflower seeds
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 3 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Dried sage
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp Soy sauce or tamari
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 400 g Parsnips
  • 200 g Kale, destemmed and chopped
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 50 ml Vegetable broth
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F). Peel and chop the parsnips into 1-inch pieces, toss with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet, and roast for 25-30 minutes, or until tender and slightly golden. While parsnips roast, prepare the lentil loaf. In a large bowl, mash the cooked lentils roughly with a fork or potato masher. Add the rolled oats, sunflower seeds, tomato paste, nutritional yeast, sage, thyme, smoked paprika, soy sauce, and Dijon mustard. Mix thoroughly until well combined. Form the mixture into a compact loaf shape and place it on a separate parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake the loaf for 30-35 minutes, or until firm and golden brown. In the last 10 minutes of cooking, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the chopped kale and vegetable broth, sautéing for 5-7 minutes until the kale is wilted and tender. Slice the lentil loaf into two equal portions and serve alongside the roasted parsnips and sautéed kale.
