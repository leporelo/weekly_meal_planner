High-Protein Spicy Black Bean & Quinoa Breakfast Bowl with Avocado

🥜 32g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.5 cup Quinoa (uncooked)
  • 1 cup Water (for quinoa)
  • 2 cup Black Beans (canned, drained and rinsed)
  • 0.5 cup Salsa (mild or medium)
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Chili Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 1 whole Avocado
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Cilantro (chopped)
  • 1 whole Lime
  • 2 tbsp Pumpkin Seeds
Instructions
Rinse the quinoa thoroughly under cold water. Combine quinoa and 1 cup of water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed and quinoa is fluffy. Set aside. In a separate saucepan, combine the drained and rinsed black beans with salsa, cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper. Cook over medium heat for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until heated through and flavors are well combined. While beans are heating, dice the avocado and chop the fresh cilantro. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two serving bowls. Top each with an equal portion of the spicy black beans. Garnish with diced avocado, fresh cilantro, a squeeze of fresh lime juice, and a sprinkle of pumpkin seeds.

High-Protein Roasted Chickpea & Kale Power Bowl with Farro and Hemp Seeds

🥜 35g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.5 cup Farro (uncooked)
  • 2 cup Water (for farro)
  • 2 cup Chickpeas (canned, drained and rinsed)
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 4 cup Kale (stems removed, chopped)
  • 2 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 2 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Warm Water (for dressing)
  • 0.5 tsp Maple Syrup (optional, for dressing)
  • 4 tbsp Hemp Seeds
Instructions
Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Cook farro: In a saucepan, combine farro with 2 cups of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until tender. Drain any excess water and set aside. While farro cooks, prepare chickpeas: Toss drained and rinsed chickpeas with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, smoked paprika, cumin, salt, and black pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway, until slightly crispy. Prepare kale: In a large bowl, massage chopped kale with 1 tablespoon of olive oil and a pinch of salt until slightly softened. Make tahini dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together lemon juice, tahini, 2 tablespoons warm water, and optional maple syrup until smooth and creamy. If too thick, add more water, 1 teaspoon at a time. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked farro and massaged kale between two bowls. Top with the roasted chickpeas. Drizzle generously with the tahini dressing and sprinkle with hemp seeds before serving.

High-Protein Smoky Tempeh & Black Bean Chili with Cornbread

🥜 60g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 224 g Tempeh
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 whole Bell Pepper (any color, diced)
  • 400 g Diced Tomatoes (canned, unsalted)
  • 1.5 cup Black Beans (canned, drained and rinsed)
  • 1 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 1.5 tbsp Chili Powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 0.8 cup Cornmeal
  • 0.2 cup All-Purpose Flour
  • 1 tsp Baking Powder
  • 0.8 cup Plant-Based Milk (unsweetened)
  • 1 tbsp Maple Syrup (for cornbread)
Instructions
First, prepare the chili. Crumble the tempeh into small pieces. Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced bell pepper and sauté for 5 minutes until softened. Add the crumbled tempeh and cook for another 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned. Stir in the diced tomatoes (undrained), black beans, vegetable broth, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, salt, black pepper, and nutritional yeast. Bring the chili to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and let it cook for at least 20 minutes, stirring occasionally. While the chili simmers, preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C) and prepare the cornbread. In a medium bowl, whisk together cornmeal, flour, baking powder, a pinch of salt. In a separate bowl, whisk together plant-based milk and maple syrup. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and mix until just combined (do not overmix). Pour the batter into a lightly oiled 8x8 inch baking dish or an equivalent cast iron skillet. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Serve the hot chili topped with a piece of fresh cornbread for each person.

Snack 2

High-Protein Edamame Hummus with Bell Pepper Sticks

🥜 31g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 5 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 3 cup Edamame (shelled, frozen)
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 3 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 4 tbsp Cold Water
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 1 whole Red Bell Pepper
  • 1 whole Yellow Bell Pepper
Instructions
Cook the edamame according to package directions, usually by boiling or steaming for 3-5 minutes until tender. Drain well and let cool slightly. In a food processor, combine the cooked edamame, tahini, lemon juice, olive oil, cold water, salt, and black pepper. Process until completely smooth and creamy, scraping down the sides as needed. If the hummus is too thick, add more cold water 1 tablespoon at a time until desired consistency is reached. Taste and adjust seasonings if necessary. Slice the red and yellow bell peppers into thin sticks suitable for dipping. Divide the edamame hummus into two portions and serve each with an equal amount of bell pepper sticks for a satisfying, protein-rich snack.

High-Protein Savory Tofu Scramble with Roasted Bell Peppers & Kale

🥜 36g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 350 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 3 cup Kale
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.2 tsp Black salt (Kala Namak)
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 60 ml Soy milk (unsweetened)
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes
Instructions
Preheat oven to 200°C (390°F). Press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15 minutes to remove excess water. Toss diced red bell pepper and halved cherry tomatoes with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes until tender-crisp. While vegetables roast, crumble the pressed tofu into a large mixing bowl. Add nutritional yeast, turmeric, black salt, garlic powder, smoked paprika, black pepper, and unsweetened soy milk. Mix gently to combine, breaking up any large tofu chunks. Heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large non-stick pan over medium heat. Add the seasoned tofu mixture and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it starts to brown slightly and the liquid has evaporated. Add the chopped kale to the pan and cook for another 3-5 minutes, or until the kale has wilted. Stir in the roasted bell peppers and cherry tomatoes. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Miso-Glazed Tempeh & Edamame Bowl with Brown Rice and Roasted Broccoli

🥜 31g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Tempeh
  • 1 cup Edamame (shelled, frozen)
  • 0.5 cup Brown rice (uncooked)
  • 3 cup Broccoli florets
  • 2 tbsp White miso paste
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp Water
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
Instructions
First, cook the brown rice: Combine 0.5 cup of brown rice with 1.25 cups of water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 35-40 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 10 minutes. Fluff with a fork. While rice cooks, preheat oven to 200°C (390°F). Toss broccoli florets with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes until tender-crisp. For the miso glaze, whisk together white miso paste, maple syrup, tamari, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and water in a small bowl. Steam or boil the frozen edamame according to package instructions, then drain. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat. Add tempeh slices (cut into 1cm thick pieces) and sear for 3-4 minutes per side until golden. Pour the miso glaze over the tempeh, ensuring each slice is coated. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, gently turning the tempeh, until the glaze thickens and caramelizes. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked brown rice between two serving bowls. Top with miso-glazed tempeh, roasted broccoli, and steamed edamame. Garnish with sesame seeds.

High-Protein Seitan & White Bean Minestrone with Protein Pasta

🥜 45g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 150 g Seitan
  • 400 g Canned cannellini beans
  • 400 g Canned diced tomatoes
  • 700 ml Vegetable broth
  • 80 g Protein pasta (e.g., lentil-based fusilli)
  • 1 whole Carrots
  • 1 whole Celery
  • 1 whole Zucchini
  • 2 whole Garlic
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 tsp Dried basil
  • 2 cup Fresh spinach
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley
Instructions
Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add diced carrots, diced celery, and minced garlic and sauté for 5-7 minutes until vegetables begin to soften. Stir in the diced seitan and cook for another 3-4 minutes, browning lightly. Add canned diced tomatoes, vegetable broth, dried oregano, and dried basil. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes to allow flavors to meld. Add the rinsed and drained cannellini beans, diced zucchini, and protein pasta to the pot. Cook for another 8-10 minutes, or according to pasta package directions, until the pasta is al dente and the zucchini is tender. Stir in the chopped fresh spinach until it wilts, about 1-2 minutes. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. Ladle the hot minestrone into two bowls and garnish with fresh chopped parsley.

High-Protein Savory Baked Edamame & Hemp Seed Clusters

🥜 27g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 cup Shelled edamame (frozen)
  • 6 tbsp Hemp seeds
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. If using frozen edamame, thaw it by rinsing under warm water or microwaving briefly, then pat very dry with a clean towel to ensure crispiness. In a medium bowl, combine the thawed edamame, hemp seeds, tamari, olive oil, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and black pepper. Toss well to ensure all ingredients are evenly coated. Spread the mixture in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. Bake for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the edamame is slightly crisp and golden. Let cool completely on the baking sheet before serving, as they will crisp up further as they cool. Store any leftovers in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.

High-Protein Scrambled Chickpea Flour & Tofu with Spinach and Bell Peppers

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 350 g Extra-Firm Tofu
  • 50 g Chickpea Flour
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black Salt (Kala Namak)
  • 120 ml Vegetable Broth
  • 1 whole Red Bell Pepper
  • 1 whole Green Bell Pepper
  • 150 g Fresh Spinach
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Press the extra-firm tofu for at least 20 minutes to remove excess water. Once pressed, crumble the tofu into a bowl using your hands or a fork. 2. In a small bowl, whisk together the chickpea flour, nutritional yeast, turmeric powder, black salt, and vegetable broth until smooth to create a slurry. 3. Dice both red and green bell peppers into small, uniform pieces. 4. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced bell peppers and sauté for 5-7 minutes until they start to soften. 5. Add the crumbled tofu to the skillet with the bell peppers. Cook for another 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tofu begins to lightly brown and firm up. 6. Pour the chickpea flour slurry over the tofu and vegetables, stirring continuously to coat everything evenly. Continue to cook for 2-3 minutes, allowing the slurry to thicken and coat the tofu, creating a 'scrambled' texture. 7. Stir in the fresh spinach, salt, and black pepper. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, or until the spinach has wilted. 8. Serve hot, portioning evenly into two bowls.

High-Protein Sesame-Ginger Tofu & Broccoli Quinoa Bowl

🥜 45g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 120 g Quinoa, dry
  • 240 ml Water
  • 350 g Extra-Firm Tofu
  • 400 g Broccoli Florets
  • 1 tbsp Sesame Oil
  • 4 tbsp Low-Sodium Tamari (or Soy Sauce)
  • 2 tbsp Rice Vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Fresh Ginger, grated
  • 1 tbsp Maple Syrup
  • 2 tbsp Tahini
  • 1 tsp Sesame Seeds, for garnish
Instructions
1. Rinse the quinoa thoroughly under cold water. Combine quinoa and water in a small pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 2. Press the extra-firm tofu to remove excess water. Cut the pressed tofu into 1-inch cubes. 3. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss the broccoli florets with 1 teaspoon of sesame oil and spread them on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes, or until tender-crisp and slightly charred. 4. While broccoli roasts, prepare the tofu. In a large bowl, whisk together 2 tablespoons of tamari, 1 tablespoon of rice vinegar, 1/2 tablespoon of grated ginger, and 1/2 tablespoon of maple syrup. Add the tofu cubes and toss gently to coat. 5. Heat 1 teaspoon of sesame oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the marinated tofu in a single layer and pan-fry for 5-7 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden brown and crispy on all sides. 6. For the dressing, whisk together the remaining 2 tablespoons of tamari, 1 tablespoon of rice vinegar, 1/2 tablespoon of grated ginger, 1/2 tablespoon of maple syrup, and the tahini until smooth. Add a tablespoon or two of water if needed to reach desired consistency. 7. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two serving bowls. Top with roasted broccoli and pan-fried tofu. Drizzle generously with the tahini-ginger dressing and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

High-Protein Lentil & Seitan Skillet with Roasted Carrots and Greens

🥜 50g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 250 g Seitan
  • 300 g Cooked Green Lentils (canned or pre-cooked)
  • 2 whole Carrots
  • 3 whole Garlic Cloves
  • 240 ml Vegetable Broth
  • 2 tbsp Tomato Paste
  • 1 tsp Dried Thyme
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 100 g Kale (or other sturdy greens), chopped
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Peel and slice carrots into 1/2-inch thick rounds. Toss with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and lightly caramelized. 2. While carrots roast, prepare the seitan. Cut the seitan into bite-sized pieces or strips. Mince the garlic cloves. 3. Heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet (preferably oven-safe if you want to finish in the oven, otherwise transfer later) over medium-high heat. Add the seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned on all sides. Remove seitan from the skillet and set aside. 4. Reduce heat to medium. Add minced garlic to the skillet and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant. Stir in the tomato paste, dried thyme, and smoked paprika, cooking for another minute. 5. Pour in the vegetable broth and bring to a simmer, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the skillet. Add the cooked lentils and bring the seitan back to the skillet. Cook for 5 minutes, allowing the sauce to thicken slightly. 6. Stir in the chopped kale and nutritional yeast. Cook for another 3-5 minutes, or until the kale has wilted. Season with additional salt and pepper to taste. 7. Stir in the roasted carrots. Serve hot, portioning evenly into two bowls or plates.

High-Protein Smoked Tofu & Black-Eyed Pea Hash with Sweet Potatoes & Kale

🥜 28g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g smoked firm tofu
  • 250 g cooked black-eyed peas (canned, rinsed)
  • 250 g sweet potato
  • 100 g kale, destemmed and chopped
  • 1 whole red bell pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp salt
  • 0.2 tsp black pepper
Instructions

Stir in the drained and rinsed black-eyed peas, chopped kale, garlic powder, smoked paprika, dried thyme, salt, and black pepper. Continue to cook for 3-5 minutes, or until the kale has wilted and the ingredients are well combined. Finally, stir in the nutritional yeast and cook for an additional minute. Serve hot.

High-Protein Savory Chickpea & Quinoa Patties with Roasted Tomatoes

Ingredients
  • 120 g Chickpea flour
  • 0.5 cup Cooked quinoa
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Baking powder
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika

  • 0.1 tsp Black pepper

  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
