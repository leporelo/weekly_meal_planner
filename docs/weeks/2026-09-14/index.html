High-Protein Savory Tofu & Black Bean Scramble with Roasted Bell Peppers and Spinach

🥜 27.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu, drained and pressed
  • 1 cup Canned black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 1 whole Yellow bell pepper
  • 4 cup Fresh spinach
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (kala namak, optional for eggy flavor)
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Wash and deseed the bell peppers, then cut them into 1-inch strips. Toss the bell pepper strips with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and black pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes until tender and slightly charred. 2. While the peppers are roasting, crumble the pressed tofu into a large mixing bowl. Add the nutritional yeast, turmeric powder, garlic powder, cumin powder, black salt (if using), 0.5 tsp salt, and 0.25 tsp black pepper. Mix thoroughly until the tofu is evenly coated and the spices are well distributed. 3. Heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the seasoned tofu crumble to the hot skillet and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it starts to dry out and lightly brown. 4. Stir in the drained black beans and cook for another 3-5 minutes, allowing them to heat through. Add the fresh spinach and cook until it wilts, about 2-3 minutes. 5. Remove the skillet from heat and gently fold in the roasted bell peppers. Divide the scramble evenly into two bowls and serve immediately.

High-Protein Teriyaki Tempeh & Quinoa Bowl with Roasted Carrots and Sautéed Kale

🥜 26.5g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 250 g Tempeh
  • 0.8 cup Dry quinoa
  • 2 whole Carrots, large
  • 4 cup Kale, stems removed and chopped
  • 4 tbsp Soy sauce or tamari
  • 2 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Ginger powder
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 2 tbsp Water
Instructions
1. Cook the quinoa: Rinse the 0.75 cup dry quinoa thoroughly. Combine with 1.5 cups of water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside. 2. Prepare the carrots: Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Peel the carrots and slice them into 1/2-inch thick rounds or sticks. Toss with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and slightly caramelized. 3. Prepare the tempeh: While carrots roast, steam the tempeh block for 10 minutes (this helps it absorb flavors better). Let it cool slightly, then slice it into thin strips. 4. Make the teriyaki sauce: In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, maple syrup, rice vinegar, ginger powder, sesame oil, and 2 tablespoons of water. 5. Cook the tempeh: Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tempeh strips and cook for 5-7 minutes, flipping occasionally, until golden brown. Pour the teriyaki sauce over the tempeh, reduce heat to medium, and simmer for 2-3 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the tempeh. 6. Sauté the kale: In the same skillet (or a separate one if preferred), quickly sauté the chopped kale until wilted, about 3-5 minutes. You can add a splash of water if needed to help it steam. 7. Assemble the bowls: Divide the cooked quinoa into two bowls. Top with the teriyaki tempeh, roasted carrots, and sautéed kale. Garnish with sesame seeds.

High-Protein Seitan Stroganoff with Creamy Cashew Sauce, Farro, and Roasted Asparagus

🥜 36.5g protein/serving 🔪 25 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Seitan, sliced into strips
  • 0.8 cup Dry farro
  • 400 g Asparagus, trimmed
  • 0.5 cup Raw cashews
  • 1 cup Vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Dried dill
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped
Instructions
1. Cook the farro: Rinse the 0.75 cup dry farro. Combine with 2.25 cups of water or vegetable broth in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 25-30 minutes until tender. Drain any excess liquid and set aside. 2. Prepare cashews: Soak the raw cashews in hot water for at least 15 minutes (or cold water for 1 hour). Drain and rinse. 3. Roast the asparagus: Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss the trimmed asparagus with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and black pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 10-12 minutes until tender-crisp. 4. Make the cashew cream: In a blender, combine the soaked cashews, 0.5 cup vegetable broth, nutritional yeast, dried dill, Dijon mustard, and lemon juice. Blend until completely smooth and creamy. Add a little more broth if needed to reach desired consistency. 5. Cook the seitan: Heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seitan strips and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned. 6. Assemble the stroganoff: Pour the cashew cream sauce into the skillet with the seitan. Add the remaining 0.5 cup vegetable broth and smoked paprika. Stir well and bring to a gentle simmer. Cook for 3-5 minutes, stirring, until the sauce thickens slightly and coats the seitan. Season with salt and pepper to taste. 7. Serve: Divide the cooked farro into two bowls. Top with the seitan stroganoff and roasted asparagus. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley.

High-Protein Savory Lentil & Bulgur Breakfast Bowl with Smoked Tofu and Kale

🥜 36.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Smoked Tofu
  • 100 g Brown or Green Lentils (dry)
  • 100 g Medium Bulgur (dry)
  • 4 cup Kale (stems removed, chopped)
  • 4 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 2 tbsp Tamari
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Rinse lentils thoroughly. In a medium saucepan, combine lentils with 2 cups of vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until tender. Drain any excess liquid and set aside. 2. In a separate small saucepan, combine bulgur with 2 cups of vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed and bulgur is tender. Fluff with a fork and set aside. 3. While lentils and bulgur cook, cut the smoked tofu into small cubes. In a large skillet, heat 1 tbsp of olive oil over medium heat. Add the tofu cubes and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and slightly crispy. Sprinkle with 1/2 tsp garlic powder and 1/2 tsp smoked paprika during the last few minutes of cooking. 4. Add the chopped kale to the skillet with the tofu. Drizzle with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil and cook for 3-5 minutes, stirring until the kale is wilted. 5. In a small bowl, whisk together the tamari, nutritional yeast, remaining 1/2 tsp garlic powder, remaining 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper to create a savory sauce. 6. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked bulgur, lentils, and kale-tofu mixture between two serving bowls. Drizzle generously with the savory sauce. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Smoky Seitan & Chickpea Salad Bowl with Quinoa and Roasted Bell Peppers

🥜 40.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Seitan (pre-cooked)
  • 400 g Canned Chickpeas (rinsed and drained)
  • 100 g Quinoa (dry)
  • 2 whole Bell Peppers (any color, deseeded, sliced)
  • 1 whole Cucumber (diced)
  • 1 cup Cherry Tomatoes (halved)
  • 0.2 cup Fresh Parsley (chopped)
  • 3 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 3 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp Maple Syrup
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. Cook the quinoa: In a small saucepan, combine quinoa with 1 cup of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside. 3. Prepare the roasted vegetables: On the lined baking sheet, toss the sliced bell peppers with 1 tbsp of olive oil, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp cumin, 1/4 tsp garlic powder, and a pinch of salt and pepper. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender-crisp and slightly charred. 4. While vegetables roast, cut the seitan into bite-sized pieces. In a skillet, heat 1 tbsp of olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes until lightly browned. Stir in the remaining 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp cumin, and 1/4 tsp garlic powder, cooking for another minute. 5. Prepare the dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together the lemon juice, remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, maple syrup, salt, and black pepper. 6. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two serving bowls. Top with roasted bell peppers, seitan, rinsed chickpeas, diced cucumber, halved cherry tomatoes, and fresh parsley. Drizzle generously with the lemon-herb dressing. Serve warm or at room temperature.

High-Protein Black Bean & Tempeh Chili with Whole Wheat Couscous

🥜 46g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 227 g Tempeh
  • 800 g Canned Black Beans (rinsed and drained)
  • 400 g Diced Tomatoes (canned)
  • 2 tbsp Tomato Paste
  • 1 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 2 tbsp Chili Powder
  • 1 tbsp Cumin
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp Unsweetened Cocoa Powder (optional)
  • 1 tbsp Maple Syrup
  • 150 g Whole Wheat Couscous (dry)
  • 355 ml Water
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.2 cup Fresh Cilantro (chopped, for garnish)
  • 1 whole Avocado (sliced, for garnish)
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Crumble the tempeh into small pieces using your hands or a fork. 2. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat 2 tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add the crumbled tempeh and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned. 3. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, cocoa powder (if using), salt, and black pepper. Cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly until fragrant. 4. Add the rinsed black beans, diced tomatoes, tomato paste, vegetable broth, and maple syrup to the pot. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and let it gently simmer for at least 20-25 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld. Stir occasionally. 5. While the chili simmers, prepare the whole wheat couscous: In a small saucepan, bring 355ml water to a boil. Remove from heat, stir in the dry couscous, cover, and let stand for 5 minutes until the water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork. 6. Serve the chili hot over beds of whole wheat couscous. Garnish each serving with fresh chopped cilantro and sliced avocado.

High-Protein Chili-Lime Roasted Pepitas & Almonds

🥜 28g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 15 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Raw Pepitas (Pumpkin Seeds)
  • 1 cup Raw Almonds
  • 2 tbsp Lime Juice
  • 1 tbsp Chili Powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 160°C (325°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. In a medium bowl, combine the raw pepitas and raw almonds. 3. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the lime juice, chili powder, smoked paprika, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and olive oil to create a spicy-tangy coating. 4. Pour the spice mixture over the pepitas and almonds, tossing thoroughly until all nuts and seeds are evenly coated. 5. Spread the coated pepitas and almonds in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. 6. Roast for 15-20 minutes, stirring halfway through, until fragrant and lightly toasted. Keep a close eye to prevent burning. 7. Remove from oven and let cool completely on the baking sheet. They will become crispier as they cool. Divide into two equal portions for sharing or meal prepping. Store in an airtight container for up to a week.

High-Protein Seitan & Kidney Bean Skillet with Roasted Potatoes and Spinach

🥜 22.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g baby potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper
  • 200 g seitan
  • 400 g kidney beans, canned, rinsed and drained
  • 2 cup fresh spinach
  • 0.2 cup vegetable broth
  • 1 tbsp tamari
  • 0.2 cup fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)
Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. Quarter the baby potatoes. In a large bowl, toss the quartered potatoes with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, smoked paprika, garlic powder, ½ teaspoon of salt, and ¼ teaspoon of black pepper. Spread them in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. 3. Roast the potatoes for 25-30 minutes, or until they are tender and slightly crispy, making sure to flip them halfway through for even cooking. 4. While the potatoes are roasting, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it is nicely browned. 5. Add the rinsed and drained kidney beans, vegetable broth, and tamari to the skillet with the seitan. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer and cook for 3-5 minutes, allowing the sauce to reduce slightly. 6. Stir in the fresh spinach and continue to cook for another 2-3 minutes, or until the spinach has completely wilted. 7. To serve, divide the roasted potatoes evenly between two bowls. Top with the warm seitan and kidney bean mixture from the skillet. Garnish with fresh chopped cilantro, if desired, before serving immediately.

High-Protein Hearty Adzuki Bean & Seitan Stew with Barley

🥜 25g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 200 g seitan, diced
  • 2 whole carrots, peeled and diced
  • 2 whole celery stalks, diced
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 0.5 tsp dried rosemary
  • 4 cup vegetable broth
  • 400 g adzuki beans, canned, rinsed and drained
  • 0.5 cup pearl barley
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes (canned or fresh)
  • 0.8 tsp salt
  • 0.2 tsp black pepper
  • 0.2 cup fresh parsley, chopped
Instructions
1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring until it is lightly browned on all sides. Remove the seitan from the pot and set it aside. 2. Add the diced carrots and celery to the same pot. Sauté for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they begin to soften. Stir in the dried thyme and dried rosemary, cooking for another minute until fragrant. 3. Pour in the vegetable broth, then add the rinsed and drained adzuki beans, pearl barley, and diced tomatoes. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover the pot, and simmer for 30-35 minutes, or until the barley is tender. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking. 4. Return the cooked seitan to the pot and stir it thoroughly to combine with the stew. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. 5. Ladle the hearty stew into two bowls, ensuring an even distribution of ingredients. Garnish each serving with fresh chopped parsley before serving hot.

High-Protein Sheet Pan Tempeh & Chickpea Fajitas with Quinoa

🥜 26.5g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g tempeh, crumbled or cut into strips
  • 400 g chickpeas, canned, rinsed and drained
  • 1 whole red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1 whole yellow bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning (onion-free)
  • 0.5 cup quinoa, rinsed
  • 1 cup vegetable broth or water
  • 0.5 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1 whole avocado, sliced
  • 2 whole lime wedges
Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). In a medium saucepan, combine the rinsed quinoa with the vegetable broth or water. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all the liquid is absorbed and the quinoa is fluffy. Let it stand for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 2. While the quinoa cooks, prepare the sheet pan ingredients. In a large bowl, combine the crumbled or stripped tempeh, the rinsed and drained chickpeas, and the thinly sliced red and yellow bell peppers. 3. Add the 2 tablespoons of olive oil and the fajita seasoning to the bowl. Toss everything well until the tempeh, chickpeas, and bell peppers are evenly coated with the seasoning. 4. Spread the seasoned mixture in a single layer on a large baking sheet. 5. Roast for 25-30 minutes, stirring the ingredients halfway through, until the tempeh is browned and slightly crispy, and the vegetables are tender-crisp. 6. To assemble the fajita bowls, divide the cooked quinoa evenly between two bowls. Top each bowl generously with the roasted tempeh and chickpea fajita mixture. 7. Garnish each bowl with fresh chopped cilantro and several slices of avocado. Serve immediately with lime wedges for squeezing over the top.

Ingredients
  • 200 g Whole wheat pasta (e.g., penne, fusilli)

High-Protein Savory Quinoa & White Bean Breakfast Bowl with Sautéed Kale and Sun-Dried Tomatoes

🥜 28g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup quinoa, uncooked
  • 2 cup water
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 whole garlic, minced
  • 4 cup kale, chopped
  • 400 g cannellini beans, rinsed and drained
  • 0.5 cup sun-dried tomatoes (packed in oil), drained and chopped
  • 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper
  • 0.2 tsp red pepper flakes
Instructions
