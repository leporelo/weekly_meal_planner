High-Protein Savory Millet Bowl with Spiced Tofu Crumbles and Kale

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Millet
  • 1.5 cup Water
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 3 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.2 tsp Black salt (kala namak, optional)
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce (or tamari for GF)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 4 cup Kale, destemmed and chopped
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes, halved
Instructions
1. Rinse the millet under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine millet and water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until water is absorbed and millet is fluffy. Let stand, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 2. While millet cooks, press the extra-firm tofu to remove excess water. Crumble the tofu into a large bowl. Add nutritional yeast, garlic powder, turmeric, smoked paprika, black salt (if using), salt, black pepper, and soy sauce. Mix well to ensure the tofu is evenly coated with the spices. 3. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seasoned tofu crumbles and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and slightly crispy. 4. Add the chopped kale to the skillet with the tofu. Cook for 3-5 minutes, stirring until the kale wilts. Stir in the halved cherry tomatoes and cook for another 2 minutes until slightly softened. 5. Divide the cooked millet between two bowls. Top with the spiced tofu, kale, and tomato mixture. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Spicy Black Bean & Quinoa Bowl with Roasted Sweet Potatoes and Pepitas

🥜 27g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Quinoa
  • 1.5 cup Water
  • 2 whole Sweet potato, medium, peeled and diced
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 whole Canned black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 0.5 cup Salsa
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 4 tbsp Pumpkin seeds (pepitas), toasted
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. Toss diced sweet potatoes with olive oil, chili powder, cumin, salt, and black pepper on the prepared baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, until tender and lightly caramelized. 3. While sweet potatoes roast, rinse quinoa thoroughly under cold water. In a small saucepan, combine quinoa and water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed and quinoa is fluffy. Let stand, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 4. In a separate small saucepan, combine the rinsed and drained black beans with salsa. Heat over medium-low heat for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until warmed through. 5. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two serving bowls. Top each with roasted sweet potatoes, warm black beans, fresh cilantro, and toasted pumpkin seeds. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Baked Peanut Tofu with Roasted Broccoli and Brown Rice

🥜 45g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 4 tbsp Peanut butter, natural, unsweetened
  • 2 tbsp Soy sauce (or tamari for GF)
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup (or agave)
  • 1 tsp Sriracha (optional)
  • 2 tbsp Water
  • 4 cup Broccoli florets
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.8 cup Brown rice, uncooked
  • 1.5 cup Water for rice
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Press extra-firm tofu to remove excess water, then cut into 1-inch cubes. 2. In a medium bowl, whisk together peanut butter, soy sauce, rice vinegar, maple syrup, sriracha (if using), and 2 tablespoons of water until smooth. Add the tofu cubes to the bowl and gently toss to coat them evenly with the peanut sauce. 3. Spread the coated tofu cubes in a single layer on one half of the prepared baking sheet. On the other half, toss the broccoli florets with olive oil and salt. 4. Bake for 25-30 minutes, flipping tofu and broccoli halfway through, until tofu is golden and slightly crispy, and broccoli is tender-crisp. 5. While tofu and broccoli bake, cook the brown rice. Combine brown rice and 1.5 cups water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 35-40 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Let stand, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 6. Divide the cooked brown rice between two plates. Top with the baked peanut tofu and roasted broccoli. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Seitan & Chickpea Hash with Spinach and Roasted Red Peppers

🥜 35g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Seitan (pre-cooked)
  • 240 g Chickpeas (canned, drained and rinsed)
  • 2 whole Red Bell Peppers
  • 4 cup Fresh Spinach
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.2 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Wash and core the red bell peppers, then cut them into 1-inch pieces. Toss with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes, or until tender and slightly charred. 2. While peppers are roasting, cut the seitan into bite-sized pieces or crumble it. Heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it starts to brown and crisp. 3. Stir in the drained and rinsed chickpeas, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Cook for another 3-5 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld. 4. Add the vegetable broth and spinach to the skillet. Cook, stirring frequently, until the spinach has wilted completely, about 2-3 minutes. 5. Finally, gently fold in the roasted red bell peppers. Serve the hash immediately in two bowls.

High-Protein Lentil & Hemp Seed Bulgur Bowl with Roasted Zucchini and Lemon-Herb Dressing

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Bulgur Wheat
  • 1.5 cup Cooked Green Lentils
  • 4 tbsp Hemp Seeds
  • 2 whole Zucchini
  • 1 cup Cherry Tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 tsp Dried Oregano
  • 0.5 tsp Dried Thyme
  • 3 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 1 tsp Dijon Mustard
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Parsley (chopped)
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 cup Water
Instructions
1. Rinse the bulgur wheat thoroughly. In a medium saucepan, combine the bulgur with 2 cups of water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 12-15 minutes, or until the water is absorbed and the bulgur is tender. Fluff with a fork and set aside. 2. While the bulgur cooks, preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Wash the zucchini and cut it into half-moon slices. Toss the zucchini and cherry tomatoes with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, dried oregano, dried thyme, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes, or until the zucchini is tender and slightly caramelized. 3. Prepare the lemon-herb dressing by whisking together the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, and fresh parsley in a small bowl. Season with salt and pepper to taste. 4. Divide the cooked bulgur between two bowls. Top with the cooked green lentils, roasted zucchini and cherry tomatoes. Drizzle generously with the lemon-herb dressing. 5. Finish each bowl with a sprinkle of hemp seeds before serving. This meal can be prepped by cooking the bulgur and lentils ahead of time, roasting the vegetables, and storing the dressing separately.

David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-Firm Tofu (pressed and cubed)
  • 1 cup Brown Rice (dry)
  • 2 tbsp Tomato Paste
  • 400 g Crushed Tomatoes (canned)
  • 120 ml Full-Fat Coconut Milk
  • 1 tbsp Grated Fresh Ginger
  • 2 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Chili Powder (optional)
  • 4 cup Fresh Spinach
  • 120 ml Vegetable Broth
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Cilantro (chopped, for garnish)
Instructions
1. Cook the brown rice according to package instructions. Typically, combine 1 cup of brown rice with 2 cups of water in a saucepan, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 40-45 minutes until water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork. 2. While rice cooks, press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15-20 minutes to remove excess water. Cut the pressed tofu into 1-inch cubes. 3. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet or pot over medium-high heat. High-Protein Savory Freekeh Bowl with Black Beans and Sun-Dried Tomatoes

🥜 25g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 120 g Cracked Freekeh
  • 500 ml Water
  • 250 ml Vegetable Broth
  • 400 g Canned Black Beans, rinsed and drained
  • 40 g Sun-Dried Tomatoes (oil-packed), drained and chopped
  • 100 g Baby Spinach
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Tahini
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 2 tbsp Warm Water (for tahini dressing)
Instructions
Rinse the cracked freekeh under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine the rinsed freekeh with 500ml water and 250ml vegetable broth. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until the freekeh is tender and most of the liquid has been absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside. While the freekeh is cooking, prepare the black beans by rinsing them thoroughly under cold water and draining well. Chop the sun-dried tomatoes into small pieces. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the tahini, lemon juice, and 2 tablespoons of warm water until a smooth, pourable dressing forms; add more water if needed to reach desired consistency. Once the freekeh is cooked, return the saucepan to medium-low heat. Stir in the rinsed black beans, chopped sun-dried tomatoes, baby spinach, nutritional yeast, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, stirring continuously, or until the spinach has wilted completely. Divide the savory freekeh mixture evenly into two bowls. Drizzle each bowl generously with the tahini-lemon dressing before serving warm.

High-Protein Seitan & Green Lentil Pilaf with Roasted Carrots and Parsley

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Seitan, finely diced
  • 100 g Green Lentils, dry
  • 500 ml Vegetable Broth
  • 200 g Carrots, peeled and diced
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Coriander
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 0.2 cup Fresh Parsley, chopped
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (390°F). Toss the diced carrots with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 1/2 teaspoon of cumin, and a pinch of salt and pepper on a baking sheet. Spread them in a single layer and roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and slightly caramelized, stirring halfway through. Rinse the green lentils thoroughly under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine the rinsed lentils with 500ml vegetable broth. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the lentils are tender and have absorbed most of the liquid. While lentils cook, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the finely diced seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned and slightly crispy. Stir in the remaining 1/2 teaspoon of cumin, coriander, turmeric, salt, and black pepper, cooking for another minute until fragrant. Combine the cooked lentils, browned seitan, and roasted carrots in the large skillet or saucepan. Stir well to combine all ingredients evenly. Remove from heat, then stir in the fresh chopped parsley and lemon juice. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed. Divide the pilaf evenly into two serving bowls and serve warm.

High-Protein Glazed Tofu & Asparagus with Toasted Quinoa

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-Firm Tofu, pressed and cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 160 g Quinoa, dry
  • 320 ml Vegetable Broth (for quinoa)
  • 250 g Asparagus, woody ends trimmed
  • 3 tbsp Soy Sauce or Tamari
  • 2 tbsp Maple Syrup
  • 1 tbsp Rice Vinegar
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp Cornstarch
  • 1 tsp Sesame Oil
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil (for cooking)
  • 1 tsp Toasted Sesame Seeds (for garnish)
Instructions
First, press the extra-firm tofu for at least 30 minutes to remove excess water, then cut into uniform 1-inch cubes. Preheat oven to 200°C (390°F). In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce or tamari, maple syrup, rice vinegar, garlic powder, cornstarch, and sesame oil to create the glaze; set aside. Rinse the quinoa thoroughly under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine the rinsed quinoa with 320ml vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all liquid is absorbed. Remove from heat and let sit, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. Meanwhile, spread the tofu cubes in a single layer on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake for 20 minutes, flipping halfway through, until slightly golden and firm. During the last 10 minutes of tofu cooking, add the trimmed asparagus to the baking sheet, tossing lightly with a drizzle of olive oil, salt, and pepper if desired, to roast alongside the tofu. Pour the prepared glaze over the baked tofu and asparagus on the baking sheet, tossing gently to coat evenly. Return to the oven for another 5-7 minutes, or until the glaze thickens and becomes sticky, coating the tofu and asparagus beautifully. Divide the toasted quinoa evenly into two plates or bowls. Top each with the glazed tofu and asparagus. Garnish generously with toasted sesame seeds before serving hot.

High-Protein Savory Marinated Tofu Cubes with Sesame Seeds

🥜 22g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-Firm Tofu, pressed and cut into 1/2-inch cubes
  • 3 tbsp Soy Sauce or Tamari
  • 1 tbsp Rice Vinegar
  • 1 tsp Maple Syrup
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp Toasted Sesame Oil
  • 1 tbsp Toasted Sesame Seeds
Instructions
Begin by pressing the extra-firm tofu for at least 30 minutes to remove excess moisture, then cut it into uniform 1/2-inch cubes. In a medium bowl, whisk together the soy sauce or tamari, rice vinegar, maple syrup, garlic powder, and toasted sesame oil to create the marinade. Add the pressed and cubed tofu to the marinade, ensuring all pieces are well coated. Gently toss or stir the tofu, then cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld. For best results, marinate for 2-4 hours, or even overnight, stirring occasionally. This step is crucial for flavor absorption. Once marinated, preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F). Spread the tofu cubes in a single layer on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway through, until the tofu is firm and lightly golden. Alternatively, you can air fry at 180°C (350°F) for 15-20 minutes, shaking the basket periodically. This snack can be prepped ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.

High-Protein Chickpea & Hemp Seed Scramble with Roasted Asparagus and Quinoa

🥜 43g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 396 g extra-firm tofu
  • 400 g canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 250 g asparagus spears, trimmed and chopped
  • 150 g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 3 cup kale, destemmed and chopped
  • 2 cup cooked quinoa
  • 4 tbsp hemp seeds
  • 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp turmeric powder
  • 0.2 tsp black salt (kala namak, optional for eggy flavor)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp salt
  • 0.2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss the chopped asparagus spears with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 10-12 minutes, or until tender-crisp. Stir in the hemp seeds and cook for 1 minute more. To serve, divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls. Top with the scramble mixture and the roasted asparagus. Enjoy immediately.

Snack 2

High-Protein Chili-Garlic Roasted Peanuts & Hemp Seeds

🥜 25g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 12 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 120 g raw peanuts
  • 6 tbsp hemp seeds
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp tamari or soy sauce
  • 0.5 tsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp olive oil
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 160°C (325°F). In a medium bowl, combine the raw peanuts with olive oil, chili powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, tamari, and maple syrup. Toss well to ensure the peanuts are evenly coated. Spread the seasoned peanuts in a single layer on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
