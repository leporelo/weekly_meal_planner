High-Protein Spicy Black Bean & Tofu Scramble with Roasted Sweet Potatoes and Spinach

🥜 44.7g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Sweet potato
  • 340 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 240 g Black beans, canned, rinsed and drained
  • 90 g Spinach
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Chili powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Wash and peel the sweet potato. Cut it into 1/2-inch cubes. Toss the sweet potato cubes with 1 tbsp olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and slightly caramelized, flipping halfway. 2. While sweet potatoes roast, press the extra-firm tofu to remove excess water. Crumble the pressed tofu into a large non-stick skillet using your hands. Add 1 tbsp olive oil to the skillet and place over medium heat. Cook the tofu for 7-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it starts to brown slightly and some moisture has evaporated. 3. Stir in the nutritional yeast, turmeric, smoked paprika, chili powder, garlic powder, cumin, salt, and pepper into the tofu. Cook for another 2 minutes, ensuring the spices are well distributed and fragrant. 4. Add the rinsed and drained black beans to the skillet, stirring to combine with the tofu. Cook for 3-5 minutes, allowing the beans to warm through. 5. Finally, add the spinach to the skillet. Cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring until the spinach wilts down completely. 6. Divide the scramble and roasted sweet potatoes evenly into two bowls. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Balsamic Glazed Tempeh & Quinoa Bowl with Roasted Asparagus and Cherry Tomatoes

🥜 34.7g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 227 g Tempeh
  • 0.8 cup Quinoa, dry
  • 250 g Asparagus
  • 1.5 cup Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 3 tbsp Balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 2 tbsp Soy sauce (or tamari for GF)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
  • 0.1 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Cook quinoa according to package directions. Typically, combine 0.75 cup quinoa with 1.5 cups water or vegetable broth in a small pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork. 2. While quinoa cooks, preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Slice the tempeh into 1/2-inch thick rectangles. In a small bowl, whisk together balsamic vinegar, maple syrup, soy sauce, garlic powder, and oregano to create the glaze. 3. Toss the trimmed asparagus and halved cherry tomatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Spread in a single layer. 4. Arrange the tempeh slices on a separate part of the baking sheet or a second baking sheet. Brush both sides of the tempeh generously with the balsamic glaze. 5. Roast the vegetables and tempeh for 15-20 minutes. Flip the tempeh halfway through and brush with any remaining glaze. The vegetables should be tender-crisp, and the tempeh lightly browned and caramelized. 6. Divide the cooked quinoa into two bowls. Top each with roasted asparagus, cherry tomatoes, and balsamic-glazed tempeh. Serve warm.

High-Protein Creamy White Bean & Kale Farro Bowl with Pan-Seared Seitan

🥜 50g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Farro, dry
  • 200 g Seitan, sliced
  • 1.5 cup Canned cannellini beans (white beans), rinsed and drained
  • 4 cup Kale, stems removed, chopped
  • 1 cup Vegetable broth
  • 3 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Dried rosemary
  • 1 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Cook farro according to package directions. Typically, combine 0.75 cup farro with 2.25 cups water or vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 25-30 minutes, or until tender and chewy. Drain any excess liquid. 2. While farro cooks, heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seitan slices and pan-sear for 3-4 minutes per side, until golden brown and slightly crispy. Remove seitan from the skillet and set aside. 3. In the same skillet, add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add the chopped kale and sauté for 3-5 minutes until it begins to wilt. 4. Stir in the rinsed cannellini beans, vegetable broth, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, and rosemary. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens slightly and the beans are heated through. 5. Remove from heat and stir in the lemon juice, salt, and pepper. 6. Divide the cooked farro into two bowls. Top with the creamy white bean and kale mixture, then arrange the seared seitan pieces on top. Serve warm.

High-Protein Tamari Roasted Almonds & Pumpkin Seeds

🥜 20g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Whole raw almonds
  • 0.5 cup Raw pumpkin seeds (pepitas)
  • 1 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Maple syrup (optional)
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. In a medium bowl, combine the whole raw almonds and raw pumpkin seeds. 3. Add tamari, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and the optional maple syrup to the bowl. Toss thoroughly until the nuts and seeds are evenly coated. 4. Spread the coated nuts and seeds in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. 5. Roast for 15-20 minutes, stirring halfway through, until they are lightly golden brown and fragrant. Be careful not to burn them. 6. Remove from the oven and let them cool completely on the baking sheet. They will become crispier as they cool. Divide into two equal portions for snacking. Store any leftovers in an airtight container.

High-Protein Cheesy Tofu Scramble with Kale and Toasted Whole Wheat English Muffins

🥜 47.5g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 15 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 2 cup Fresh kale, chopped
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.2 tsp Black salt (kala namak)
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 whole Whole wheat English muffins
Instructions
1. Crumble the pressed extra-firm tofu into a bowl with your hands or a fork until it resembles scrambled eggs. 2. In a small bowl, whisk together the nutritional yeast, garlic powder, turmeric powder, black salt, and black pepper. Set aside. 3. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the diced red bell pepper and sauté for 3-5 minutes until slightly softened. 4. Add the crumbled tofu to the skillet and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until some moisture has evaporated and the tofu starts to brown slightly. 5. Stir in the chopped kale and cook for another 2-3 minutes, until the kale has wilted. 6. Sprinkle the nutritional yeast mixture over the tofu and kale, stirring well to combine and coat evenly. Cook for 1-2 more minutes, ensuring all ingredients are heated through. 7. While the scramble finishes cooking, toast the whole wheat English muffins until golden brown. 8. Serve the cheesy tofu scramble immediately alongside the toasted English muffins.

High-Protein Mediterranean Seitan & Lentil Buckwheat Bowl with Roasted Peppers and Olives

🥜 57g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Seitan, sliced or cubed
  • 1 cup Dried green lentils
  • 0.5 cup Dry buckwheat groats
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 whole Yellow bell pepper, sliced
  • 0.5 cup Pitted Kalamata olives, halved
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cup Cucumber, diced
  • 0.2 cup Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss the sliced red and yellow bell peppers with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1/2 tsp dried oregano on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes, or until tender and slightly charred. 2. While peppers roast, rinse the green lentils thoroughly. Combine lentils with 3 cups of water in a saucepan and bring to a boil. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until tender. Drain any excess water. 3. In a separate saucepan, combine buckwheat groats with 1 cup of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes until water is absorbed and buckwheat is tender. 4. Heat the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seitan slices/cubes and cook for 5-7 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden brown and slightly crispy. Season with salt and pepper. 5. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked lentils and buckwheat between two large serving bowls. Top with roasted bell peppers, seared seitan, halved Kalamata olives, cherry tomatoes, and diced cucumber. 6. Drizzle with lemon juice and sprinkle with fresh chopped parsley. Serve warm or at room temperature.

High-Protein Crispy Peanut Tofu and Edamame with Brown Rice and Roasted Bok Choy

🥜 57.5g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 350 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
  • 1.5 cup Frozen shelled edamame
  • 0.5 cup Dry brown rice
  • 4 whole Bok choy, halved lengthwise
  • 4 tbsp Creamy peanut butter
  • 4 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 2 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds (for garnish)
Instructions
1. Cook brown rice according to package instructions. Typically, combine 0.5 cup brown rice with 1 cup water, bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 40-45 minutes until water is absorbed. 2. While rice cooks, preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss the halved bok choy with a drizzle of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 10-15 minutes until tender and slightly caramelized. 3. In a medium bowl, whisk together peanut butter, tamari, rice vinegar, maple syrup, garlic powder, grated ginger, and sesame oil until smooth to create the peanut sauce. 4. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the pressed and cubed tofu in a single layer. Cook for 5-7 minutes, turning occasionally, until all sides are golden brown and crispy. 5. Add the frozen shelled edamame to the skillet with the crispy tofu. Cook for 3-5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until heated through. 6. Pour about two-thirds of the peanut sauce over the tofu and edamame, stirring gently to coat. Cook for 1-2 minutes until the sauce slightly thickens. Reserve the remaining sauce for serving. 7. To serve, divide the cooked brown rice between two bowls. Top with the crispy peanut tofu and edamame mixture, and the roasted bok choy. Drizzle with the remaining peanut sauce and garnish with sesame seeds.

High-Protein Spiced Black Lentil & Millet Hash with Roasted Cherry Tomatoes

🥜 35g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Black Lentils (dry)
  • 1 cup Millet (dry)
  • 4 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 2 cup Cherry Tomatoes
  • 4 cup Spinach
  • 4 whole Garlic
  • 1 tsp Ground Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Ground Coriander
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Rinse black lentils and millet thoroughly. In a medium pot, combine rinsed lentils, millet, and 4 cups of vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 25-30 minutes, or until water is absorbed and grains are tender. 2. While lentils and millet cook, preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Halve the cherry tomatoes and mince the garlic. Toss cherry tomatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes, until slightly softened and caramelized. 3. In a large skillet, heat the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant. Stir in ground cumin, coriander, turmeric, and smoked paprika, cooking for another 30 seconds until aromatic. 4. Add the cooked lentil and millet mixture to the skillet with the spices. Stir well to combine. Add the spinach and cook until wilted, about 2-3 minutes. Season with salt and pepper to taste. 5. Divide the hash between two bowls and top with the roasted cherry tomatoes. Serve warm.

High-Protein Seitan & Chickpea Tagine-Inspired Bowl with Toasted Pearl Couscous and Roasted Carrots

🥜 46g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Seitan
  • 400 g Chickpeas (canned, drained and rinsed)
  • 1 cup Pearl Couscous
  • 1.5 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 2 whole Carrots
  • 1 cup Crushed Tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp Tomato Paste
  • 3 whole Garlic
  • 1.5 tsp Ground Cumin
  • 1 tsp Ground Coriander
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Ground Ginger
  • 0.2 tsp Cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.2 cup Fresh Parsley (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Peel and chop carrots into 1-inch pieces. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and slightly caramelized. 2. While carrots roast, prepare the pearl couscous. Heat 0.5 tbsp olive oil in a small saucepan over medium heat. Add pearl couscous and toast for 2-3 minutes until lightly golden. Add 1.5 cups vegetable broth, bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 10-12 minutes, or until liquid is absorbed and couscous is tender. Fluff with a fork. 3. Cut seitan into bite-sized pieces. Mince garlic. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat the remaining 0.5 tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes until lightly browned. Remove seitan from the pot and set aside. 4. Add minced garlic to the pot and sauté for 1 minute. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, ground cumin, coriander, turmeric, smoked paprika, ginger, and cinnamon. Bring to a simmer, then add the drained chickpeas and cooked seitan back to the pot. Cook for 10-15 minutes, allowing flavors to meld. If the mixture is too thick, add a splash of vegetable broth. 5. Stir in fresh parsley and lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Divide the toasted pearl couscous into two bowls. Top with the seitan and chickpea tagine, and serve with the roasted carrots.

High-Protein Miso-Glazed Tempeh & Adzuki Bean Bowl with Steamed Bok Choy and Brown Rice

🥜 33g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Tempeh
  • 400 g Adzuki Beans (canned, drained and rinsed)
  • 1 cup Brown Rice (dry)
  • 2 cup Water (for rice)
  • 3 whole Bok Choy
  • 2 tbsp White Miso Paste
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tbsp Maple Syrup
  • 1 tbsp Rice Vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Fresh Ginger (grated)
  • 1 tbsp Sesame Oil
  • 1 tsp Sesame Seeds (for garnish)
Instructions
1. Rinse brown rice thoroughly. In a medium pot, combine brown rice and 2 cups of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 40-45 minutes, or until water is absorbed and rice is tender. Keep covered off the heat for 10 minutes before fluffing. 2. While rice cooks, prepare the miso glaze. In a small bowl, whisk together white miso paste, tamari, maple syrup, rice vinegar, grated ginger, and sesame oil until smooth. 3. Cut tempeh into 1/2-inch thick slices or cubes. In a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat, add the tempeh and cook for 5-7 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden brown. Pour the miso glaze over the tempeh, ensuring each piece is coated. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, stirring gently, until the glaze thickens and caramelizes slightly. 4. Wash the bok choy and trim the ends. Cut each bok choy head in half lengthwise. Steam the bok choy in a steamer basket over boiling water for 3-5 minutes, until tender-crisp. Alternatively, you can sauté it lightly in a pan with a splash of water or sesame oil. 5. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked brown rice between two serving bowls. Top with the miso-glazed tempeh, drained adzuki beans, and steamed bok choy. Garnish with sesame seeds and serve immediately.

High-Protein Mung Bean & Spinach Scramble with Roasted Cherry Tomatoes and Farro

🥜 60.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 100 g Dry Mung Beans
  • 300 g Extra-Firm Tofu
  • 200 g Cherry Tomatoes
  • 100 g Fresh Spinach
  • 100 g Dry Farro
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 1 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black Salt (Kala Namak)
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. Cook the farro according to package instructions (typically 1 cup farro to 3 cups water, simmer for 25-30 minutes until tender). Drain any excess water. 3. While farro cooks, wash cherry tomatoes and spread them on the prepared baking sheet. Drizzle with 1 tbsp olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper. Roast for 15-20 minutes, or until softened and slightly burst. 4. If using dry mung beans, cook them separately according to package instructions until tender. If using canned, rinse and drain thoroughly. 5. Press the extra-firm tofu to remove excess water. Crumble it into a large non-stick pan. 6. Heat the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil in the pan over medium heat. Add the crumbled tofu, turmeric powder, black salt, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Sauté for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tofu is lightly browned and heated through. 7. Stir in the cooked mung beans, nutritional yeast, and fresh spinach. Cook for another 3-5 minutes, until the spinach has wilted. 8. To serve, divide the cooked farro between two bowls. Top with the mung bean and spinach scramble and the roasted cherry tomatoes. Mix gently before enjoying.

High-Protein Southwest Tofu & Black Bean Farro Bowl with Roasted Corn and Avocado Crema

🥜 61.5g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Extra-Firm Tofu
  • 400 g Canned Black Beans
  • 100 g Dry Farro
  • 200 g Corn Kernels (frozen or fresh)
  • 1 whole Avocado
  • 2 tbsp Lime Juice
  • 0.2 cup Fresh Cilantro, chopped
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 1 tsp Ground Cumin
  • 1 tsp Chili Powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 3 tbsp Water
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. Cook the farro according to package instructions (typically 1 cup farro to 3 cups water, simmer for 25-30 minutes until tender). Drain any excess water. 3. Press the extra-firm tofu to remove excess water, then crumble it into bite-sized pieces or small crumbles. In a bowl, toss the crumbled tofu with 1 tbsp olive oil, smoked paprika, cumin, chili powder, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until well coated. 4. Spread the seasoned tofu crumbles and corn kernels on the prepared baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, until the tofu is golden brown and the corn is slightly charred. 5. While tofu and corn roast, prepare the avocado crema: In a small food processor or blender, combine the avocado, lime juice, 2 tbsp of the chopped cilantro, and 3 tbsp water. Blend until smooth and creamy. Season with a pinch of salt if desired. 6. Rinse and drain the canned black beans thoroughly. 7. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked farro between two serving bowls. Top each with the roasted tofu and corn, black beans, and a generous dollop of avocado crema. Garnish with the remaining fresh cilantro.

High-Protein Spiced Seitan & Chickpea Coconut Curry with Basmati Rice and Spinach

🥜 50.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 250 g Seitan
  • 400 g Canned Chickpeas
  • 100 g Dry Basmati Rice
  • 400 ml Full-Fat Coconut Milk
  • 400 g Canned Diced Tomatoes
  • 150 g Fresh Spinach
  • 2 tbsp Curry Powder
  • 1 tsp Ground Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Ground Cumin
  • 1 tbsp Grated Fresh Ginger
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 100 ml Vegetable Broth
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 0.2 cup Fresh Cilantro, for garnish
Instructions
1. Cook the basmati rice according to package instructions. Typically, 1 cup of rice to 1.5-2 cups of water, simmer for 15-20 minutes until water is absorbed and rice is fluffy. 2. While rice cooks, cut the seitan into bite-sized pieces. Rinse and drain the canned chickpeas. 3. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot or deep skillet over medium heat. Add the grated fresh ginger and garlic powder, sauté for 1 minute until fragrant. 4. Add the seitan pieces to the pot and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned. 5. Stir in the curry powder, ground turmeric, and ground cumin. Cook for another minute, stirring constantly, to toast the spices. 6. Pour in the canned diced tomatoes (undrained), coconut milk, and vegetable broth. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for 10-15 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld. 7. Stir in the rinsed and drained chickpeas and the fresh spinach. Continue to cook for 3-5 minutes, or until the spinach has wilted and the chickpeas are heated through. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. 8. Serve the spiced seitan and chickpea coconut curry hot over the cooked basmati rice. Garnish with fresh cilantro, if desired.

High-Protein Tamari Roasted Walnuts & Sunflower Seeds

🥜 22.75g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 15 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 150 g Walnuts
  • 100 g Sunflower Seeds
  • 3 tbsp Tamari
  • 1.5 tsp Maple Syrup
  • 0.8 tsp Garlic Powder
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 160°C (325°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. In a medium bowl, combine the walnuts and sunflower seeds. 3. In a small separate bowl, whisk together the tamari, maple syrup (if using), and garlic powder. 4. Pour the tamari mixture over the walnuts and sunflower seeds. Remove from the oven and let cool completely on the baking sheet. They will crisp up further as they cool. Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to one week. 🥜 39g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 1.5 cup Cooked black beans
  • 1 cup Uncooked millet
  • 150 g Fresh spinach
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (kala namak)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2.5 cup Water or vegetable broth
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper

High-Protein Peanut Tempeh & Chickpea Rice Bowl with Roasted Broccolini

🥜 42g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 250 g Tempeh
  • 1.5 cup Cooked chickpeas
  • 1 cup Uncooked brown rice
  • 300 g Broccolini
  • 4 tbsp Peanut butter
  • 3 tbsp Tamari or soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Sriracha (optional)
  • 2 tbsp Lime juice
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 cup Water

High-Protein Lentil & Mushroom Shepherd's Pie with Creamy Sweet Potato Topping

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 25 min prep 🔥 60 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Brown or green lentils (dry)
  • 600 g Sweet potatoes
  • 200 g Cremini mushrooms
  • 3 cup Vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 cup Cashews (raw, unsalted)
  • 1 tsp Thyme (dried)
  • 0.5 tsp Rosemary (dried)
  • 1 tbsp Tamari or soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Hemp seeds (for topping)
Instructions
Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Peel and chop sweet potatoes into 1-inch cubes. Boil or steam them until very tender, about 15-20 minutes. Drain well. For the cashew cream, soak cashews in hot water for at least 15 minutes, then drain. Blend soaked cashews with 0.5 cup fresh water until completely smooth and creamy. Set aside. In a large saucepan, rinse lentils and combine with 2 cups vegetable broth, thyme, and rosemary. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes until lentils are tender. Meanwhile, slice mushrooms. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a separate skillet over medium-high heat. Add mushrooms and cook until browned and most liquid has evaporated. Stir in tomato paste and tamari to the lentils, then add the cooked mushrooms. Season with 0.25 tsp salt and 0.1 tsp black pepper, adding more broth if too dry. For the sweet potato topping, mash the cooked sweet potatoes with 2 tbsp nutritional yeast, 0.25 tsp salt, and the prepared cashew cream until smooth. Transfer the lentil and mushroom mixture into a 9x9 inch baking dish. Carefully spread the creamy sweet potato topping over the lentils. Sprinkle with hemp seeds. Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until the topping is lightly golden and the filling is bubbly. Let it rest for a few minutes before serving.

Ingredients
  • 140 g Raw almonds
  • 4 tbsp Hemp seeds
  • 2 tbsp Tamari or soy sauce

  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
Instructions

David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 400 g Canned cannellini beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 1 whole Yellow bell pepper
  • 100 g Fresh spinach
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Black salt (kala namak)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Core and deseed the bell peppers, then slice them into thin strips. Add the tofu and bean mixture to the skillet and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tofu is heated through and slightly browned. High-Protein Spiced Tempeh & Lentil Wild Rice Bowl with Roasted Cauliflower and Tahini Dressing

🥜 52g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 225 g Tempeh
  • 120 g Dry brown lentils
  • 120 g Dry wild rice
  • 1 whole Cauliflower head
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 2 whole Garlic cloves, minced
  • 1.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Ground coriander
  • 0.5 tsp Chili powder
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Water
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Wash and chop the cauliflower into bite-sized florets. Toss the cauliflower with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet, then roast for 25-30 minutes until tender and caramelized, flipping halfway through. Cook the wild rice: Rinse the wild rice thoroughly. In a small saucepan, combine 120g wild rice with 2.5 cups (approx 590ml) water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 40-45 minutes, or until the rice is tender and has absorbed the water. Fluff with a fork and set aside. Cook the lentils: While the rice cooks, rinse the lentils. In another small saucepan, combine 120g lentils with 3 cups (approx 710ml) water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until tender but not mushy. Drain any excess water and set aside. Prepare the tempeh: Cut the tempeh into 1-inch cubes. In a medium bowl, whisk together the tamari, smoked paprika, cumin, coriander, chili powder, and 2 tablespoons of water to form a marinade. Add the tempeh cubes and toss to coat. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the marinated tempeh and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and slightly crispy. Make the tahini dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together the tahini, lemon juice, minced garlic, 1 tablespoon of olive oil, and 2-4 tablespoons of warm water until smooth and creamy. Season with a pinch of salt if desired. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked wild rice, lentils, roasted cauliflower, and spiced tempeh between two serving bowls. Drizzle generously with the tahini dressing. Serve warm.

High-Protein Seitan & White Bean Marinara with Whole Wheat Pasta

🥜 78g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Whole wheat pasta
  • 400 g Seitan, cubed or sliced
  • 800 g Canned crushed tomatoes
  • 400 g Canned cannellini beans, rinsed and drained
  • 4 whole Garlic cloves, minced
  • 1.5 tsp Dried basil
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 tsp Dried parsley
  • 0.5 tsp Red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Add the whole wheat pasta and cook according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve about 1 cup (240ml) of pasta water before draining the pasta. Set aside. While the pasta cooks, heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a large deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the cubed or sliced seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned on all sides. Remove the seitan from the skillet and set aside. Add the minced garlic to the same skillet and cook for 1 minute until fragrant, being careful not to burn it. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, then stir in the dried basil, oregano, parsley, red pepper flakes (if using), salt, and black pepper. Bring the sauce to a gentle simmer. Return the seitan to the skillet, along with the rinsed and drained cannellini beans. Stir to combine. Cover and simmer for 10-15 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld and the seitan to absorb some of the sauce. If the sauce becomes too thick, add a splash of the reserved pasta water. Serve the seitan and white bean marinara immediately over the cooked whole wheat pasta. Garnish with fresh parsley if desired.
