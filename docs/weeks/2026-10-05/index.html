High-Protein Savory Buckwheat Groats with Smoked Tofu and Spinach

🥜 46g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Buckwheat Groats (dry)
  • 300 g Smoked Tofu
  • 200 g Fresh Spinach
  • 2 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 2 tbsp Tamari
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
Instructions
Rinse buckwheat groats thoroughly under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine the rinsed buckwheat groats and vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until the liquid is absorbed and buckwheat is tender. While the buckwheat cooks, press the smoked tofu to remove excess water, then crumble it into small pieces. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat, add the crumbled smoked tofu, and sauté for 5-7 minutes until lightly browned and slightly crispy. Add the fresh spinach to the skillet with the tofu, stirring until wilted, about 2-3 minutes. Stir in tamari, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, and black pepper. Once the buckwheat is cooked, fluff it with a fork and then gently fold it into the tofu and spinach mixture in the skillet. Serve immediately in bowls, ensuring even distribution of all ingredients.

High-Protein White Bean & Artichoke Heart Seitan Bowl with Roasted Root Vegetables and Lemon-Tahini Dressing

🥜 68.7g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Quinoa (dry)
  • 300 g Seitan (pre-cooked)
  • 1 whole Canned White Beans (e.g., cannellini), drained and rinsed
  • 200 g Artichoke Hearts (canned/jarred, in water, drained)
  • 2 whole Carrots
  • 2 whole Parsnips
  • 3 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 3 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 3 tbsp Tahini
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp Dried Oregano
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Parsley (for garnish, optional)
Instructions
Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Wash and chop carrots and parsnips into bite-sized pieces. Toss them with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 25-30 minutes, or until tender and slightly caramelized, flipping halfway through. Meanwhile, cook quinoa according to package directions. In a small bowl, whisk together tahini, lemon juice, 2 tablespoons of olive oil, garlic powder, dried oregano, and a pinch of salt and pepper to create the dressing; add a splash of water if needed to reach desired consistency. Dice the seitan into bite-sized cubes. Once quinoa is cooked, fluff with a fork. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two large bowls. Top with roasted root vegetables, drained white beans, quartered artichoke hearts, and seitan. Drizzle generously with the lemon-tahini dressing and garnish with fresh parsley if desired. This meal works well for meal prep; store components separately and assemble just before serving.

High-Protein Spicy Peanut Butter Tofu & Chickpea Skillet with Red Cabbage and Brown Rice

🥜 61.3g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Brown Rice (dry)
  • 400 g Extra-Firm Tofu (pressed and cubed)
  • 1 whole Canned Chickpeas (drained and rinsed)
  • 0.5 whole Red Cabbage (thinly sliced)
  • 6 tbsp Natural Peanut Butter (unsweetened)
  • 3 tbsp Tamari
  • 2 tbsp Rice Vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Sriracha (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp Grated Fresh Ginger
  • 1 tbsp Sesame Oil
  • 2 tbsp Water
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 tbsp Toasted Sesame Seeds (for garnish)
Instructions
First, cook the brown rice according to package instructions. While the rice cooks, prepare the tofu: press extra-firm tofu for at least 15 minutes, then cut it into 1-inch cubes. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tofu cubes and cook for 7-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and slightly crispy on all sides. In a small bowl, whisk together peanut butter, tamari, rice vinegar, sriracha, garlic powder, grated ginger, sesame oil, and 2 tablespoons of water until a smooth sauce forms. Add the drained chickpeas and thinly sliced red cabbage to the skillet with the cooked tofu. Sauté for 3-5 minutes until the cabbage begins to soften. Pour the prepared peanut sauce over the tofu, chickpeas, and cabbage, stirring well to coat everything evenly. Let it simmer for 2-3 minutes to allow the sauce to thicken slightly. Serve the skillet mixture immediately over the cooked brown rice, garnished with toasted sesame seeds. This dish reheats well for meal prep, simply store the rice and skillet mixture separately.

High-Protein Savory Mung Bean Scramble with Roasted Cherry Tomatoes and Whole Wheat Toast

🥜 41g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 100 g Dried Mung Beans
  • 200 g Extra-Firm Tofu, pressed
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 300 g Cherry Tomatoes
  • 200 g Kale, chopped
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (Kala Namak)
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 4 whole Whole Wheat Bread
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Soak mung beans overnight, or quick-soak by boiling for 5 minutes, then letting them sit in hot water for 1 hour. Drain and rinse. 2. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss cherry tomatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until softened and slightly browned. 3. While tomatoes roast, boil the soaked mung beans in fresh water for 15-20 minutes, or until tender but not mushy. Drain well. 4. In a large non-stick pan, heat 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Crumble the pressed tofu directly into the pan. Cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it begins to brown slightly. 5. Add the cooked mung beans, chopped kale, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, turmeric, black salt, salt, and black pepper to the pan. Cook for another 5-7 minutes, stirring frequently, until the kale has wilted and the scramble is heated through. 6. Toast the whole wheat bread. Divide the mung bean scramble and roasted cherry tomatoes between two plates. Serve immediately with the toasted bread.

High-Protein Spiced Chickpea & Lentil Couscous Bowl with Roasted Bell Peppers and Tahini Dressing

🥜 40.5g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 140 g Whole Wheat Couscous
  • 240 g Cooked Chickpeas, rinsed and drained
  • 200 g Cooked Green Lentils, rinsed and drained
  • 3 whole Bell Peppers (mixed colors), cored and sliced
  • 300 g Zucchini, diced
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 4 tbsp Water
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 2 tbsp Hemp Seeds
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). On a large baking sheet, toss the sliced bell peppers and diced zucchini with 1 tbsp olive oil, smoked paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper. Spread in a single layer and roast for 25-30 minutes, or until tender and slightly charred, flipping halfway through. 2. While vegetables roast, prepare the couscous. Bring 280ml of water or vegetable broth to a boil. Remove from heat, stir in the whole wheat couscous, cover, and let sit for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork. 3. For the tahini dressing, whisk together tahini, lemon juice, water, garlic powder, and a pinch of salt until smooth and creamy. Add more water if needed to reach desired consistency. 4. In a large bowl, combine the fluffed couscous, cooked chickpeas, and cooked green lentils. 5. Divide the couscous mixture between two serving bowls. Top with the roasted bell peppers and zucchini. Drizzle generously with the tahini dressing and sprinkle with hemp seeds before serving.

High-Protein Lemon-Herb Roasted Almonds & Roasted Chickpeas

🥜 23g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 100 g Whole Almonds
  • 300 g Cooked Chickpeas, rinsed and thoroughly patted dry
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dried Rosemary
  • 1 tsp Dried Thyme
  • 1 tsp Lemon Zest
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. In a medium bowl, combine the almonds, thoroughly patted dry chickpeas, olive oil, dried rosemary, dried thyme, lemon zest, salt, and black pepper. Remove from oven and let cool completely on the baking sheet. This will help them crisp up further. Store in an airtight container once cooled. Serve as a crunchy, protein-rich snack.

High-Protein Chickpea & Spinach Breakfast Patties with Roasted Tomatoes and Millet

🥜 25g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 240 g Cooked Chickpeas
  • 100 g Fresh Spinach
  • 120 g Cooked Millet
  • 3 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Flaxseed Meal
  • 4 tbsp Water
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 250 g Cherry Tomatoes
  • 0.5 tsp Dried Oregano
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. Halve the cherry tomatoes and toss them with 1 tbsp olive oil, 0.5 tsp dried oregano, and a pinch of salt and pepper. Spread on the prepared baking sheet and roast for 15-20 minutes, or until softened and slightly caramelized. 3. While tomatoes roast, prepare the flax egg by combining 2 tbsp flaxseed meal with 4 tbsp water in a small bowl. Stir and let sit for 5 minutes to thicken. 4. In a large bowl, mash the cooked chickpeas with a fork or potato masher until mostly smooth, leaving some texture. 5. Finely chop the fresh spinach and add it to the mashed chickpeas along with the cooked millet, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, smoked paprika, cumin, 0.5 tsp salt, 0.25 tsp black pepper, and the flax egg. Mix thoroughly until well combined. 6. Divide the mixture into 4 equal portions and shape each into a patty, about 1-inch thick. 7. Heat the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Carefully place the patties in the hot skillet and cook for 5-7 minutes per side, or until golden brown and crispy. 8. Serve two patties per person alongside the roasted cherry tomatoes.

High-Protein Black Lentil & Sweet Potato Grain Bowl with Turmeric Tempeh

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 226 g Tempeh
  • 300 g Sweet Potato
  • 240 g Cooked Black Lentils
  • 160 g Cooked Quinoa
  • 1 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 tbsp Maple Syrup
  • 1 tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon Mustard
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Parsley
  • 100 g Spinach or Mixed Greens
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. Steam the tempeh for 10 minutes to reduce bitterness, then pat dry and cut into 1/2-inch cubes. 3. In a bowl, toss the diced sweet potato with 1 tbsp olive oil, 0.5 tsp turmeric, 0.25 tsp salt, and a pinch of black pepper. Spread on the baking sheet and roast for 20 minutes. 4. While sweet potatoes roast, prepare the tempeh marinade. In a small bowl, whisk together 1 tbsp olive oil, 0.5 tsp turmeric, 0.5 tsp smoked paprika, 0.5 tsp garlic powder, 0.5 tsp cumin powder, 1 tbsp maple syrup, 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar, 1 tsp dijon mustard, 0.25 tsp salt, and a pinch of black pepper. Add the tempeh cubes to the marinade and toss to coat. 5. After 20 minutes, add the marinated tempeh to the baking sheet with the sweet potatoes. Toss gently and return to the oven for another 15-20 minutes, or until tempeh is golden and sweet potatoes are tender. 6. To assemble the bowls, divide the spinach or mixed greens between two large serving bowls. Add half of the cooked quinoa and half of the cooked black lentils to each bowl. 7. Top each bowl with half of the roasted sweet potatoes and half of the turmeric tempeh. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley before serving.

David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-Firm Tofu
  • 240 g Black Beans
  • 160 g Cooked Quinoa
  • 2 whole Bell Peppers
  • 1 whole Avocado
  • 1 whole Lime
  • 2 tsp Chili Powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.8 tsp Salt

High-Protein Smoky Chipotle Tempeh & Black Bean Baked Sweet Potatoes

🥜 37g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 60 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 whole Sweet Potatoes, medium
  • 250 g Tempeh, block
  • 1 whole Black Beans, canned
  • 1 whole Diced Tomatoes, canned
  • 1 tbsp Chipotle in Adobo Sauce
  • 2 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Cilantro, fresh, chopped
  • 0.5 whole Avocado, diced
Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Scrub the sweet potatoes clean under running water, then prick them several times with a fork to allow steam to escape. Place them directly on the oven rack and bake for 45-60 minutes, or until they are very tender when squeezed. 2. While the sweet potatoes are baking, prepare the tempeh filling. Crumble the tempeh block into small pieces using your hands or a fork, mimicking ground meat texture. 3. Heat 1 tbsp of olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tempeh to the skillet and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring frequently, until it is nicely browned and slightly crispy. 4. Stir in the chipotle in adobo sauce, smoked paprika, cumin, and garlic powder with the tempeh. Cook for an additional 1 minute, stirring constantly, until the spices are fragrant. 5. Add the rinsed and drained black beans, canned diced tomatoes (undrained), vegetable broth, salt, and black pepper to the skillet. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce the heat to low and cook for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened slightly. 6. Once the sweet potatoes are cooked, carefully remove them from the oven. Slice each potato lengthwise down the middle, being careful not to cut all the way through. Use a fork to gently fluff the sweet potato flesh inside each half. 7. Divide the smoky tempeh and black bean filling evenly between the two sweet potatoes, spooning it generously into the opened potatoes. 8. Garnish each baked sweet potato with fresh chopped cilantro and diced avocado before serving.
