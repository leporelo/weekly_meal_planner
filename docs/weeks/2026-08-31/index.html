High-Protein Tofu and Spinach Scramble with Toasted Quinoa and Hemp Seeds

🥜 31g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.5 cup Quinoa
  • 1 cup Water
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 140 g Spinach
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.2 tsp Black salt (kala namak)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Hemp seeds
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Rinse the quinoa thoroughly under cold water. Combine quinoa and 1 cup of water in a small pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. While quinoa cooks, press the extra-firm tofu to remove excess water for at least 15 minutes. In a medium bowl, crumble the pressed tofu with your hands or a fork until it resembles scrambled eggs. Stir in nutritional yeast, turmeric powder, black salt (kala namak), garlic powder, salt, and black pepper, mixing well to coat the tofu. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the seasoned tofu mixture to the skillet and cook for 7-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it starts to brown slightly. Add the spinach to the skillet and cook until wilted, about 2-3 minutes. To serve, divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls. Top each with half of the tofu scramble and sprinkle with hemp seeds.

High-Protein Mediterranean Chickpea & Farro Bowl with Roasted Zucchini and Lemon-Tahini Dressing

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.7 cup Farro
  • 2.5 cup Water
  • 2 whole Zucchini
  • 250 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 600 g Canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 0.2 cup Pine nuts
  • 0.2 cup Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 3 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp Water (for dressing)
  • 1 tsp Maple syrup
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
Instructions
Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Rinse farro thoroughly. In a medium pot, combine farro with 2.5 cups of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 25-30 minutes, or until tender. Drain any excess water and set aside. While farro cooks, chop zucchini into bite-sized pieces and halve the cherry tomatoes. On a baking sheet, toss zucchini and cherry tomatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, oregano, salt, and black pepper. Roast for 15-20 minutes, or until vegetables are tender and slightly caramelized. In a separate small pan, toast pine nuts over medium-low heat for 3-5 minutes until lightly golden and fragrant, watching carefully to prevent burning. Remove from heat immediately. For the lemon-tahini dressing, whisk together tahini, lemon juice, 2 tbsp water, maple syrup, and garlic powder in a small bowl until smooth and creamy. Add more water 1 tsp at a time if a thinner consistency is desired. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked farro between two serving bowls. Top each with roasted zucchini and cherry tomatoes, chickpeas, and a generous sprinkle of fresh parsley. Drizzle with the lemon-tahini dressing and garnish with toasted pine nuts.

High-Protein Seitan & Black Bean Skillet with Roasted Sweet Potatoes and Spinach

🥜 36g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 whole Sweet potatoes
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 200 g Seitan, sliced into strips
  • 400 g Canned diced tomatoes
  • 400 g Canned black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 0.5 cup Vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin
  • 100 g Spinach
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)
Instructions
Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Wash and cube sweet potatoes into 1-inch pieces. Toss sweet potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, 0.5 tsp smoked paprika, 0.5 tsp garlic powder, 0.25 tsp salt, and 0.125 tsp black pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and slightly browned, flipping halfway through. While sweet potatoes roast, heat the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seitan strips and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned. Stir in the diced tomatoes, drained black beans, vegetable broth, remaining 0.5 tsp smoked paprika, remaining 0.5 tsp garlic powder, chili powder, cumin, 0.25 tsp salt, and remaining 0.125 tsp black pepper. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to medium-low and let it cook for 10 minutes, allowing flavors to meld and sauce to thicken slightly. Stir in the fresh spinach and cook until just wilted, about 2-3 minutes. Carefully divide the seitan and black bean mixture into two bowls. Top each serving with the roasted sweet potatoes. Garnish with fresh cilantro, if desired.

High-Protein Savory Millet & Smoked Tempeh Breakfast Bowl

🥜 40g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 180 g Millet, dry
  • 470 ml Vegetable broth
  • 226 g Smoked tempeh
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 4 cup Fresh spinach
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
Instructions
1. Rinse the millet under cold water until the water runs clear. In a medium saucepan, combine the rinsed millet with 2 cups (470ml) of vegetable broth and 1/4 teaspoon of salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until all liquid is absorbed and the millet is fluffy. Remove from heat and let sit, covered, for 5 minutes. 2. While the millet cooks, crumble the smoked tempeh into small pieces. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tempeh and sauté for 5-7 minutes, breaking up any large chunks, until lightly browned and crispy. 3. Add 2 tablespoons of tamari, 1/2 teaspoon of garlic powder, and 1/4 teaspoon of black pepper to the tempeh. Stir well and continue to cook for another 2-3 minutes, allowing the tempeh to absorb the flavors. Stir in the nutritional yeast. 4. Add the fresh spinach to the skillet with the tempeh and cook for 2-3 minutes, or until the spinach has wilted. 5. To assemble, divide the cooked millet evenly between two bowls. Top each bowl with the savory tempeh and spinach mixture. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Harissa Lentil & Couscous Bowl with Roasted Bell Peppers and Lemon-Tahini Dressing

🥜 45g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 190 g Green lentils, dry
  • 500 ml Vegetable broth
  • 170 g Whole wheat couscous
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 1 whole Zucchini
  • 2 tbsp Harissa paste
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 whole Garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 4 tbsp Cold water
  • 2 cup Fresh baby spinach
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Wash and dice the red bell pepper and zucchini into 1-inch pieces. In a medium bowl, toss the diced vegetables with 1 tablespoon of olive oil and 2 tablespoons of harissa paste until evenly coated. Spread them in a single layer on a baking sheet and roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and slightly charred, flipping halfway through. 2. While vegetables roast, cook the lentils. Rinse the green lentils thoroughly. In a medium saucepan, combine the rinsed lentils with 2 cups (500ml) of vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until tender. Drain any excess liquid and set aside. 3. Prepare the couscous according to package directions, typically by combining 170g of whole wheat couscous with 1.5 cups of hot water or vegetable broth in a bowl, covering, and letting it sit for 5 minutes until fluffy. For the lemon-tahini dressing, whisk together 4 tablespoons of tahini, 2 tablespoons of lemon juice, 1 minced garlic clove, 4 tablespoons of cold water, and a pinch of salt and pepper in a small bowl until smooth and creamy. Add more water if needed to reach desired consistency. 5. To assemble the bowls, divide the couscous, cooked lentils, roasted harissa vegetables, and fresh baby spinach between two large bowls. Drizzle generously with the lemon-tahini dressing and garnish with fresh chopped parsley. Serve warm.

High-Protein Seitan & Edamame Stir-fry with Brown Rice

🥜 66g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 190 g Brown rice, dry
  • 226 g Seitan, pre-made
  • 300 g Shelled edamame (frozen)
  • 2 cup Broccoli florets
  • 2 whole Carrots, sliced
  • 1 tbsp Ginger, grated
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 4 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 2 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 2 tsp Cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp Cold water
  • 2 tbsp Sesame oil
Instructions
1. Begin by cooking the brown rice. Rinse 190g of brown rice under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine the rice with 3 cups (710ml) of water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 40-45 minutes, or until water is absorbed and rice is tender. Remove from heat and let sit, covered, for 10 minutes. 2. While the rice cooks, prepare the stir-fry sauce. In a small bowl, whisk together 4 tablespoons of tamari, 2 tablespoons of rice vinegar, 2 tablespoons of maple syrup, 2 teaspoons of cornstarch, and 2 tablespoons of cold water until smooth. Set aside. 3. Slice the seitan into bite-sized pieces. If using frozen edamame, thaw by rinsing under hot water. Chop the broccoli into small florets and thinly slice the carrots. Grate the ginger and mince the garlic. 4. Heat 1 tablespoon of sesame oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seitan and stir-fry for 5-7 minutes until lightly browned and slightly crispy. Remove seitan from the wok and set aside. 5. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon of sesame oil to the wok. Add the grated ginger and minced garlic, stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the broccoli florets and sliced carrots, stir-fry for 5-7 minutes until slightly tender-crisp. Add the shelled edamame and cook for another 2-3 minutes. 6. Return the seitan to the wok. Give the stir-fry sauce a quick whisk again and pour it over the ingredients in the wok. Stir constantly for 1-2 minutes, allowing the sauce to thicken and coat all ingredients. 7. Serve the seitan and edamame stir-fry immediately over the cooked brown rice.

High-Protein Everything Bagel Roasted Edamame & Cashews

🥜 21g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 15 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 225 g Shelled edamame (frozen)
  • 75 g Raw cashews
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Everything bagel seasoning
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). If using frozen shelled edamame, thaw them by rinsing under hot water for a minute or two and drain well, patting dry with a paper towel. 2. In a medium bowl, combine the thawed edamame and raw cashews. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon of olive oil and toss to coat evenly. 3. Sprinkle 2 tablespoons of everything bagel seasoning over the edamame and cashews, ensuring all pieces are well coated. 4. Spread the seasoned edamame and cashews in a single layer on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Roast for 12-15 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the cashews are golden brown and the edamame are slightly crispy. Be careful not to burn the cashews. 5. Remove from the oven and let cool slightly before serving. Store any leftovers in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.

🥜 28g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.7 cup Dry Farro
  • 2 cup Water
  • 1 whole Canned Cannellini Beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1.5 cup Cherry Tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.2 tsp Dried Thyme
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 2 cup Fresh Arugula
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. First, cook the farro. Rinse the dry farro thoroughly under cold water. Combine the rinsed farro and 2 cups of water in a medium saucepan.   • 1 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.3 cup Water
  • 1.5 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Start by cooking the lentils and wild rice. Rinse both thoroughly. In separate saucepans, cook the lentils with 1.5 cups water or broth until tender (about 20-25 minutes). High-Protein Baked Tofu & Green Lentil Pilaf with Roasted Carrots

🥜 53g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-Firm Tofu, pressed and cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 3 tbsp Tamari or Soy Sauce
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Dried Rosemary
  • 0.5 tsp Dried Thyme
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 3 whole Carrots, peeled and sliced into 1/2-inch rounds
  • 1 cup Dry Green Lentils, rinsed
  • 1 cup Dry Quinoa, rinsed
  • 4 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped (for garnish)
Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). In a medium bowl, whisk together 2 tablespoons of tamari, 1 tablespoon of olive oil, nutritional yeast, 0.5 tsp garlic powder, 0.25 tsp dried rosemary, and 0.25 tsp dried thyme. Add the pressed tofu cubes and toss gently to coat. Let marinate for at least 10 minutes. 2. Spread the marinated tofu cubes in a single layer on a baking sheet. In the same bowl, toss the sliced carrots with the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 0.5 tsp garlic powder, 0.25 tsp dried rosemary, and 0.25 tsp dried thyme, and spread them on a separate part of the baking sheet or on another baking sheet. 3. Bake the tofu and carrots for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway, until the tofu is golden brown and firm, and the carrots are tender and slightly caramelized. 4. While the tofu and carrots bake, prepare the lentil pilaf. In a large pot or Dutch oven, combine the rinsed green lentils, rinsed quinoa, 4 cups of vegetable broth, 1 tablespoon of tamari, 0.5 tsp salt, and 0.25 tsp black pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the lentils and quinoa are tender and the liquid has been absorbed. 5. Once everything is cooked, gently fluff the lentil pilaf with a fork. Divide the pilaf between two serving plates, top with the baked tofu and roasted carrots. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley before serving.

High-Protein Seitan & White Bean Chili with Cornbread Crumbles

🥜 57.5g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Seitan, diced
  • 800 g Canned diced tomatoes
  • 400 g Cooked cannellini beans
  • 240 ml Vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp Chili powder
  • 1 tbsp Cumin
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 0.5 whole Jalapeño, minced (optional)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 60 g Cornmeal
  • 60 g All-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp Baking powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt (for cornbread)
  • 1 tbsp Sugar (optional, for cornbread)
  • 120 ml Unsweetened plant milk
  • 2 tbsp Melted vegan butter or neutral oil
Instructions
For the Chili: Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced seitan and sauté for 5-7 minutes until lightly browned. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, dried oregano, and minced jalapeño (if using). Cook for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the diced tomatoes, cooked cannellini beans, and vegetable broth. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and cook for at least 20-25 minutes, stirring occasionally, to allow the flavors to meld. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. For the Cornbread Crumbles: Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F). In a medium bowl, whisk together the cornmeal, all-purpose flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar (if using).
