High-Protein Scrambled Tofu & Spinach with Roasted Sweet Potato & Black Beans

🥜 29g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Sweet potato
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin powder
  • 500 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 3 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Kala Namak (black salt)
  • 150 g Baby spinach
  • 400 g Canned black beans
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro (optional, for garnish)
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Peel the sweet potato and cut it into 1-inch cubes. Toss the sweet potato cubes with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 1/2 teaspoon of smoked paprika, and 1/4 teaspoon of cumin powder on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and slightly caramelized. 2. While the sweet potato roasts, press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15 minutes to remove excess water. Crumble the pressed tofu into a large mixing bowl. Add the nutritional yeast, turmeric powder, black pepper, garlic powder, remaining 1/2 teaspoon of smoked paprika, remaining 1/4 teaspoon of cumin powder, and Kala Namak to the crumbled tofu. Mix well to combine, ensuring the tofu is evenly coated with the spices. 3. Heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the seasoned tofu crumble and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it starts to brown slightly. 4. Rinse and drain the canned black beans thoroughly. Add the black beans and baby spinach to the skillet with the tofu. Cook for another 3-5 minutes, stirring until the spinach has wilted completely and the beans are heated through. 5. To serve, divide the scrambled tofu and black bean mixture between two plates. Top each portion with the roasted sweet potato cubes. Garnish with fresh cilantro if desired.

High-Protein Lemon-Herb Chickpea & Quinoa Salad with Toasted Almonds and Cucumber

🥜 27g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Dry quinoa
  • 2 cup Water or vegetable broth
  • 800 g Canned chickpeas
  • 1 whole Cucumber
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes
  • 0.2 cup Fresh parsley
  • 0.5 cup Slivered almonds
  • 3 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Rinse the dry quinoa thoroughly under cold water. Combine the quinoa and water or vegetable broth in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside to cool. 2. While the quinoa cooks, preheat a dry skillet over medium heat. Add the slivered almonds and toast for 3-5 minutes, stirring frequently, until golden brown and fragrant. Be careful not to burn them. Remove from heat and let cool. 3. Rinse and drain the chickpeas well. Dice the cucumber and halve the cherry tomatoes. Chop the fresh parsley. 4. In a large bowl, combine the cooled quinoa, drained chickpeas, diced cucumber, halved cherry tomatoes, chopped parsley, and toasted slivered almonds. 5. In a small separate bowl, whisk together the lemon juice, olive oil, dried oregano, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper to create the dressing. Pour the dressing over the salad ingredients and toss gently until everything is well combined and coated. Divide the salad into two portions for serving.

High-Protein Lentil & Walnut 'Meat' Sauce with Protein Pasta and Roasted Broccoli

🥜 33g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 160 g Protein pasta (e.g., chickpea or red lentil pasta)
  • 300 g Broccoli florets
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 cup Dried green or brown lentils
  • 720 ml Vegetable broth
  • 400 g Crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 tsp Dried basil
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 cup Walnuts
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss the broccoli florets with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes, or until tender-crisp and slightly charred. 2. While the broccoli roasts, rinse the dried lentils thoroughly. In a large pot or deep skillet, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium heat. Add the rinsed lentils, vegetable broth, crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, dried oregano, dried basil, garlic powder, and smoked paprika. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for 25-30 minutes, or until the lentils are tender and most of the liquid has been absorbed, stirring occasionally. 3. While the lentils are simmering, cook the protein pasta according to package instructions. Drain well. 4. Finely chop the walnuts or pulse them in a food processor until they resemble coarse crumbs. Once the lentils are tender, stir in the chopped walnuts and nutritional yeast. Season the lentil 'meat' sauce with salt and black pepper to taste. Continue to simmer for another 5 minutes to allow the flavors to meld. 5. Divide the cooked protein pasta between two plates. Top generously with the lentil and walnut 'meat' sauce. Serve with the roasted broccoli on the side.

High-Protein Spiced Edamame and Pumpkin Seed Roast

🥜 28g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 1.0 servings Baska: 0.8 servings
Ingredients
  • 1.5 cup Shelled edamame (frozen)
  • 0.2 cup Pumpkin seeds
  • 1 tsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.2 tsp Chili flakes (optional)
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). If using frozen edamame, thaw them by rinsing under warm water and patting dry with a paper towel. 2. In a medium bowl, combine the shelled edamame and pumpkin seeds. Add the olive oil, smoked paprika, cumin powder, garlic powder, chili flakes (if using), and salt. Toss thoroughly to ensure all ingredients are evenly coated with the spices. 3. Spread the seasoned edamame and pumpkin seeds in a single layer on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. 4. Roast for 15-20 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the edamame are slightly crispy and the pumpkin seeds are toasted. Keep a close eye on them to prevent burning. 5. Remove from the oven and let cool slightly before serving as a crunchy, high-protein snack.

High-Protein Baked Tofu & Farro Breakfast Bowl with Sautéed Greens

🥜 44g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
  • 2 cup Cooked farro (or quinoa)
  • 200 g Fresh spinach
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). In a medium bowl, toss the cubed pressed tofu with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 1 tablespoon of tamari, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and black pepper until evenly coated. While the tofu bakes, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the spinach and sauté until wilted, about 3-5 minutes. Stir in the remaining 1 tablespoon of tamari and season with a pinch of salt if desired. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked farro between two serving bowls. Top each with half of the baked tofu and half of the sautéed spinach. Sprinkle each bowl with 2 tablespoons of nutritional yeast before serving.

High-Protein Peanut Tempeh & Broccoli Quinoa Bowl

🥜 46g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Tempeh, cubed
  • 2 cup Cooked quinoa
  • 300 g Broccoli florets
  • 3 tbsp Peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 0.2 tsp Red pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 4 tbsp Water
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
Instructions
In a small bowl, whisk together the peanut butter, tamari, rice vinegar, maple syrup, grated ginger, red pepper flakes, sesame oil, and water to create the peanut sauce. Set aside. Steam or blanch the broccoli florets until tender-crisp, about 3-5 minutes. You can also roast them if preferred. Heat a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the cubed tempeh and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned on all sides. Pour the peanut sauce over the tempeh in the skillet and toss to coat evenly. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, allowing the sauce to thicken slightly. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls. Top each with half of the sauced tempeh and half of the steamed broccoli. Serve warm.

High-Protein Black Bean & Corn Stuffed Poblano Peppers with TVP and Cashew Cream

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 whole Poblano peppers
  • 1 whole Canned black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 0.8 cup Frozen corn kernels
  • 40 g Textured Vegetable Protein (TVP), dry
  • 0.5 cup Vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1.5 tsp Chili powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.5 cup Raw cashews, soaked
  • 0.2 cup Water (for cashew cream)
  • 1 tbsp Lime juice
Instructions
Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Cut the poblano peppers in half lengthwise and remove the seeds and membranes. Place them cut-side up on a baking sheet. In a medium bowl, combine the TVP with vegetable broth and let it sit for 5 minutes to rehydrate. In a large skillet over medium heat, add the rehydrated TVP, drained black beans, corn, tomato paste, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper. Cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until heated through and well combined. Spoon the filling evenly into the poblano pepper halves. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the peppers are tender. While the peppers bake, drain the soaked cashews. Blend them with 1/4 cup of fresh water and lime juice until completely smooth and creamy. If the cream is too thick, add water a teaspoon at a time until desired consistency. Serve the stuffed poblano peppers hot, drizzled generously with the cashew cream.

High-Protein Spiced Tofu & Lentil Hash with Kale and Sweet Potatoes

🥜 27g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 1.5 cup Cooked green lentils
  • 1 whole Sweet potato
  • 4 cup Kale
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Peel the sweet potato and cut it into small 1cm dice. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, 0.5 tsp smoked paprika, 0.25 tsp cumin, and 0.125 tsp salt. Spread in a single layer on a baking sheet and roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and slightly caramelized, flipping halfway through. 2. While sweet potatoes roast, press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15 minutes to remove excess water. Crumble the pressed tofu into a bowl. Add garlic powder, remaining 0.5 tsp smoked paprika, remaining 0.25 tsp cumin, turmeric powder, black pepper, and 0.25 tsp salt. Mix well to coat the tofu evenly. 3. Heat the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seasoned crumbled tofu and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it's golden brown and slightly crispy. 4. Add the cooked green lentils to the skillet with the tofu, stirring to combine and heat through for 2-3 minutes. 5. Stir in the chopped kale, allowing it to wilt down for 3-5 minutes. Once wilted, add the roasted sweet potatoes and nutritional yeast to the skillet, gently folding everything together. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed. 6. Divide the hash evenly into two bowls and serve warm.

High-Protein Creamy White Bean Pasta with Toasted Walnuts and Spinach

🥜 36g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 160 g Protein pasta
  • 1 whole Cannellini beans
  • 60 g Walnuts
  • 4 cup Fresh spinach
  • 0.8 cup Vegetable broth
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Dried thyme
  • 0.2 tsp Red pepper flakes
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.5 whole Lemon
Instructions
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the protein pasta and cook according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 0.5 cup of the pasta cooking water before draining the pasta. 2. While the pasta cooks, heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the chopped walnuts and toast for 3-5 minutes, stirring frequently, until fragrant and lightly browned. Remove the walnuts from the skillet and set aside. 3. In the same skillet, add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil. Add the drained cannellini beans, garlic powder, dried thyme, red pepper flakes (if using), 0.25 tsp salt, and 0.125 tsp black pepper. Sauté for 3-5 minutes, gently mashing about half of the beans with the back of a spoon to create a creamy base. 4. Stir in the vegetable broth and nutritional yeast. Bring to a gentle simmer, stirring until the sauce thickens slightly, about 3-4 minutes. If the sauce is too thick, add a splash of the reserved pasta water. 5. Add the fresh spinach to the skillet, stirring until it wilts completely into the sauce, about 2-3 minutes. 6. Add the cooked and drained pasta to the skillet with the creamy white bean sauce. Toss gently to coat the pasta evenly. Squeeze in the juice from half a lemon. 7. Divide the pasta into two bowls. Garnish generously with the toasted walnuts and serve immediately.

High-Protein Seitan & White Bean Chili with Toasted Corn and Avocado

🥜 58.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 250 g Seitan (diced)
  • 400 g Canned White Beans (rinsed and drained)
  • 400 g Canned Diced Tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp Tomato Paste
  • 200 ml Vegetable Broth
  • 150 g Corn (frozen or canned, drained)
  • 2 tsp Chili Powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 1 whole Avocado
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Cilantro (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the diced seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned. Return the seitan to the pot. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, and smoked paprika, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the tomato paste and cook for another minute, stirring constantly. 4. Pour in the diced tomatoes (undrained), vegetable broth, and rinsed white beans. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and let it cook for 15-20 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking. 5. Season the chili with salt and black pepper to taste. To serve, ladle the chili into two bowls. Top generously with the toasted corn, diced avocado, and fresh chopped cilantro.

  • 2 tbsp Fresh Mint (chopped)
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Coriander powder
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 4 cup Mixed Greens
  • 1 cup Cherry Tomatoes (halved)
  • 1 cup Cucumber (diced)
  • 3 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 4 tbsp Water (for dressing)
  • 1 tbsp Fresh Dill (chopped)

Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 400 g Broccoli florets
  • 120 g Brown rice
  • 350 ml Water
  • 4 tbsp Soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)
  • 4 tbsp Peanut butter (creamy, unsweetened)
  • 2 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 0.5 tsp Sriracha (optional)
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds (for garnish)
Instructions
