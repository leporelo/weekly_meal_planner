High-Protein Savory Tofu & Lentil Scramble with Roasted Bell Peppers and Arugula

🥜 67g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 600 g Extra-Firm Tofu (pressed)
  • 1 cup Cooked Red Lentils
  • 1 whole Red Bell Pepper
  • 2 cup Arugula
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 1.5 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black Salt (Kala Namak)
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Core and slice the red bell pepper into thin strips. Toss with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes until tender and slightly charred. 2. While peppers roast, crumble the pressed tofu into a large non-stick pan over medium heat. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil, garlic powder, turmeric, black salt, and black pepper. Sauté for 5-7 minutes, breaking up any large pieces, until the tofu is heated through and slightly browned. 3. Stir in the cooked red lentils, nutritional yeast, and tamari into the tofu scramble. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld. 4. To serve, divide the scramble mixture between two bowls. Top generously with the roasted bell pepper strips and fresh arugula. This meal is great for meal prep as the scramble and roasted peppers can be made ahead and reheated.

High-Protein Black-Eyed Pea & Seitan Farro Bowl with Roasted Broccoli and a Lemon-Herb Dressing

🥜 67.5g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 cup Cooked Farro
  • 300 g Seitan
  • 1.5 cup Cooked Black-eyed Peas
  • 4 cup Broccoli Florets
  • 3 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 whole Lemon (juice)
  • 0.2 cup Fresh Parsley (chopped)
  • 1 tsp Dried Dill
  • 2 whole Garlic (minced)
  • 3 tbsp Tahini
  • 4 tbsp Water
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss the broccoli florets with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and slightly crispy. 2. While the broccoli roasts, slice the seitan into bite-sized pieces or strips. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and sauté the seitan for 5-7 minutes until lightly browned. Set aside. 3. Prepare the lemon-herb dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together the tahini, lemon juice, minced garlic, fresh parsley, dried dill, 4 tablespoons of water, and a pinch of salt and pepper until smooth and creamy. Add more water if needed to reach desired consistency. 4. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked farro, seitan, black-eyed peas, and roasted broccoli between two large serving bowls. Drizzle generously with the lemon-herb dressing and serve immediately. This meal works well for meal prep; store components separately and combine when ready to eat.

High-Protein Spiced Tempeh & Chickpea Curry with Wild Rice and Spinach

🥜 58.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Tempeh
  • 2 cup Cooked Wild Rice
  • 1.5 whole Canned Chickpeas (drained and rinsed)
  • 400 ml Full-Fat Coconut Milk
  • 400 g Crushed Tomatoes
  • 4 cup Fresh Spinach
  • 3 whole Garlic (minced)
  • 1 tbsp Fresh Ginger (grated)
  • 2 tbsp Curry Powder
  • 1 tsp Ground Cumin
  • 1 tsp Ground Coriander
  • 1 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Chili Powder (optional)
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 0.2 cup Fresh Cilantro (chopped, for garnish)
Instructions
1. Dice the tempeh into 1-inch cubes. Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the tempeh and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned on all sides. Remove tempeh from the pot and set aside. 2. Add minced garlic and grated ginger to the pot. Sauté for 1 minute until fragrant. Stir in the curry powder, cumin, coriander, turmeric, and chili powder (if using). Cook for another minute, stirring constantly, until aromatic. 3. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and coconut milk, stirring to combine and scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer. Add the drained chickpeas and return the tempeh to the pot. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-20 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld. 4. Stir in the fresh spinach and cook until wilted, about 2-3 minutes. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve the curry hot over cooked wild rice, garnished with fresh cilantro. Leftovers store well and can be reheated for easy future meals.

High-Protein Spiced Garbanzo Beans & Sunflower Seeds

🥜 33g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 whole Canned Garbanzo Beans (Chickpeas, drained and rinsed)
  • 1 cup Raw Sunflower Seeds
  • 1.5 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1.5 tsp Ground Cumin
  • 1.5 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Cayenne Pepper (optional)
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Thoroughly drain and rinse the canned garbanzo beans, then pat them very dry with a paper towel. The drier they are, the crispier they will get. 2. In a medium bowl, combine the dried garbanzo beans, raw sunflower seeds, olive oil, ground cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and cayenne pepper (if using). Toss well to ensure all ingredients are evenly coated. 3. Spread the mixture in a single layer on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Roast for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the garbanzo beans are crispy and the sunflower seeds are lightly toasted. 4. Allow to cool completely before serving or storing in an airtight container for a crunchy, high-protein snack throughout the week.

High-Protein Savory Tempeh & Spinach Steel-Cut Oats

🥜 62g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Steel-cut oats
  • 3 cup Water
  • 1 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 200 g Tempeh, crumbled
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 100 g Fresh spinach
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (gluten-free)
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper, to taste
Instructions
First, cook the steel-cut oats: In a medium saucepan, combine steel-cut oats and water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until water is absorbed and oats are tender. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking. While oats are cooking, prepare the tempeh. Heat extra-virgin olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tempeh, garlic powder, and smoked paprika. Sauté for 5-7 minutes, breaking up the tempeh further with a spoon, until it's lightly browned and crispy. Stir in the nutritional yeast, tamari, and black pepper. Add the fresh spinach to the skillet and cook until wilted, about 2-3 minutes. Once the oats are cooked, divide them into two bowls. Top each bowl generously with the savory tempeh and spinach mixture. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Smoky Paprika Seitan & Chickpea Grain Bowl with Roasted Bell Peppers and Quinoa

🥜 44g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Quinoa, dry
  • 2 cup Water or vegetable broth
  • 2 whole Bell peppers (red and yellow), sliced
  • 200 g Seitan, cut into cubes
  • 400 g Canned chickpeas, rinsed and drained
  • 2 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt, to taste
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish
  • 2 whole Lemon wedges, for serving
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). First, cook the quinoa: Rinse the quinoa thoroughly under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine the rinsed quinoa with water or vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all the liquid is absorbed. Let it sit covered for 5 minutes off the heat, then fluff with a fork. In a large bowl, combine the sliced bell peppers, seitan cubes, and rinsed chickpeas. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, smoked paprika, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Toss well to ensure everything is evenly coated. Spread the seasoned vegetables, seitan, and chickpeas in a single layer on a large baking sheet. Roast in the preheated oven for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the bell peppers are tender-crisp and the seitan and chickpeas are lightly browned. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two serving bowls. Top each with a generous portion of the roasted seitan, chickpeas, and bell peppers. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley and serve with a lemon wedge for a squeeze of fresh juice.

High-Protein Spiced Tofu & Black Bean Bake with Roasted Sweet Potato Wedges

🥜 50g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed and crumbled
  • 400 g Canned black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 2 whole Sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into wedges
  • 1 whole Bell pepper (any color), diced
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 120 ml Vegetable broth
  • 1.5 tsp Chili powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt, to taste
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). First, prepare the sweet potato wedges: In a bowl, toss the sweet potato wedges with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper. Spread them in a single layer on a baking sheet and roast for 25-30 minutes, or until tender and slightly caramelized, flipping halfway through. While the sweet potatoes roast, prepare the tofu and black bean mixture. In a large mixing bowl, combine the crumbled pressed tofu, rinsed black beans, diced bell pepper, tomato paste, vegetable broth, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and black pepper. Mix everything thoroughly until well combined. Transfer the tofu and black bean mixture to a 9x13 inch (approx. 23x33 cm) baking dish and spread it evenly. Bake in the preheated oven alongside the sweet potatoes for 20-25 minutes, or until heated through and slightly browned on top. Once baked, divide the tofu and black bean mixture and the roasted sweet potato wedges between two plates. Garnish generously with fresh chopped cilantro before serving.

High-Protein Garlicky Roasted Almonds & Pepitas

🥜 25g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 12 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 100 g Raw almonds
  • 100 g Raw pepitas (pumpkin seeds)
  • 1 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.2 tsp Salt, to taste
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 160°C (325°F). In a medium bowl, combine the raw almonds and raw pepitas. Drizzle with extra-virgin olive oil, then sprinkle with garlic powder, smoked paprika, and salt. Toss thoroughly to ensure all nuts and seeds are evenly coated with the seasonings. Spread the seasoned almonds and pepitas in a single layer on a baking sheet. Roast in the preheated oven for 10-12 minutes, stirring once halfway through, until lightly golden brown and fragrant. Be careful not to burn them. Remove from the oven and let them cool completely on the baking sheet. As they cool, they will become crispier. Store any leftovers in an airtight container at room temperature.

High-Protein Savory Buckwheat & Smoked Tofu Bowl with Roasted Asparagus

🥜 35g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 250 g Smoked Tofu, firm
  • 100 g Buckwheat Groats, raw
  • 300 g Asparagus spears
  • 3 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (gluten-free soy sauce)
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
  • 1.5 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 500 ml Vegetable Broth
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped
Instructions
Rinse the buckwheat groats thoroughly under cold water using a fine-mesh sieve. In a medium saucepan, combine the rinsed buckwheat groats with the vegetable broth and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until the liquid is absorbed and the buckwheat is tender. While the buckwheat cooks, preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Trim the woody ends off the asparagus spears and toss them with 0.5 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 10-12 minutes until tender-crisp. Slice the smoked tofu into small cubes. In a non-stick pan, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium heat. Add the tofu cubes and cook, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and slightly crispy on all sides, about 8-10 minutes. Remove from heat and toss with tamari, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, and black pepper. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked buckwheat between two bowls. Top with the crispy smoked tofu and roasted asparagus. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley.

High-Protein Lemon-Herb Marinated Tempeh & Wild Rice Power Bowl

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 250 g Tempeh
  • 120 g Wild Rice, dry
  • 2 whole Zucchini, medium
  • 200 g Cherry Tomatoes
  • 1 whole Lemon, juiced
  • 4 tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Dill, chopped
  • 3 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Dried Oregano
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 600 ml Vegetable Broth
Instructions
First, cook the wild rice: rinse 120g of wild rice and place it in a saucepan with 600ml of vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 40-45 minutes, or until the rice is tender and has absorbed the liquid. While the rice cooks, preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Slice the zucchini into half-moons and halve the cherry tomatoes. Toss them with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet and roast for 15-20 minutes, until tender and slightly caramelized. For the tempeh, steam the block whole for 10 minutes to reduce bitterness, then pat dry and slice into 1 cm thick pieces. In a shallow dish, whisk together 2 tablespoons of olive oil, the juice of one lemon, chopped parsley, chopped dill, garlic powder, and dried oregano. Add the tempeh slices and marinate for at least 15 minutes. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Add the marinated tempeh slices and cook for 3-4 minutes per side, until golden brown and slightly crispy. To assemble the power bowls, divide the cooked wild rice between two bowls. Arrange the roasted zucchini and cherry tomatoes and the pan-seared tempeh over the rice. Drizzle with any remaining marinade from the tempeh if desired.

High-Protein Creamy White Bean & Kale Gratin with Seitan Crumbles

🥜 55g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 800 g Cannellini Beans, canned, drained and rinsed
  • 200 g Seitan, finely crumbled
  • 200 g Kale, destemmed and chopped
  • 300 ml Unsweetened Plant-Based Milk
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 1.5 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp Dijon Mustard
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 100 ml Vegetable Broth
  • 0.5 cup Panko Breadcrumbs
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Lightly grease a medium-sized gratin dish or oven-safe baking dish. In a large skillet or pot, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium heat. Add the finely crumbled seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned and crispy. Remove the seitan from the skillet and set aside. In the same skillet, add the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil and the chopped kale. Sauté for 3-5 minutes until the kale is wilted, then pour in the vegetable broth and simmer for another 2 minutes. Stir in the drained cannellini beans, plant-based milk, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, Dijon mustard, lemon juice, salt, and black pepper. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened. Remove from heat and stir in about half of the cooked seitan crumbles. Pour the bean and kale mixture into the prepared gratin dish. Sprinkle the remaining seitan crumbles and the Panko breadcrumbs evenly over the top. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the topping is golden brown and the gratin is bubbling. Let it rest for a few minutes before serving.

High-Protein Spicy Wasabi Roasted Edamame & Cashews

🥜 22g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Shelled Edamame, frozen
  • 100 g Raw Cashews
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (gluten-free soy sauce)
  • 1 tsp Wasabi Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp Sesame Oil
  • 0.2 tsp Cayenne Pepper (optional)
Instructions

High-Protein Spicy Tofu & Kidney Bean Scramble with Bell Peppers and Turmeric Quinoa

🥜 36g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 240 g Canned kidney beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 whole Green bell pepper, diced
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Chili powder
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp Soy sauce or tamari
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.8 cup Quinoa, uncooked
  • 1.5 cup Water
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
Instructions
First, prepare the quinoa. Rinse 0.75 cup of quinoa under cold water. Combine the rinsed quinoa with 1.5 cups of water in a small saucepan, add a pinch of salt, and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Let it sit, covered, for 5 minutes off the heat, then fluff with a fork. While the quinoa cooks, prepare the tofu scramble. Crumble the pressed tofu into a large bowl. Add the turmeric powder, cumin powder, chili powder, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, soy sauce or tamari, salt, and black pepper. Mix well to ensure the tofu is evenly coated. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced red and green bell peppers and sauté for 5-7 minutes, until softened. Add the seasoned tofu crumble to the skillet and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tofu starts to brown and any excess moisture has evaporated. Stir in the rinsed and drained kidney beans and cook for another 3-5 minutes to heat through. Serve the spicy tofu and kidney bean scramble over the fluffy turmeric quinoa. Garnish with fresh cilantro, if desired. This meal is perfect for sharing and reheats well.

High-Protein Savory Lentil & Mushroom Stew with Buckwheat Groats

🥜 34g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Brown or green lentils, dry
  • 4 cup Vegetable broth
  • 200 g Cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 whole Carrots, diced
  • 2 whole Celery stalks, diced
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 0.5 tsp Dried rosemary
  • 1 whole Bay leaf
  • 2 cup Kale, chopped
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.8 cup Buckwheat groats, uncooked
  • 1.5 cup Water (for groats)
Instructions
Rinse the dry lentils thoroughly under cold water. Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the sliced mushrooms and sauté for 5-7 minutes until they release their moisture and start to brown. Add the diced carrots, diced celery, and minced garlic to the pot, cooking for another 5 minutes until vegetables soften slightly. Stir in the tomato paste, dried thyme, and dried rosemary, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the rinsed lentils, vegetable broth, and bay leaf. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 30-35 minutes, or until lentils are tender. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. Stir in the chopped kale during the last 5 minutes of cooking until wilted. While the stew simmers, prepare the buckwheat groats. Combine 0.75 cup of buckwheat groats with 1.5 cups of water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork. Remove the bay leaf from the stew before serving. Ladle the hearty lentil and mushroom stew over a portion of the cooked buckwheat groats. This stew is robust and makes excellent leftovers.

High-Protein Chickpea & Spinach Patties with a Side of Wild Rice and Roasted Bell Peppers

🥜 27g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 240 g Canned chickpeas, rinsed and drained
  • 0.5 cup Chickpea flour
  • 1 cup Fresh spinach, chopped
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil (for cooking patties and roasting)
  • 0.8 cup Wild rice, uncooked
  • 2.2 cup Water (for rice)
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper, sliced into strips
  • 1 whole Yellow bell pepper, sliced into strips
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). In a large bowl, mash the rinsed and drained chickpeas with a fork or potato masher until mostly smooth but with some texture remaining. Add the chickpea flour, chopped spinach, garlic powder, smoked paprika, cumin powder, salt, and black pepper to the bowl. Mix thoroughly until a cohesive dough forms. Divide the mixture into 4 equal portions and form them into patties, about 1-inch thick. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat. Cook the patties for 4-5 minutes per side, until golden brown and crispy. Alternatively, if baking, place patties on a parchment-lined baking sheet and bake for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden. For the wild rice, combine 0.75 cup of uncooked wild rice with 2.25 cups of water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 40-45 minutes, or until the rice is tender and has absorbed all the water. Fluff with a fork. On a separate baking sheet, toss the sliced red and yellow bell peppers with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper. Roast in the preheated oven for 15-20 minutes, until softened and slightly charred at the edges. Serve two chickpea and spinach patties per person alongside a generous portion of wild rice and the roasted bell peppers.

High-Protein Spicy Black Bean Dip with Seitan Crumbles and Veggie Sticks

🥜 20g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 8 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 240 g Canned black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 100 g Seitan, finely crumbled
  • 2 tbsp Lime juice
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 0.5 whole Jalapeño, deseeded and minced (optional, for extra heat)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
  • 0.1 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 cup Carrot sticks
  • 1 cup Celery sticks
