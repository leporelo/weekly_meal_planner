High-Protein Spiced Tempeh & Black Bean Scramble with Bell Peppers and Toasted Buckwheat

🥜 39g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 227 g Tempeh
  • 240 g Black beans, canned (drained and rinsed)
  • 1 cup Buckwheat groats (dry)
  • 2 cup Vegetable broth (low sodium)
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 1 whole Green bell pepper
  • 4 cup Spinach
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Begin by cooking the buckwheat groats: rinse 1 cup of dry buckwheat groats thoroughly under cold water. Combine the rinsed buckwheat with 2 cups of vegetable broth in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until the liquid is absorbed and buckwheat is tender. While the buckwheat cooks, crumble the tempeh into small pieces using your hands or a fork. Dice the red and green bell peppers into small, uniform pieces. Heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tempeh and diced bell peppers and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tempeh is lightly browned and the peppers are tender-crisp. Stir in the cumin powder, smoked paprika, turmeric powder, salt, and black pepper, cooking for another minute until fragrant. Add the drained and rinsed black beans and nutritional yeast to the skillet, stirring to combine. Finally, add the fresh spinach, cooking until it wilts, about 2-3 minutes. To serve, divide the cooked buckwheat groats between two bowls, then top generously with the spiced tempeh and black bean scramble. Mix well before eating.

High-Protein Lemon-Herb Roasted Seitan & White Bean Farro Bowl with Roasted Asparagus

🥜 56g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 250 g Seitan (pre-made, plain)
  • 1 cup Farro (dry)
  • 3 cup Vegetable broth (low sodium)
  • 240 g White beans, canned (e.g., cannellini, drained and rinsed)
  • 200 g Asparagus spears
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 3 tbsp Lemon juice (freshly squeezed)
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley (chopped)
  • 2 cup Baby spinach or arugula
Instructions
First, prepare the farro: rinse 1 cup of dry farro under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine the rinsed farro with 3 cups of vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 25-30 minutes, or until farro is tender and liquid is absorbed. While farro cooks, preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Cut the seitan into bite-sized cubes. Snap the woody ends off the asparagus spears and chop them into 2-inch pieces. In a large bowl, toss the seitan cubes and asparagus with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 1 tablespoon of lemon juice, dried oregano, dried thyme, 0.5 tsp salt, and 0.25 tsp black pepper. Spread the seasoned seitan and asparagus in a single layer on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes, flipping halfway, until the seitan is slightly browned and the asparagus is tender-crisp. Once the farro is cooked, drain any excess liquid if necessary. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 2 tablespoons of lemon juice, 0.25 tsp salt, and 0.25 tsp black pepper to create a dressing. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked farro between two large bowls. Top with a bed of baby spinach or arugula, then add the roasted seitan and asparagus. Evenly distribute the drained and rinsed white beans. Drizzle the lemon-herb dressing over each bowl and garnish with fresh chopped parsley.

High-Protein Tamari-Ginger Tofu & Edamame Stir-fry with Brown Rice

🥜 54g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 2 cup Frozen shelled edamame
  • 1 cup Brown rice (dry)
  • 4 whole Bok choy
  • 2 whole Carrot
  • 1 whole Bell pepper (any color)
  • 4 tbsp Tamari (gluten-free soy sauce)
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger (grated)
  • 2 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp Water
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds (for garnish)
Instructions
Start by pressing the extra-firm tofu: wrap the block in a clean kitchen towel and place something heavy on top for at least 30 minutes to remove excess water. Meanwhile, cook the brown rice according to package directions; typically, combine 1 cup dry brown rice with 2 cups water, bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 35-45 minutes until tender. While rice and tofu are prepping, prepare the vegetables: thinly slice the bok choy, julienne the carrots, and slice the bell pepper into thin strips. For the stir-fry sauce, whisk together tamari, grated ginger, rice vinegar, and sesame oil in a small bowl. In a separate tiny bowl, combine cornstarch with 2 tablespoons of water to create a slurry. Once tofu is pressed, dice it into 1-inch cubes. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add the tofu cubes and cook for 8-10 minutes, flipping occasionally, until golden brown and crispy on all sides. Remove tofu from the skillet and set aside. Add the sliced carrots and bell pepper to the same skillet and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until slightly softened. Add the sliced bok choy and frozen shelled edamame, cooking for another 3 minutes until bok choy is wilted and edamame is heated through. Return the cooked tofu to the skillet. Give the stir-fry sauce a quick stir and pour it over the vegetables and tofu. Bring to a gentle simmer, then add the cornstarch slurry, stirring constantly until the sauce thickens, about 1-2 minutes. To serve, divide the cooked brown rice between two bowls and top generously with the Tamari-Ginger Tofu & Edamame Stir-fry. Garnish with sesame seeds.

High-Protein Herb-Roasted Chickpeas & Pumpkin Seeds

🥜 25g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 480 g Chickpeas, canned (drained, rinsed, and pat dry)
  • 0.5 cup Pumpkin seeds (pepitas)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dried rosemary
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Ensure the canned chickpeas are thoroughly drained, rinsed, and patted very dry with a kitchen towel; this step is crucial for crispiness. In a medium bowl, combine the dried chickpeas and pumpkin seeds. Drizzle with olive oil and toss to coat evenly. Add the dried rosemary, dried thyme, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper, tossing again until all ingredients are well distributed. Spread the seasoned chickpeas and pumpkin seeds in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the chickpeas are golden brown and crunchy, and the pumpkin seeds are lightly toasted. Keep a close eye on them during the last few minutes to prevent burning. Remove from oven and let cool slightly before serving. Store any leftovers in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3-4 days.

High-Protein Tofu & Spinach Scramble with Toasted Hemp Seeds and Bell Pepper

🥜 43g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 15 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 2 cup Spinach
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.2 tsp Black salt (Kala Namak)
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Hemp seeds
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15 minutes to remove excess water. While tofu is pressing, dice the red bell pepper. Once pressed, crumble the tofu into a large bowl using your hands or a fork. Stir in the nutritional yeast, turmeric, black salt, garlic powder, and black pepper, ensuring the tofu is evenly coated. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the diced bell pepper and sauté for 3-5 minutes until slightly softened. Add the seasoned crumbled tofu to the skillet and cook for 7-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it starts to brown slightly and some moisture has evaporated. Stir in the fresh spinach and cook for another 2-3 minutes, until the spinach has wilted. Divide the scramble evenly into two bowls. Garnish each serving with 1 tablespoon of hemp seeds before serving.

High-Protein Seitan & Kidney Bean Power Bowl with Roasted Sweet Potatoes and Tahini-Lime Dressing

🥜 49g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Seitan
  • 400 g Canned kidney beans
  • 1 whole Sweet potato
  • 1 cup Quinoa (dry)
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lime juice
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Peel and cube the sweet potato into 1-inch pieces. Toss the sweet potato with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 1/2 teaspoon of smoked paprika, 1/2 teaspoon of cumin, and a pinch of salt and pepper. Spread in a single layer on a baking sheet and roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and slightly caramelized, flipping halfway. While the sweet potatoes roast, cook the quinoa according to package directions (typically 1 cup dry quinoa with 2 cups water, simmer for 15 minutes, then let stand for 5 minutes). Slice the seitan into bite-sized pieces or strips. In a non-stick skillet, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes until browned and slightly crispy. Stir in the remaining 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika, 1/2 teaspoon cumin, and garlic powder, cooking for another minute. Drain and rinse the canned kidney beans thoroughly. For the dressing, whisk together tahini, lime juice, a splash of water (1-2 tbsp) to reach desired consistency, and a pinch of salt. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls. Top with roasted sweet potatoes, sautéed seitan, and kidney beans. Drizzle generously with the tahini-lime dressing and sprinkle with fresh cilantro before serving.

High-Protein Black Bean & Tempeh Tacos with Corn Salsa and Cashew Crema

🥜 44g protein/serving 🔪 25 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 227 g Tempeh
  • 400 g Canned black beans
  • 8 whole Corn tortillas
  • 0.5 cup Raw cashews
  • 3 tbsp Lime juice
  • 0.2 cup Water
  • 1 tsp Chipotle powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 cup Fresh or frozen corn kernels
  • 0.5 whole Red bell pepper, diced
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
Instructions
First, prepare the cashew crema: Soak the raw cashews in hot water for at least 15 minutes (or cold water for 2 hours) until softened. Drain and rinse the cashews, then blend them in a high-speed blender with 2 tablespoons of lime juice, 1/4 cup of water, and a pinch of salt until completely smooth and creamy. Set aside. Next, prepare the tempeh and black bean filling: Crumble the tempeh into small pieces. Drain and rinse the black beans. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tempeh and cook for 5-7 minutes, breaking it up with a spoon, until lightly browned. Stir in the chipotle powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the drained black beans to the skillet, along with 1/4 cup of water, and simmer for 5 minutes, mashing some of the beans with the back of your spoon to create a slightly thicker consistency. While the filling simmers, make the corn salsa: In a small bowl, combine the corn kernels, diced red bell pepper, remaining 1 tablespoon of lime juice, and chopped cilantro. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper. Warm the corn tortillas by heating them in a dry skillet over medium heat for 30 seconds per side, or microwave them briefly. To serve, divide the tempeh and black bean filling among the warm tortillas. Top with generous spoonfuls of the fresh corn salsa and a drizzle of the cashew crema.

High-Protein Savory Tofu Bites

🥜 40g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 2 tbsp Tamari or soy sauce
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp Cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
Instructions
Press the extra-firm tofu for at least 30 minutes to remove as much water as possible. Once pressed, cut the tofu into 1-inch cubes. In a medium bowl, whisk together the tamari, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, and smoked paprika. Add the tofu cubes to the bowl and gently toss to coat them evenly with the marinade. Let sit for 5 minutes. Sprinkle the cornstarch over the marinated tofu and toss again until each piece is lightly coated. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tofu cubes in a single layer, ensuring not to overcrowd the pan. Cook for 5-7 minutes, flipping occasionally, until all sides are golden brown and crispy. Alternatively, you can bake them: arrange coated tofu on a parchment-lined baking sheet and bake at 200°C (400°F) for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, until crispy. Serve hot as a savory, high-protein snack.

High-Protein Black Lentil & Forbidden Rice Bowl with Crispy Tofu and Roasted Broccoli

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Forbidden black rice, dry
  • 1.5 cup Water
  • 0.5 cup Black lentils, dry
  • 1.5 cup Vegetable broth
  • 340 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 400 g Broccoli florets
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Tamari
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.2 tsp Red pepper flakes (optional)
Instructions

High-Protein Smoky Tempeh & Kidney Bean Skillet with Collard Greens and Quinoa

🥜 35g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Quinoa, dry
  • 1.5 cup Water
  • 226 g Tempeh, extra firm
  • 425 g Kidney beans, canned
  • 150 g Collard greens, destemmed and chopped
  • 400 g Crushed tomatoes
  • 0.5 cup Vegetable broth
  • 1.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Chili powder
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp Apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Rinse quinoa thoroughly. In a small saucepan, combine quinoa and water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Remove from heat and let sit, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. While quinoa cooks, crumble the tempeh into small pieces. Drain and rinse the canned kidney beans. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add crumbled tempeh and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring frequently, until lightly browned. Stir in smoked paprika, cumin, garlic powder, chili powder, salt, and black pepper. Cook for another minute until fragrant. Add the collard greens to the skillet and sauté for 3-5 minutes until they begin to soften. Stir in the drained kidney beans, crushed tomatoes, vegetable broth, maple syrup, and apple cider vinegar. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and cook for 10-15 minutes, or until the collard greens are tender and the flavors have melded. Adjust seasoning if needed. Serve the smoky tempeh and kidney bean skillet over the cooked quinoa, dividing evenly into two bowls.

High-Protein Mediterranean Lentil & Freekeh Salad with Marinated Tofu

High-Protein Black Bean & Seitan Meatloaf with Roasted Root Vegetables

🥜 41g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1.5 cup Cooked black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 200 g Seitan, finely chopped or pulsed
  • 0.5 cup Rolled oats
  • 0.2 cup Walnuts, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Dried thyme
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 whole Carrots, peeled and chopped
  • 2 whole Parsnips, peeled and chopped
  • 2 whole Potatoes, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Fresh rosemary, chopped
Instructions
Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). In a large bowl, mash the black beans until mostly smooth but still with some texture. Add the finely chopped seitan, rolled oats, chopped walnuts, tomato paste, tamari, smoked paprika, garlic powder, dried thyme, salt, and black pepper to the mashed black beans. Mix well with your hands until thoroughly combined and the mixture can hold its shape. Form the mixture into a small loaf shape and place it on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. In a separate bowl, toss the chopped carrots, parsnips, and potatoes with olive oil, rosemary, salt, and pepper. Spread the seasoned root vegetables around the meatloaf on the baking sheet.
