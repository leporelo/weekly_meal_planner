High-Protein Savory Tofu & Chickpea Scramble with Spinach and Bell Peppers

🥜 28g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 165 g Cooked chickpeas
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper, diced
  • 100 g Baby spinach
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (kala namak)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Black pepper, to taste
Instructions
1. Thoroughly press the extra-firm tofu to remove excess water, then crumble it into a bowl using your hands or a fork. 2. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the diced red bell pepper and sauté for 5-7 minutes until softened. 3. Add the crumbled tofu and chickpeas to the skillet. Sprinkle in the nutritional yeast, turmeric powder, black salt, garlic powder, and black pepper. Stir well to combine and cook for 8-10 minutes, breaking up any large tofu clumps and allowing it to lightly brown. 4. Stir in the baby spinach and cook until wilted, about 2-3 minutes. 5. Divide the scramble evenly into two bowls and serve immediately. This recipe works well for meal prep; components can be cooked ahead and reheated.

High-Protein Black Bean & Millet Bowl with Roasted Zucchini and Tahini Dressing

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1.2 cup Dry millet
  • 3.8 cup Vegetable broth or water
  • 330 g Cooked black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 2 whole Zucchini, diced
  • 100 g Baby spinach
  • 0.5 whole Bell pepper (any color), sliced
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 4 tbsp Water (for dressing)
  • 1 tsp Salt and black pepper, to taste
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss the diced zucchini with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes, or until tender and slightly caramelized. 2. While zucchini roasts, combine dry millet and vegetable broth (or water) in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until all liquid is absorbed and millet is fluffy. 3. Prepare the tahini dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together tahini, lemon juice, garlic powder, 4 tablespoons of water, salt, and pepper until smooth and creamy. Add more water if needed to reach desired consistency. 4. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked millet, black beans, roasted zucchini, baby spinach, and sliced bell pepper evenly between two serving bowls. 5. Drizzle generously with the tahini dressing. This meal is excellent for meal prep; store components separately and assemble just before serving.

High-Protein Seitan & Lentil Curry with Basmati Rice

🥜 45g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Basmati rice, dry
  • 2 cup Water (for rice)
  • 250 g Seitan, sliced or cubed
  • 0.5 cup Dry brown lentils
  • 400 ml Full-fat coconut milk
  • 400 g Diced tomatoes, canned
  • 150 g Baby spinach
  • 1.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger, grated (or 0.5 tsp dried)
  • 2 tbsp Curry powder
  • 1 tsp Garam masala
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 tsp Salt and black pepper, to taste
Instructions
1. Rinse basmati rice thoroughly. Combine rice and 2 cups of water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Remove from heat and let sit, covered, for another 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 2. While the rice cooks, heat olive oil in a large pot or deep skillet over medium heat. Add the sliced seitan and red bell pepper, cooking for 5-7 minutes until the seitan is lightly browned and the pepper softens. 3. Stir in the garlic powder, grated ginger, curry powder, and garam masala. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the dry brown lentils, coconut milk, and diced tomatoes (undrained). Stir well, bring to a gentle simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for 20-25 minutes, or until lentils are tender, stirring occasionally. 4. Stir in the baby spinach and cook until wilted, about 2-3 minutes. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. 5. Serve the seitan and lentil curry hot over the fluffy basmati rice. This curry stores well in the refrigerator for future meals.

High-Protein Savory Baked Walnuts & Pepitas

🥜 24g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Walnut halves
  • 1 cup Pepitas (pumpkin seeds)
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (gluten-free soy sauce)
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 300°F (150°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. In a medium bowl, combine the walnut halves and pepitas. 3. Add the tamari, smoked paprika, and garlic powder to the bowl. Toss thoroughly until the nuts and seeds are evenly coated with the seasonings. 4. Spread the seasoned walnuts and pepitas in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. 5. Bake for 15-20 minutes, stirring halfway through, until they are fragrant and lightly toasted. Keep a close eye on them to prevent burning. Let cool completely before serving, as they will crisp up further as they cool. Store in an airtight container for up to a week.

High-Protein Savory Buckwheat Groats with Smoked Tofu and Spinach

🥜 43g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 160 g Buckwheat Groats
  • 4 cup Water
  • 300 g Smoked Tofu, firm
  • 200 g Fresh Spinach
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 2 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
Instructions
1. Rinse buckwheat groats thoroughly under cold water. Combine buckwheat and water in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until water is absorbed and buckwheat is tender. Set aside. 2. While buckwheat cooks, press any excess water from the smoked tofu, then cut it into small cubes or crumble it. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. 3. Add the smoked tofu to the skillet and cook, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned and crispy, about 8-10 minutes. 4. Add the spinach to the skillet with the tofu and cook until wilted, about 2-3 minutes. 5. Stir in the cooked buckwheat, nutritional yeast, tamari, garlic powder, and black pepper. Mix everything well to combine, ensuring the nutritional yeast coats the buckwheat and tofu. Cook for another 2-3 minutes to heat through. Serve immediately in bowls.

High-Protein Seitan & Quinoa Power Bowl with Roasted Carrots & Green Beans and a Tahini-Lemon Dressing

🥜 52g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Seitan, sliced or cubed
  • 140 g Quinoa
  • 2.5 cup Water (for quinoa)
  • 300 g Carrots, chopped
  • 200 g Green Beans, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 2 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 2 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 4 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 4 tbsp Water (for dressing)
  • 2 tsp Maple Syrup (optional)
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. In a large bowl, toss the chopped carrots and trimmed green beans with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Spread them in a single layer on the baking sheet. 2. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until vegetables are tender-crisp and slightly caramelized. Halfway through roasting, add the seitan to the baking sheet and toss with the vegetables to coat in seasoning. Continue roasting until seitan is lightly browned. 3. While vegetables and seitan roast, cook the quinoa. Combine quinoa and 2.5 cups of water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork. 4. Prepare the tahini-lemon dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together tahini, lemon juice, 4 tablespoons of water, and optional maple syrup. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Add more water if needed to reach desired consistency. 5. To assemble bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls. Top with roasted carrots, green beans, and seitan. Drizzle generously with the tahini-lemon dressing. Serve warm.

High-Protein White Bean & Seitan Stew with Kale and Roasted Red Peppers

🥜 43g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 3 cup Canned White Beans (e.g., Cannellini), rinsed and drained
  • 200 g Seitan, diced
  • 200 g Kale, stems removed, chopped
  • 2 whole Red Bell Pepper, deseeded and chopped
  • 3 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 2 cup Canned Diced Tomatoes
  • 4 whole Garlic Cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp Dried Thyme
  • 1 tsp Dried Rosemary
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add the diced red bell pepper and minced garlic. Sauté for 5-7 minutes until the bell pepper begins to soften. 2. Add the diced seitan to the pot and cook for another 3-5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned. 3. Stir in the vegetable broth, diced tomatoes, dried thyme, and dried rosemary. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for 15 minutes to allow the flavors to meld. 4. Add the rinsed and drained white beans and chopped kale to the pot. Stir well and continue to cook for another 5-7 minutes, or until the kale has wilted and the beans are heated through. 5. Season the stew with salt and black pepper to taste. Serve hot in bowls, optionally with a side of crusty bread.

High-Protein Rosemary & Sea Salt Roasted Chickpeas & Almonds

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 whole Canned Chickpeas, rinsed and thoroughly dried
  • 100 g Whole Almonds
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1.5 tsp Dried Rosemary
  • 1 tsp Sea Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions

High-Protein Tempeh, Quinoa & Black Bean Hash with Sweet Potato

🥜 45g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 150 g Tempeh
  • 1 cup Cooked Quinoa
  • 1 cup Cooked Black Beans
  • 300 g Sweet Potato
  • 2 cup Spinach
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
Peel the sweet potato and cut it into small, uniform cubes, about 1/2-inch in size. Crumble the tempeh roughly with your hands or a fork. Heat the olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the sweet potato cubes and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they start to soften and brown slightly. Add the crumbled tempeh to the skillet along with the smoked paprika, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Cook for another 5-7 minutes, stirring frequently, until the tempeh is lightly browned and seasoned. Stir in the cooked quinoa and black beans, mixing well to combine with the sweet potato and tempeh. Finally, add the fresh spinach, cooking just until it wilts, about 2-3 minutes. Divide the hash evenly between two plates and serve warm.

High-Protein Seitan "Steak" Salad with White Beans and Greens

🥜 65g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 10 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 150 g Seitan (pre-made or homemade)
  • 1 cup Cooked Cannellini Beans
  • 4 cup Mixed Greens
  • 1 whole Red Bell Pepper
  • 0.5 whole Cucumber
  • 0.5 cup Cherry Tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil (for cooking seitan)
  • 2 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 1 tbsp Tahini
  • 1 tsp Dijon Mustard
  • 0.5 tsp Dried Oregano
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
Drain and rinse the cannellini beans. Slice the seitan into thin strips or small 'steak' like pieces. Dice the red bell pepper and cucumber, and halve the cherry tomatoes. In a small bowl, whisk together the lemon juice, tahini, Dijon mustard, dried oregano, salt, and pepper to create the dressing. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seitan strips and cook for 5-7 minutes, turning occasionally, until they are nicely browned and slightly crispy. In two large bowls, arrange the mixed greens as a base. Top with the cooked cannellini beans, diced bell pepper, cucumber, and halved cherry tomatoes. Divide the cooked seitan 'steaks' evenly between the salads. Drizzle generously with the prepared lemon-tahini dressing just before serving.

High-Protein Seitan Cutlets with Roasted Root Vegetables and Savory Gravy

🥜 60g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 150 g Seitan Cutlets
  • 150 g Carrots
  • 150 g Parsnips
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 tsp Dried Thyme
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 1.5 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 1 tbsp Cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 1 tsp Soy Sauce or Tamari
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Peel the carrots and parsnips, then chop them into bite-sized pieces. In a large bowl, toss the chopped root vegetables with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, dried thyme, salt, and pepper until evenly coated. Spread the vegetables in a single layer on a baking sheet and roast for 25-30 minutes, or until tender and slightly caramelized, flipping them halfway through. While the vegetables are roasting, prepare the seitan cutlets. Heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the seitan cutlets for 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown and heated through. For the gravy, in a small saucepan, whisk together 1/4 cup of the vegetable broth with the cornstarch until smooth. Add the remaining 1 1/4 cups of vegetable broth, nutritional yeast, and soy sauce (or tamari) to the saucepan. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the gravy thickens to your desired consistency, about 3-5 minutes. Serve the seitan cutlets and roasted root vegetables with a generous pour of the savory gravy.

High-Protein Tofu, Spinach, and Sun-Dried Tomato Scramble with Buckwheat Groats

🥜 28g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Firm tofu
  • 85 g Buckwheat groats (uncooked)
  • 250 ml Water (for groats)
  • 100 g Baby spinach
  • 30 g Sun-dried tomatoes (oil-packed, drained and chopped)
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.2 tsp Kala Namak (black salt)
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
Instructions
1. Rinse the buckwheat groats thoroughly under cold water. Combine the groats with 250ml of water in a small saucepan, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside. 2. While groats are cooking, press the tofu to remove excess water. Crumble the tofu into a medium-sized bowl using your hands or a fork. Stir in the nutritional yeast, turmeric powder, Kala Namak, and black pepper until well combined. The Kala Namak provides an 'eggy' flavor. 3. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tofu mixture and cook, stirring occasionally, for 8-10 minutes, until the tofu is heated through and slightly browned. Ensure it's not sticking to the pan. 4. Stir in the chopped sun-dried tomatoes and baby spinach. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, until the spinach wilts. 5. To serve, divide the cooked buckwheat groats between two plates. Top each portion with half of the tofu scramble. Enjoy immediately.

High-Protein Lentil & Quinoa Salad with Roasted Asparagus and Lemon-Dill Dressing

🥜 21g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 100 g Green lentils (dry)
  • 85 g Quinoa (dry)
  • 250 g Asparagus
  • 150 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 0.5 whole Cucumber
  • 2 tbsp Fresh dill (chopped)
  • 1 whole Lemon
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 800 ml Water (for cooking lentils/quinoa)
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Snap off the woody ends of the asparagus. Toss the asparagus spears with 1 tbsp of olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread them on a baking sheet and roast for 10-12 minutes, or until tender-crisp. Once done, chop into 1-inch pieces. 2. While asparagus roasts, cook the lentils: Rinse lentils, then combine with 400ml water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until tender. Drain any excess water. Cook the quinoa: Rinse quinoa, then combine with 400ml water in a separate saucepan. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork. 3. Dice the cucumber and halve the cherry tomatoes. In a large bowl, combine the cooked lentils, quinoa, roasted asparagus, diced cucumber, and halved cherry tomatoes. 4. Prepare the dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together the juice of one lemon, 2 tbsp of olive oil, Dijon mustard, fresh dill, salt, and black pepper until emulsified. 5. Pour the dressing over the salad ingredients and toss gently to combine. Serve warm or at room temperature.

High-Protein Tempeh & Black Bean Enchilada Bake

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 227 g Tempeh
  • 400 g Black beans (canned, rinsed and drained)
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 360 ml Enchilada sauce
  • 6 whole Corn tortillas
  • 0.5 cup Raw cashews (for crema)
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast (for crema)
  • 1 tbsp Lime juice
  • 60 ml Water (for crema)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro (chopped, for garnish)
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Lightly grease a small baking dish (approx. 8x8 inch). 2. Prepare the tempeh filling: Crumble the tempeh into small pieces. Dice the red bell pepper. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tempeh and bell pepper and cook for 5-7 minutes, until the tempeh is lightly browned and the pepper is tender-crisp. Stir in the black beans, cumin powder, chili powder, and salt. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, then remove from heat. 3. Prepare the cashew crema: Soak cashews in hot water for at least 15 minutes, then drain. Blend the soaked cashews with nutritional yeast, lime juice, and 60ml fresh water until completely smooth and creamy. Add more water if needed to reach desired consistency. 4. Assembly: Spread about 1/4 cup of enchilada sauce on the bottom of the prepared baking dish. Warm tortillas slightly (in microwave or dry skillet) to make them pliable. Spoon some tempeh and black bean filling down the center of each tortilla, then roll it up tightly and place seam-side down in the baking dish. Repeat with remaining tortillas and filling. 5. Pour the remaining enchilada sauce over the rolled tortillas, ensuring they are well covered. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until bubbling and heated through. 6. Remove from oven, drizzle with cashew crema, and sprinkle with fresh cilantro before serving. Serve hot.

High-Protein Tofu & Black Bean Breakfast Hash with Roasted Bell Peppers and Avocado

🥜 32g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 340 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed

  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 1 whole Yellow bell pepper
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika

  • 0.5 tsp Chili powder
