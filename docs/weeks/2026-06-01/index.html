High-Protein Herbed Tofu Scramble with Spinach and Mushrooms

🥜 32g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 450 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 200 g Cremini mushrooms
  • 150 g Fresh spinach
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Dried dill
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (kala namak)
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 4 tbsp Unsweetened plant milk (e.g., soy milk)
Instructions
Press the tofu to remove excess water; wrap it in paper towels or a clean cloth and place a heavy object on top for at least 15-20 minutes. While tofu presses, wash and slice the cremini mushrooms, and wash the spinach. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Crumble the pressed tofu directly into the skillet with your hands, breaking it into small pieces resembling scrambled eggs. Add sliced mushrooms to the skillet and sauté for 5-7 minutes until mushrooms are softened and tofu is lightly browned. Stir in the nutritional yeast, turmeric powder, garlic powder, dried dill, black salt, and black pepper, mixing well to evenly coat the tofu. Pour in the unsweetened plant milk and continue to cook for 2-3 minutes, allowing the scramble to become slightly creamy. Finally, add the fresh spinach and cook until wilted, about 2-3 minutes. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Mediterranean Lentil & Quinoa Bowl with Roasted Zucchini and Bell Peppers

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 120 g Green lentils, dried
  • 100 g Quinoa, uncooked
  • 500 ml Vegetable broth
  • 1 whole Zucchini, medium
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 150 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 0.5 whole Cucumber, half
  • 60 g Kalamata olives, pitted
  • 0.2 cup Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 whole Lemon, for juice
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 4 tbsp Hemp seeds
Instructions
Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Rinse quinoa thoroughly under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine rinsed quinoa with 250ml of vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all liquid is absorbed. Remove from heat and let sit covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. In a separate saucepan, combine green lentils with 250ml of vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until lentils are tender but still hold their shape. Drain any excess liquid. While quinoa and lentils cook, chop the zucchini and red bell pepper into 1-inch pieces. Toss them with 1 tbsp olive oil, 0.5 tsp dried oregano, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes, flipping halfway, until tender and lightly charred. In a large bowl, combine cooked quinoa, cooked lentils, roasted zucchini and bell pepper, halved cherry tomatoes, diced cucumber, and sliced Kalamata olives. In a small bowl, whisk together the remaining 2 tbsp olive oil, the juice of one lemon, 0.5 tsp dried oregano, and a pinch of salt and pepper to make the dressing. Pour the dressing over the bowl ingredients and toss gently. Divide the mixture into two bowls. Garnish each with fresh chopped parsley and 2 tbsp of hemp seeds.

High-Protein Seitan Steaks with Rosemary Roasted Asparagus and Savory Millet

🥜 45g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Seitan steaks (pre-made)
  • 300 g Asparagus spears
  • 120 g Millet, uncooked
  • 360 ml Vegetable broth
  • 2 whole Fresh rosemary
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Soy sauce (or tamari)
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Rinse millet under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine rinsed millet with 360ml vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until all liquid is absorbed and millet is tender. Fluff with a fork and set aside. Snap off the woody ends of the asparagus spears. On a baking sheet, toss the asparagus with 1 tbsp olive oil, the leaves from 1 sprig of fresh rosemary (finely chopped if dried), and a pinch of salt and pepper. Roast for 10-12 minutes, or until tender-crisp. While asparagus roasts, heat the remaining 2 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. If your seitan steaks are large, slice them in half horizontally to create thinner cutlets. Pan-fry the seitan steaks for 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown and slightly crispy. In the last minute of cooking, add the soy sauce (or tamari), garlic powder, smoked paprika, and a sprig of fresh rosemary to the skillet, coating the seitan well. Remove rosemary sprig before serving. Divide the savory millet between two plates, place the seitan steaks alongside, and serve with the roasted asparagus.

High-Protein Spiced Roasted Chickpeas

🥜 32g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 800 g Canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.2 tsp Cayenne pepper
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Drain and rinse the canned chickpeas thoroughly. Pat them very dry with paper towels; this is crucial for crispiness. In a large bowl, toss the dried chickpeas with olive oil until well coated. Add the smoked paprika, cumin powder, garlic powder, cayenne pepper (if using), salt, and black pepper. Mix thoroughly to ensure all chickpeas are evenly seasoned. Spread the seasoned chickpeas in a single layer on a large baking sheet. Roast for 30-40 minutes, shaking the pan every 10-15 minutes, until the chickpeas are golden brown and crispy. Keep an eye on them towards the end of the cooking time to prevent burning. Let them cool slightly before serving; they will crisp up more as they cool. Store any leftovers in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3-4 days.

High-Protein Savory Steel-Cut Oats with Baked Tempeh and Roasted Greens

🥜 30.3g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 100 g Steel-cut oats
  • 700 ml Vegetable broth
  • 200 g Firm tempeh
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 2 tbsp Tamari
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 100 g Baby spinach
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tsp Everything bagel seasoning
Instructions
1. **Prepare the Tempeh and Vegetables:** Preheat your oven to 200°C (390°F). Slice the tempeh into 1/2-inch thick strips or cubes. In a medium bowl, toss the tempeh with 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp tamari, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, and 1/2 tsp garlic powder until well coated. On a separate baking sheet, toss the cherry tomatoes and baby spinach with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp tamari, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, and 1/2 tsp garlic powder. 2. **Roast:** Spread the seasoned tempeh on one baking sheet and the seasoned vegetables on another (or use separate areas of a large sheet). Roast for 15-20 minutes, flipping the tempeh halfway through, until the tempeh is golden brown and the vegetables are tender-crisp. 3. **Cook the Steel-Cut Oats:** While the tempeh and vegetables are roasting, bring 700ml vegetable broth to a boil in a medium saucepan. Stir in the steel-cut oats, reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the oats are tender and most of the liquid is absorbed. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking. 4. **Finish the Oats:** Once the oats are cooked, stir in the nutritional yeast until fully incorporated and the oats become slightly creamy. 5. **Assemble and Serve:** Divide the cooked savory oats between two serving bowls. Top each bowl generously with the roasted tempeh, roasted cherry tomatoes, and roasted spinach. Sprinkle with everything bagel seasoning before serving.

High-Protein Peanut Tempeh & Quinoa Bowl with Roasted Carrots & Greens

🥜 47.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Firm tempeh
  • 100 g Quinoa
  • 2 cup Vegetable broth
  • 2 whole Carrots
  • 200 g Baby bok choy or kale
  • 4 tbsp Natural peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp Tamari
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Sriracha
  • 1 tbsp Lime juice
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Toasted sesame seeds
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
Instructions
1. **Cook Quinoa:** Rinse the quinoa thoroughly under cold water. Combine quinoa and 2 cups vegetable broth in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all liquid is absorbed. Let stand for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 2. **Prepare and Roast Tempeh & Carrots:** Preheat oven to 200°C (390°F). Slice tempeh into cubes or strips. Peel and slice carrots into rounds or sticks. On a baking sheet, toss tempeh and carrots with 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp tamari, and a pinch of salt. Roast for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, until tempeh is golden and carrots are tender. 3. **Prepare Peanut Dressing:** In a small bowl, whisk together peanut butter, 1 tbsp tamari, rice vinegar, maple syrup, sriracha (if using), lime juice, and sesame oil until smooth. Add a splash of water if needed to reach desired consistency. 4. **Sauté Greens:** In the last 5 minutes of roasting, heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add baby bok choy or kale and cook until wilted, about 2-3 minutes. 5. **Assemble Bowls:** Divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls. Top with roasted tempeh, roasted carrots, and sautéed greens. Drizzle generously with the peanut dressing. Garnish with toasted sesame seeds before serving.

High-Protein Seitan and Chickpea Curry with Brown Rice

🥜 44.5g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Pre-made seitan
  • 400 g Canned chickpeas, rinsed and drained
  • 100 g Brown rice
  • 2 cup Vegetable broth
  • 400 ml Full-fat coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp Red curry paste
  • 12 g Garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp Ginger, grated
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 150 g Baby spinach
  • 2 tbsp Lime juice
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1 tbsp Coconut oil
Instructions
1. **Cook Brown Rice:** Combine brown rice and 2 cups vegetable broth in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 30-40 minutes, or until all liquid is absorbed and rice is tender. Keep warm. 2. **Prepare Curry Base:** While rice cooks, heat 1 tbsp coconut oil in a large pot or deep skillet over medium heat. Add minced garlic and grated ginger, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the red curry paste and cook for another 2-3 minutes, stirring constantly, to toast the spices. 3. **Build the Curry:** Pour in the full-fat coconut milk and stir well to combine with the curry paste. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer. Add the sliced red bell pepper and seitan (cubed or sliced as desired). Simmer for 10-12 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld and the bell pepper to soften. 4. **Finish and Serve:** Stir in the rinsed and drained chickpeas and baby spinach. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, until the spinach has wilted. Remove from heat and stir in the lime juice. Serve the curry hot over the cooked brown rice, garnished with fresh cilantro.

David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Quinoa
  • 2 cup Water
  • 224 g Tempeh
  • 4 cup Kale
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 2 tbsp Tamari
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper

High-Protein Lentil & Farro Power Bowl with Roasted Sweet Potato & Tahini Dressing

🥜 27g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 100 g Green Lentils (dry)
  • 100 g Farro (dry)
  • 1 whole Sweet Potato
  • 2 cup Broccoli Florets
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 4 tbsp Water
  • 1 tsp Maple Syrup
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 3 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Hemp Seeds
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Parsley (chopped)
Instructions
Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Peel the sweet potato and cut into 1-inch cubes. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, 0.5 tsp smoked paprika, 0.5 tsp cumin, 0.25 tsp salt, and 0.25 tsp black pepper. Spread on a baking sheet and roast for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway, until tender and lightly caramelized. In the last 10 minutes of roasting, add the broccoli florets to the baking sheet and roast until tender-crisp. While vegetables roast, cook the farro: Combine 100g farro with 2 cups water and 0.25 tsp salt in a pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until tender. Drain any excess water. In a separate pot, cook the lentils: Combine 100g green lentils with 2.5 cups water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 20-25 minutes until tender. Drain any excess water. For the dressing, whisk together tahini, lemon juice, 4 tbsp water, maple syrup, 0.5 tsp smoked paprika, 0.5 tsp cumin, 0.25 tsp salt, and 0.25 tsp black pepper. If the dressing is too thick, add more water 1 tbsp at a time until desired consistency. Assemble bowls: Divide cooked farro and lentils between two bowls. Top with roasted sweet potato and broccoli. Drizzle generously with tahini dressing and sprinkle with hemp seeds and fresh parsley.

High-Protein Seitan & White Bean 'Meatballs' with Marinara & Zucchini Noodles

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 250 g Seitan (pre-made)
  • 400 g Canned White Beans (e.g., cannellini)
  • 0.5 cup Plain Breadcrumbs (Panko or regular)
  • 3 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1 tsp Dried Oregano
  • 1 tsp Dried Basil
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1.5 cup Marinara Sauce (no onion)
  • 2 whole Zucchini
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Parsley (chopped)
Instructions
Rinse and drain the white beans, then mash them coarsely in a medium bowl. Finely chop or crumble the seitan and add it to the bowl with the mashed white beans. Add the breadcrumbs, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, dried oregano, dried basil, smoked paprika, 0.5 tsp salt, and 0.25 tsp black pepper. Mix thoroughly until well combined. Form the mixture into approximately 12-14 small 'meatballs'. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Carefully place the meatballs in the skillet and cook for 5-7 minutes, turning occasionally, until browned on all sides. Reduce heat to medium-low, pour in the marinara sauce, cover, and simmer for 10 minutes to allow the flavors to meld and the meatballs to cook through. While the meatballs simmer, prepare the zucchini noodles. Using a spiralizer, create zucchini noodles. Alternatively, use a vegetable peeler to create wide ribbons. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a separate pan over medium heat. Sauté the zucchini noodles for 3-5 minutes until tender-crisp. Season with 0.25 tsp salt and 0.25 tsp black pepper. Divide the zucchini noodles into two serving bowls, top with the seitan and white bean meatballs and marinara sauce. Garnish with fresh parsley before serving.

Ingredients

  • 2 cup Fresh spinach, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika

  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper

  • 1.5 cup Cherry tomatoes
