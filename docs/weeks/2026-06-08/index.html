High-Protein Savory Lentil & Quinoa Breakfast Bowl with Roasted Tomatoes

🥜 40g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Dry brown lentils
  • 0.5 cup Quinoa
  • 3.5 cup Vegetable broth
  • 2 cup Cherry tomatoes
  • 4 cup Fresh spinach
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.5 whole Avocado, sliced (optional)
Instructions
Rinse quinoa thoroughly under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine quinoa with 1 cup of vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all liquid is absorbed. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. In another saucepan, combine rinsed brown lentils with 2.5 cups of vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer, covered, for 20-25 minutes, or until tender. Drain any excess liquid. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss cherry tomatoes with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes, or until softened and slightly burst. In a large skillet, wilt the fresh spinach over medium heat for 2-3 minutes. To assemble, divide the cooked quinoa, lentils, wilted spinach, and roasted cherry tomatoes into two serving bowls. Sprinkle each bowl generously with 2 tablespoons of nutritional yeast. If desired, top with sliced avocado.

High-Protein Baked Tofu & Black Bean Quinoa Bowl with Roasted Bell Peppers

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 280 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 2 cup Cooked quinoa
  • 1.5 cup Black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 2 whole Bell peppers (red, yellow, or orange), seeded and sliced
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 whole Lime, juiced
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Cut the pressed tofu into 1-inch cubes. In a large bowl, toss the tofu cubes and sliced bell peppers with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, cumin, chili powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper until evenly coated. Spread the seasoned tofu and bell peppers in a single layer on a baking sheet. Bake for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway through, until the tofu is golden brown and the bell peppers are tender-crisp. While the tofu and peppers are roasting, prepare the quinoa according to package instructions if not already cooked. In a separate bowl, combine the rinsed and drained black beans with the juice of half a lime and a pinch of salt. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls. Top each with the baked tofu, roasted bell peppers, and seasoned black beans. Squeeze the remaining lime juice over the bowls and garnish with fresh cilantro.

High-Protein Lentil & Mushroom Shepherd's Pie with Sweet Potato Topping

🥜 37g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 50 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 whole Sweet potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 0.5 cup Unsweetened plant milk
  • 1 cup Dry brown lentils, rinsed
  • 3 cup Vegetable broth
  • 2 cup Cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 whole Carrots, diced
  • 2 whole Celery stalks, diced
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 whole Bay leaf
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Place diced sweet potatoes in a pot, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Cook for 15-20 minutes or until fork-tender. Drain well, then mash with plant milk, 0.25 tsp salt, and 0.25 tsp black pepper until smooth and creamy. Set aside. In a large saucepan, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add diced carrots and celery, and cook for 5-7 minutes until slightly softened. Add sliced mushrooms, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and dried thyme, cooking for another 5-7 minutes until mushrooms are browned and tender. Stir in tomato paste and cook for 1 minute. Add rinsed lentils, 3 cups vegetable broth, and the bay leaf to the saucepan. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat, cover, and cook for 25-30 minutes, or until lentils are tender and most of the liquid has been absorbed. Remove the bay leaf and stir in 0.5 tsp salt and 0.25 tsp black pepper. Pour the lentil and vegetable mixture into a small oven-safe dish (approximately 8x8 inches). Carefully spread the mashed sweet potato topping over the lentil mixture, using the back of a spoon to create peaks. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C) and bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the topping is lightly golden and the filling is bubbling. Let stand for a few minutes before serving.

High-Protein Chickpea & Red Lentil Scramble with Spinach

🥜 28g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 180 g Red lentils (dried)
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (Kala Namak)
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 60 g Chickpea flour
  • 120 ml Water
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 60 g Fresh spinach
Instructions
Rinse the red lentils thoroughly under cold water. Place them in a medium saucepan with 3 cups (720ml) of water and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until the lentils are tender and most of the water is absorbed. Set aside. In a separate bowl, whisk together the chickpea flour, 120ml water, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, turmeric, black salt, and black pepper until smooth. Heat the olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the cooked lentils to the skillet and sauté for 2-3 minutes. Pour the chickpea flour mixture over the lentils, stirring constantly to break up any clumps and create a scramble-like texture. Continue to cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chickpea mixture is cooked through and firm. Stir in the fresh spinach and cook until wilted, about 2 minutes. Divide the scramble evenly between two plates and serve immediately.

High-Protein Lemon-Herb Seitan & Farro Bowl with Roasted Zucchini and Olives

🥜 41g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Farro (dry)
  • 600 ml Water
  • 200 g Seitan, pre-made
  • 300 g Zucchini
  • 70 g Kalamata olives, pitted
  • 120 g Chickpeas (canned, drained and rinsed)
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice (freshly squeezed)
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (390°F). Rinse the farro thoroughly under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine the farro with 600ml of water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 25-30 minutes, or until the farro is tender and chewy. Drain any excess water and set aside. While the farro cooks, slice the seitan into bite-sized pieces and chop the zucchini into half-moon shapes. On a baking sheet, toss the seitan and zucchini with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper. Roast for 15-20 minutes, flipping halfway, until the zucchini is tender-crisp and the seitan is lightly browned. In two serving bowls, distribute the cooked farro. Top each bowl with the roasted seitan and zucchini, chickpeas, and Kalamata olives. Drizzle with the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil and fresh lemon juice before serving.

Spicy Peanut Tofu & Chickpea Curry with Brown Rice

🥜 56g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 50 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 180 g Brown rice (dry)
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 240 g Chickpeas (canned, drained and rinsed)
  • 400 ml Full-fat coconut milk
  • 80 g Peanut butter, natural
  • 2 tbsp Tamari
  • 1 tbsp Sriracha (or to taste)
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 60 g Fresh spinach
  • 2 tbsp Lime juice (freshly squeezed)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
Instructions
First, cook the brown rice: combine 180g brown rice with 400ml water in a saucepan, bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 35-40 minutes until water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside. While rice cooks, press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15 minutes to remove excess water, then cube into 1-inch pieces. Slice the red bell pepper into thin strips. Heat olive oil in a large pot or deep skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tofu cubes and cook for 5-7 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden brown on all sides. Remove tofu and set aside. Add bell pepper to the pot and sauté for 3-4 minutes until slightly softened. Stir in the coconut milk, peanut butter, tamari, and sriracha, whisking until the peanut butter is fully dissolved and the sauce is smooth. Bring the curry to a gentle simmer, then add the chickpeas and cooked tofu. Reduce heat to low, cover, and let it simmer for 10-15 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld. Finally, stir in the fresh spinach until it wilts and add the lime juice. Serve the curry hot over the cooked brown rice, dividing evenly into two bowls.

High-Protein Tamari Roasted Almonds & Sunflower Seeds

🥜 23g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 12 min cook
David: 1.0 servings Baska: 0.8 servings
Ingredients
  • 70 g Whole almonds
  • 35 g Sunflower seeds
  • 1 tbsp Tamari
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
Instructions

Roast in the preheated oven for 10-12 minutes, stirring once halfway through, until fragrant and lightly toasted. Be careful not to burn them. Remove from the oven and let them cool completely on the baking sheet; they will crisp up as they cool. Store in an airtight container once cooled. Enjoy as a crunchy, protein-rich snack.

High-Protein Spiced Polenta with Black Beans & Roasted Red Pepper

🥜 28g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Polenta (coarse-ground cornmeal)
  • 3 cup Vegetable broth
  • 1.5 cup Canned black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 0.8 cup Roasted red peppers (from a jar), drained and diced
  • 3 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
Instructions
In a medium saucepan, bring the vegetable broth to a boil over medium-high heat. Once boiling, slowly whisk in the polenta, stirring continuously to prevent lumps. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and cook for 15-20 minutes, stirring every few minutes, until the polenta is thick and creamy. While the polenta cooks, heat the olive oil in a separate skillet over medium heat. Add the rinsed black beans, diced roasted red peppers, smoked paprika, cumin powder, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Sauté for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the beans are heated through and the flavors have melded. Once the polenta is cooked, stir in the nutritional yeast until fully combined. Divide the creamy polenta into two bowls. Top each bowl generously with the spiced black bean and roasted red pepper mixture. Garnish with fresh chopped cilantro before serving.

High-Protein Glazed Tempeh & Barley Power Bowl with Roasted Asparagus

🥜 32g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Pearl barley
  • 2.5 cup Water
  • 224 g Block tempeh
  • 300 g Asparagus spears
  • 4 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 2 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
  • 0.1 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds (for garnish)
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Rinse the pearl barley under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine the rinsed barley and 2.5 cups of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 30-35 minutes, or until the barley is tender and most of the water is absorbed. While the barley cooks, press the block of tempeh to remove excess water, then cut it into 1.5 cm (0.6 inch) cubes. Snap the woody ends off the asparagus spears. In a small bowl, whisk together the tamari, maple syrup, rice vinegar, grated ginger, and sesame oil to create the glaze. Toss the tempeh cubes with half of the glaze. Arrange the tempeh and asparagus on a baking sheet. Drizzle the asparagus with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast for 15 minutes, then flip the tempeh and asparagus. Drizzle the remaining glaze over the tempeh and roast for another 10-15 minutes, or until the tempeh is caramelized and the asparagus is tender-crisp. Divide the cooked barley between two bowls. Top with the glazed tempeh and roasted asparagus. Sprinkle with sesame seeds before serving.

High-Protein White Bean & Artichoke Heart Skillet with Seitan and Toasted Walnuts

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 220 g Seitan, pre-made, sliced or cubed
  • 1.5 cup Canned cannellini beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 cup Canned diced tomatoes, undrained
  • 1 cup Marinated artichoke hearts (from a jar), drained and quartered
  • 3 cup Fresh spinach
  • 0.5 cup Walnut halves
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 0.5 tsp Dried basil
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.2 tsp Red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet or pan over medium-high heat. Add the seitan slices or cubes and cook for 5-7 minutes, turning occasionally, until browned and slightly crispy. Remove the seitan from the skillet and set aside. In the same skillet, add the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil. Add the drained cannellini beans, diced tomatoes (with their juice), quartered artichoke hearts, dried oregano, dried basil, garlic powder, red pepper flakes (if using), salt, and black pepper. Stir to combine and bring to a gentle simmer. Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover, and let it cook for 8-10 minutes to allow the flavors to meld. While the skillet simmers, place the walnut halves in a small dry pan over medium-low heat. Toast for 3-5 minutes, stirring frequently, until fragrant and lightly browned. Watch carefully to prevent burning. Remove from heat. Once the skillet mixture has simmered, stir in the fresh spinach until it wilts, which should take 2-3 minutes. Return the cooked seitan to the skillet and toss to combine and heat through. Divide the mixture evenly between two plates or bowls. Top each serving with the toasted walnuts before serving.

High-Protein Spicy Roasted Broad Beans (Fava Beans)

🥜 25g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Dried broad beans (fava beans)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.2 tsp Cayenne pepper (optional, for extra spice)
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
Instructions

High-Protein Savory Quinoa & Black-Eyed Pea Bowl with Baked Tofu Bites

🥜 32g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Quinoa, dry
  • 1.5 cup Water or vegetable broth
  • 300 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 1.5 cup Black-eyed peas, cooked
  • 4 cup Spinach
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Cut the pressed tofu into 1.5 cm cubes. In a medium bowl, toss the tofu cubes with 1 tbsp olive oil, tamari, garlic powder, smoked paprika, 1 tbsp nutritional yeast, 1/4 tsp salt, and 1/8 tsp black pepper until evenly coated. While the tofu bakes, rinse the quinoa thoroughly. In a medium saucepan, combine the quinoa and water or vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Let it sit covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. In a large pot or skillet, wilt the spinach over medium heat for 2-3 minutes until tender. Stir in the cooked black-eyed peas, remaining 1 tbsp nutritional yeast, 1/4 tsp salt, and 1/8 tsp black pepper, warming through for 2 minutes. To assemble, divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls. Top with the black-eyed pea and spinach mixture, then add the baked tofu bites. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Tofu & Spinach Ricotta Stuffed Shells with Marinara

🥜 40g protein/serving 🔪 25 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 18 whole Jumbo pasta shells
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 4 cup Fresh spinach
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Dried basil
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 cup Marinara sauce
  • 2 tbsp Pine nuts, toasted
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Cook the jumbo pasta shells according to package directions until al dente. Drain and rinse with cold water to prevent sticking, then set aside. While shells are cooking, prepare the tofu ricotta. Crumble the pressed tofu into a large bowl. Add spinach (wilted and squeezed dry if using fresh, or thawed and squeezed dry if frozen), nutritional yeast, garlic powder, dried basil, dried oregano, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Mash with a fork until well combined and a ricotta-like texture is achieved. Spread 1 cup of marinara sauce evenly over the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish. Carefully stuff each cooked pasta shell with the tofu ricotta mixture (about 2-3 tbsp per shell) and arrange them in the baking dish. Pour the remaining 1 cup of marinara sauce over the stuffed shells, ensuring they are mostly covered. Cover the baking dish with foil and bake for 20 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 10-15 minutes, or until the sauce is bubbly and heated through. While the shells are baking, lightly toast the pine nuts in a dry skillet over medium-low heat for 3-5 minutes, shaking frequently, until fragrant and lightly golden. Be careful not to burn them. Remove from heat and set aside. Once baked, remove the shells from the oven, garnish with toasted pine nuts, and serve hot.

High-Protein Savory Black Bean & Tofu Scramble with Bell Peppers and Kale

🥜 42g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed and crumbled
  • 1.5 cup Black beans, canned, rinsed and drained
  • 2 whole Bell peppers (red or yellow), diced
  • 3 cup Kale, stems removed and chopped
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (kala namak, optional)
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Tamari or soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Water or vegetable broth
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15-20 minutes to remove excess water. After pressing, crumble it into a bowl using your hands or a fork. 2. In a small bowl, whisk together the nutritional yeast, turmeric powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, black salt (if using), tamari, water/vegetable broth, salt, and black pepper to create the scramble seasoning. 3. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the diced bell peppers and sauté for 5-7 minutes until they start to soften. 4. Add the crumbled tofu to the skillet and cook, stirring occasionally, for about 8-10 minutes, allowing some pieces to slightly brown. 5. Stir in the rinsed black beans and the chopped kale. Cook for another 3-5 minutes, until the kale has wilted significantly. 6. Pour the prepared seasoning mixture over the tofu and vegetable mixture. Stir well to ensure all ingredients are evenly coated. Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes, stirring constantly, until the liquid has mostly absorbed and the scramble is heated through. Serve immediately.

Hearty Seitan & Chickpea Farro Bowl with Roasted Carrots and Lemon-Tahini Dressing

🥜 62g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 250 g Seitan, pre-made, sliced or cubed
  • 1.5 cup Cooked farro
  • 1.5 cup Chickpeas, canned, rinsed and drained
  • 2 whole Carrots, peeled and sliced into rounds or half-moons
  • 4 cup Spinach or mixed greens
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 3 tbsp Lemon juice, fresh
  • 4 tbsp Water
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 1 tsp Dried parsley
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. In a medium bowl, toss the sliced carrots with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 0.5 teaspoon of cumin, and a pinch of salt and pepper. Spread them in a single layer on one half of the prepared baking sheet. 3. In the same bowl, toss the seitan pieces with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, smoked paprika, dried parsley, and a pinch of salt and pepper. Spread them on the other half of the baking sheet, ensuring they are not overcrowded. 4.
