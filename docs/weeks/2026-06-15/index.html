High-Protein Herbed Tofu & Chickpea Scramble with Roasted Bell Peppers

🥜 42g protein/serving 🔪 25 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 240 g Canned chickpeas, rinsed and drained
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 1 whole Yellow bell pepper
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (Kala Namak)
  • 1 tsp Dried dill
  • 1 tsp Dried parsley
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 100 g Fresh spinach
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
First, press the extra-firm tofu for at least 20 minutes to remove excess water; a tofu press or heavy objects can be used. While tofu is pressing, preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Core and deseed the red and yellow bell peppers, then chop them into 1-inch pieces. Toss the chopped bell peppers with 1 tablespoon of olive oil on a baking sheet and roast for 15-20 minutes, or until tender-crisp and slightly charred. Meanwhile, crumble the pressed tofu into a large bowl with your hands or a fork. Stir in the nutritional yeast, turmeric powder, garlic powder, black salt, dried dill, and dried parsley until the tofu is evenly coated and seasoned. Heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the rinsed and drained chickpeas and sauté for 5 minutes until lightly golden. Add the seasoned crumbled tofu to the skillet and cook, stirring frequently, for 8-10 minutes until heated through and slightly browned. Stir in the roasted bell peppers and fresh spinach, cooking for another 2-3 minutes until the spinach has wilted. Season with black pepper to taste before serving immediately.

High-Protein Seitan & Kidney Bean Grain Bowl with Roasted Carrots and Lemon-Herb Dressing

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Cooked seitan
  • 100 g Dry quinoa
  • 240 g Canned kidney beans, rinsed and drained
  • 3 whole Carrots
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tbsp Fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 whole Lemon, juiced
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Tahini
  • 3 tbsp Water
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 100 g Baby spinach or mixed greens
Instructions
First, cook the quinoa: rinse the dry quinoa thoroughly under cold water. Combine 100g quinoa with 200ml water in a small saucepan, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes until all water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Peel and slice the carrots into 1/4-inch thick rounds or sticks. Toss the carrots with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet, then roast for 20-25 minutes until tender and slightly caramelized. While the carrots roast, slice or dice the cooked seitan into bite-sized pieces and pan-fry it in 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium-high heat for 5-7 minutes until lightly browned and crispy. To prepare the lemon-herb dressing, whisk together the lemon juice, tahini, 1 tablespoon of olive oil, water, chopped fresh parsley, and chopped fresh dill in a small bowl until smooth and creamy. Season with salt and pepper to taste. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa, roasted carrots, pan-fried seitan, rinsed kidney beans, and baby spinach or mixed greens evenly between two serving bowls. Drizzle generously with the lemon-herb dressing before serving.

High-Protein Smoky Tempeh & Black Bean Chili with Cornbread Topping

🥜 36g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Tempeh
  • 240 g Canned black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 400 g Diced tomatoes (canned)
  • 250 ml Vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp Chili powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Dried oregano
  • 0.5 whole Jalapeño (deseeded and minced, optional)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 75 g Cornmeal (fine or medium grind)
  • 25 g All-purpose flour
  • 120 ml Plant milk (unsweetened)
  • 1 tsp Baking powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Crumble the tempeh into small pieces using your hands. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in an oven-safe skillet (like cast iron) or a medium pot over medium-high heat. Add the crumbled tempeh and cook, stirring occasionally, for 5-7 minutes until it's lightly browned and slightly crisp. Add the chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, dried oregano, and minced jalapeño (if using) to the skillet, stirring for 1 minute until fragrant. Stir in the rinsed black beans, diced tomatoes (with their juices), and vegetable broth. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to low and let it cook gently for 10-15 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld and the chili to thicken slightly. While the chili simmers, prepare the cornbread topping: In a medium bowl, whisk together the cornmeal, all-purpose flour, baking powder, and salt. Gradually add the plant milk and stir until just combined; do not overmix. Pour the hot chili into an oven-safe dish (if not using an oven-safe skillet) and carefully spread the cornbread batter evenly over the top. Transfer the dish to the preheated oven and bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the cornbread topping is golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving.

🥜 25g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Shelled edamame (frozen)
  • 50 g Sunflower seeds
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Everything bagel seasoning
Instructions

High-Protein Savory Mung Bean & Quinoa Bowl with Sautéed Greens

🥜 46g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Mung beans
  • 1 cup Quinoa
  • 2 cup Water for cooking quinoa
  • 3 cup Water for cooking mung beans
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 5 cup Spinach, fresh
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 2 tbsp Tamari
  • 2 tbsp Toasted sesame seeds
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
Instructions
Rinse mung beans thoroughly. In a medium pot, combine mung beans with 3 cups of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 25-30 minutes, or until tender. Drain any excess water. In a separate pot, rinse quinoa well. Combine quinoa with 2 cups of water, bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed and quinoa is fluffy. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant. Add spinach and cook until wilted, about 2-3 minutes. In a large bowl, combine cooked mung beans, quinoa, sautéed spinach, nutritional yeast, tamari, and black pepper. Stir well to combine. Divide the mixture evenly into two serving bowls and top each with toasted sesame seeds and additional salt to taste. Serve warm.

High-Protein Spiced Tempeh & Lentil Skillet with Pearl Barley

🥜 55g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Pearl barley
  • 3 cup Water for cooking barley
  • 1 cup Green or brown lentils
  • 4 cup Vegetable broth
  • 220 g Tempeh, pressed and crumbled
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 whole Diced tomatoes, canned
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.2 cup Fresh parsley, chopped
Instructions
First, cook the pearl barley. In a medium pot, combine 1 cup pearl barley with 3 cups water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 30-35 minutes or until tender and water is absorbed. Drain if necessary. Rinse lentils thoroughly. Stir in the rinsed lentils, diced tomatoes (undrained), vegetable broth, smoked paprika, cumin, turmeric, salt, and black pepper. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat, cover, and cook for 20-25 minutes, or until lentils are tender. Stir in the cooked pearl barley. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed. High-Protein Seitan & Butter Bean Casserole with Herbed Crumb Topping

🥜 70g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Seitan, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 2 whole Butter beans, canned, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup Vegetable broth
  • 0.5 cup Full-fat coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.5 cup Whole wheat breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil (for topping)
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 0.5 tsp Dried rosemary
  • 0.1 tsp Salt (for topping)
  • 0.1 tsp Black pepper (for topping)
Instructions
Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). In a large bowl, combine the cubed seitan, drained and rinsed butter beans, vegetable broth, full-fat coconut milk, nutritional yeast, Dijon mustard, dried thyme, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Stir until all ingredients are well combined and the seitan and beans are coated. Pour the mixture into a medium baking dish (approximately 8x8 inch). For the herb crumb topping, in a small bowl, mix together the whole wheat breadcrumbs, olive oil, fresh chopped parsley, dried rosemary, and a pinch of salt and pepper until evenly coated. Sprinkle the crumb topping evenly over the seitan and bean mixture in the baking dish. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until the casserole is bubbling and the topping is golden brown and crispy. Let stand for a few minutes before serving. Divide evenly into two portions and serve warm.

High-Protein Miso Tempeh & Edamame Quinoa Bowl with Roasted Carrots

🥜 41g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Quinoa (dry)
  • 226 g Tempeh block
  • 1 cup Shelled edamame (frozen)
  • 2 whole Carrots, sliced into rounds
  • 2 tbsp White miso paste
  • 2 tbsp Tamari
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tbsp Sesame seeds (for garnish)
Instructions
Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Cook quinoa: rinse quinoa well, then combine with 2 cups of water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes until water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork. Meanwhile, toss sliced carrots with 1 tablespoon of olive oil on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes until tender and slightly caramelized. While carrots roast, steam the tempeh for 10 minutes (this helps it absorb marinades better), then cut into 1-inch cubes. In a small bowl, whisk together miso paste, tamari, maple syrup, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and garlic powder to create the marinade. In a skillet, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium heat. Add cubed tempeh and cook until lightly browned on all sides, about 5-7 minutes. Pour the miso marinade over the tempeh and cook for another 2-3 minutes, stirring, until the sauce thickens and coats the tempeh. Steam or boil the shelled edamame until tender-crisp, about 3-5 minutes. To assemble bowls, divide cooked quinoa, roasted carrots, miso tempeh, and edamame evenly into two serving bowls. Garnish with sesame seeds.

🥜 34g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.5 cup Millet (dry)
  • 400 g Canned black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 200 g Extra-firm tofu (pressed), for feta
  • 2 cup Cherry tomatoes
  • 1 cup Vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tbsp Apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). In a small bowl, crumble the pressed tofu. Add nutritional yeast, apple cider vinegar, dried oregano, 0.5 tsp garlic powder, 0.25 tsp salt, and black pepper. 🥜 20g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Canned cannellini beans, rinsed and drained
  • 2 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 4 tbsp Water
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper

  • 1 cup Carrot sticks (for dipping)

High-Protein Seitan & Green Lentil Stew with Root Vegetables

🥜 50g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 60 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 450 g Seitan (store-bought)
  • 0.5 cup Green lentils (dry)
  • 4 cup Vegetable broth
  • 2 whole Carrots
  • 2 whole Celery stalks
  • 2 whole Potatoes (Russet or Yukon Gold)
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 0.5 tsp Dried rosemary
  • 1 whole Bay leaf
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley (chopped)
Instructions
Rinse the green lentils thoroughly under cold water. Dice the seitan into 1-inch cubes. Peel and dice the carrots, celery, and potatoes into similar-sized pieces. Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the diced seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned on all sides. Remove seitan from the pot and set aside. Add the diced carrots, celery, and potatoes to the pot. Sauté for 7-10 minutes, stirring frequently, until the vegetables start to soften. Stir in the tomato paste, dried thyme, and dried rosemary, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant. Return the seitan to the pot. Add the rinsed green lentils, vegetable broth, bay leaf, salt, and black pepper. Bring the stew to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 30-40 minutes, or until the lentils are tender and the vegetables are cooked through. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking. Remove the bay leaf before serving. Ladle the hearty stew into two bowls and garnish with fresh chopped parsley. Serve hot.
