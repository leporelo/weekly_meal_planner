High-Protein Chickpea Flour & Tofu Scramble with Roasted Cherry Tomatoes and Spinach

🥜 33g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 350 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed and crumbled
  • 0.5 cup Chickpea flour
  • 0.5 cup Water
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.2 tsp Black salt (kala namak, optional for eggy flavor)
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 200 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 100 g Fresh spinach
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss the cherry tomatoes with 1 tbsp of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes until slightly burst and caramelized. While tomatoes roast, prepare the scramble base. In a medium bowl, whisk together chickpea flour, water, nutritional yeast, turmeric, black salt, and smoked paprika until smooth. Heat the remaining 1 tbsp of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tofu and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it starts to brown slightly. Pour the chickpea flour mixture over the tofu, stirring constantly for 3-5 minutes until the scramble thickens and resembles cooked eggs. Fold in the fresh spinach and cook until wilted, about 1-2 minutes. Stir in the roasted cherry tomatoes. Season with additional salt and pepper to taste before serving immediately.

High-Protein Black Bean & Seitan Bowl with Roasted Cauliflower and Green Tahini Dressing

🥜 49g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 cup Cooked quinoa
  • 200 g Seitan, sliced into strips
  • 1.5 cup Black beans, canned, rinsed and drained
  • 400 g Cauliflower florets
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.2 cup Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 4 tbsp Water
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). On a large baking sheet, toss the cauliflower florets with 1 tbsp of olive oil, half of the cumin, chili powder, and smoked paprika. Season with salt and pepper. Roast for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, until tender and slightly browned. While the cauliflower roasts, heat 1 tbsp of olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seitan strips and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and slightly crispy. Set aside. In the same skillet, add the remaining 1 tbsp of olive oil and the rinsed black beans, along with the remaining cumin, chili powder, and smoked paprika. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring, until heated through and seasoned. For the green tahini dressing, combine tahini, lemon juice, water, and fresh parsley in a small bowl. Whisk until smooth, adding more water if needed to reach desired consistency. Season with a pinch of salt. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa, roasted cauliflower, seasoned black beans, and seitan strips between two serving bowls. Drizzle generously with the green tahini dressing.

High-Protein Moroccan-Spiced Chickpea & Almond Tagine with Millet

🥜 31g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Millet, dry
  • 2 cup Water (for millet)
  • 2 whole Canned chickpeas, rinsed and drained
  • 0.5 cup Blanched almonds
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1.5 cup Vegetable broth
  • 1 whole Diced tomatoes, canned
  • 1 whole Zucchini, diced
  • 2 whole Carrots, sliced
  • 2 tsp Ras el hanout spice blend
  • 0.5 tsp Ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp Ground coriander
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
First, cook the millet: combine 1 cup dry millet with 2 cups water and 1/4 tsp salt in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat 2 tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add the diced zucchini and sliced carrots and sauté for 5-7 minutes until slightly tender. Stir in the tomato paste, ras el hanout, turmeric, and coriander, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the rinsed chickpeas, vegetable broth, and diced tomatoes to the pot. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for 15-20 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld and vegetables to soften. Stir in the blanched almonds for the last 5 minutes of cooking. Season with salt and pepper to taste. To serve, spoon the fluffy millet into bowls and ladle the hot Moroccan-spiced chickpea and almond tagine over the top. Garnish with fresh chopped cilantro.

High-Protein Savory Millet Bowl with Turmeric Tempeh and Sautéed Kale

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Millet, dry
  • 1.5 cup Vegetable broth
  • 227 g Tempeh, block
  • 4 cup Kale, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper, ground
  • 0.2 tsp Salt, to taste
Instructions
Rinse the millet thoroughly under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine the rinsed millet and vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the liquid is absorbed and the millet is tender. Fluff with a fork and set aside. While the millet cooks, crumble the tempeh into small pieces. In a large skillet, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium heat. Add the crumbled tempeh, turmeric powder, and garlic powder. Sauté for 7-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tempeh is lightly browned and crispy. Remove the tempeh from the skillet and set aside. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil to the same skillet. Add the chopped kale and sauté for 5-7 minutes, or until wilted and tender-crisp. Stir in the tamari and black pepper. Divide the cooked millet between two serving bowls. Top each bowl with half of the sautéed kale and half of the turmeric tempeh crumbles. Sprinkle each serving with 1 tablespoon of nutritional yeast and adjust salt to taste. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Mediterranean Seitan & Chickpea Bulgur Bowl with Roasted Red Peppers and Olives

🥜 46g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.5 cup Bulgur, medium grain
  • 1 cup Vegetable broth
  • 224 g Seitan, pre-made, sliced
  • 1 whole Chickpeas, canned, rinsed and drained
  • 0.5 cup Roasted red peppers, jarred, drained and sliced
  • 0.2 cup Kalamata olives, pitted and halved
  • 0.5 cup Cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 0.2 cup Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice, fresh
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt, to taste
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper, ground
Instructions
In a small saucepan, bring the vegetable broth to a boil. Add the bulgur, reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 10-12 minutes, or until the liquid is absorbed and the bulgur is tender. Remove from heat and let sit, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. While the bulgur cooks, prepare the seitan. In a large skillet, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the sliced seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and slightly crispy. Sprinkle with garlic powder, salt, and pepper during the last minute of cooking. In a large bowl, combine the cooked bulgur, browned seitan, rinsed chickpeas, roasted red peppers, Kalamata olives, diced cucumber, and halved cherry tomatoes. Add the fresh parsley. In a small bowl, whisk together the lemon juice, remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper to create the dressing. Pour the dressing over the bowl ingredients and toss gently to combine. Divide evenly into two serving bowls and serve.

High-Protein Hearty Lentil & Seitan Stew with Root Vegetables and Barley

🥜 51g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.5 cup Green lentils, dry
  • 0.2 cup Pearled barley, dry
  • 170 g Seitan, pre-made, cubed
  • 1 cup Carrots, chopped
  • 0.5 cup Celery, chopped
  • 4 cup Vegetable broth
  • 1 whole Diced tomatoes, canned
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1 whole Bay leaf
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish
  • 0.5 tsp Salt, to taste
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper, ground, to taste
Instructions
Rinse the green lentils and pearled barley thoroughly. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the chopped carrots and celery and sauté for 5-7 minutes, until slightly softened. Stir in the tomato paste, dried thyme, and smoked paprika. Cook for 1 minute, stirring continuously, to toast the spices and deepen the flavor. Add the rinsed lentils, barley, cubed seitan, diced tomatoes (undrained), vegetable broth, and bay leaf to the pot. Bring the stew to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 35-45 minutes, or until the lentils and barley are tender. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking. Remove the bay leaf before serving. Season the stew with salt and black pepper to taste. Ladle the hot stew into two serving bowls and garnish with fresh chopped parsley. Serve immediately.

Ingredients
  • 1.5 cup Shelled edamame, frozen
  • 0.8 cup Pepitas (pumpkin seeds)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.8 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Dried rosemary
  • 0.2 tsp Sea salt
  • 0.1 tsp Black pepper, ground

High-Protein Black Bean & Corn Grits Bowl with Chipotle Tempeh

🥜 38.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 160 g Corn grits (quick-cooking)
  • 2.5 cup Vegetable broth
  • 1.5 cup Canned black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 cup Frozen corn kernels
  • 200 g Tempeh
  • 1 tsp Chipotle powder
  • 0.5 tsp Ground cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1 whole Avocado, diced
  • 1 tsp Salt and black pepper
Instructions
First, prepare the grits: In a medium saucepan, bring 2.5 cups of vegetable broth to a boil. Slowly whisk in the corn grits, reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thick and creamy. Stir in 1 tbsp of nutritional yeast and season with salt and pepper to taste. Next, prepare the chipotle tempeh: Crumble the tempeh into small pieces using your hands or a fork. Heat 1 tbsp of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tempeh, chipotle powder, cumin, smoked paprika, and garlic powder. Cook, stirring frequently, for 8-10 minutes until the tempeh is golden brown and slightly crispy. While the tempeh cooks, prepare the black bean and corn mixture: In a separate small skillet, heat the remaining 1 tbsp of olive oil. Add the rinsed black beans and frozen corn. Cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until heated through. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked grits between two serving bowls. Top each with half of the chipotle tempeh, followed by half of the black bean and corn mixture. Garnish generously with fresh cilantro and diced avocado before serving.

High-Protein Kung Pao Inspired Seitan & Peanut Brown Rice Bowl with Edamame

🥜 55.0g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 190 g Brown rice (dry)
  • 3.8 cup Water
  • 250 g Seitan, sliced into strips
  • 1.5 cup Frozen shelled edamame
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper, cored and chopped
  • 1 whole Green bell pepper, cored and chopped
  • 4 tbsp Roasted peanuts, unsalted
  • 4 tbsp Low-sodium tamari or soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Sriracha (optional, for heat)
  • 1 tbsp Cornstarch
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Ground ginger
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Vegetable oil
Instructions
Begin by cooking the brown rice: In a medium saucepan, combine the brown rice with 3.75 cups of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 40-45 minutes, or until all water is absorbed and rice is tender. While the rice cooks, prepare the Kung Pao sauce: In a small bowl, whisk together tamari, rice vinegar, maple syrup, sriracha (if using), cornstarch, garlic powder, and ground ginger until smooth. Set aside. Next, heat vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add the seitan strips and bell peppers, stir-frying for 5-7 minutes until the seitan is lightly browned and peppers are tender-crisp. Add the frozen edamame to the skillet and cook for another 2-3 minutes, until heated through. Give the sauce another quick whisk and pour it over the seitan and vegetables in the skillet. Cook, stirring continuously, for 1-2 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats all ingredients. Stir in the sesame oil. To serve, divide the cooked brown rice between two bowls. Top with the Kung Pao seitan and vegetable mixture, and sprinkle generously with roasted peanuts.

High-Protein Creamy White Bean & Roasted Red Pepper Sauce Pasta with Herbed Seitan Crumble

🥜 48.0g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Whole wheat pasta
  • 2 whole Canned cannellini beans, rinsed and drained
  • 340 g Jarred roasted red peppers, drained
  • 3 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1.5 cup Unsweetened plant milk (e.g., soy or oat)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Dried basil
  • 0.5 tsp Dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 150 g Seitan, crumbled
  • 0.2 cup Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp Salt and black pepper
Instructions
First, bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the whole wheat pasta and cook according to package instructions until al dente. Drain well, reserving about 1/2 cup of pasta water. While the pasta cooks, prepare the seitan crumble: Heat 1 tbsp of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the crumbled seitan, dried basil, and dried oregano. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 8-10 minutes until the seitan is lightly browned and slightly crispy. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper, then set aside. Next, prepare the creamy sauce: In a high-speed blender, combine the rinsed cannellini beans, drained roasted red peppers, nutritional yeast, plant milk, garlic powder, and the remaining 1 tbsp of olive oil. Blend until completely smooth and creamy. If the sauce is too thick, add a tablespoon or two of the reserved pasta water until it reaches your desired consistency. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper as needed. Pour the creamy sauce into the skillet with the cooked seitan crumble. Add the drained pasta and toss everything together to coat thoroughly. If needed, add a splash of reserved pasta water to loosen the sauce. Serve immediately, garnished with fresh chopped parsley.

High-Protein Savory Tempeh Hash with Sweet Potatoes and Spinach

🥜 35g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Tempeh
  • 400 g Sweet Potato
  • 240 g Canned Black Beans, drained and rinsed
  • 200 g Fresh Spinach
  • 3 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 1.5 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Dried Thyme
  • 1.5 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
First, peel the sweet potatoes and cut them into small, uniform cubes, about 1/2 inch in size. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the sweet potato cubes and cook for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender and lightly browned. While the sweet potatoes are cooking, crumble the tempeh into small pieces with your hands or a fork. In a separate bowl, toss the crumbled tempeh with tamari, smoked paprika, cumin powder, garlic powder, dried thyme, salt, and black pepper. Once the sweet potatoes are tender, push them to one side of the skillet. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil to the empty side, then add the seasoned tempeh. Cook the tempeh for 8-10 minutes, stirring frequently, until it's golden brown and slightly crispy. Finally, add the drained and rinsed black beans and fresh spinach to the skillet. Stir everything together and cook for 2-3 minutes, until the spinach has wilted. Stir in the nutritional yeast just before serving. Divide evenly into two bowls.
