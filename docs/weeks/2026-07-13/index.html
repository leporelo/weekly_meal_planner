High-Protein Savory Spiced Tofu Scramble with Amaranth and Spinach

🥜 78g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Amaranth
  • 2.5 cup Water
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 4 cup Spinach
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.5 tsp Kala Namak (black salt)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
Instructions
1. Rinse amaranth thoroughly under cold water. Combine 1 cup amaranth with 2.5 cups water in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until all the water is absorbed and the amaranth is fluffy. Let it stand, covered, for 5 minutes off the heat, then fluff with a fork. 2. While the amaranth cooks, press the extra-firm tofu to remove any excess water. Crumble the tofu into a bowl using your hands or a fork until it resembles a coarse texture. 3. In a small separate bowl, mix together the nutritional yeast, turmeric, cumin, garlic powder, black pepper, and kala namak to create your spice blend. 4. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tofu and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it starts to brown lightly and some moisture has evaporated. 5. Sprinkle the prepared spice mixture over the tofu in the skillet and stir well to coat every piece evenly. Continue to cook for another 2 minutes, allowing the spices to become fragrant. 6. Add the fresh spinach to the skillet with the tofu. Cook, stirring frequently, until the spinach wilts down completely, which usually takes about 2-3 minutes. 7. Divide the cooked amaranth evenly between two serving bowls. Top each bowl with half of the savory tofu scramble. Serve immediately while warm, or allow to cool for meal prepping and refrigerate.

High-Protein Mediterranean Chickpea & Quinoa Bowl with Marinated Artichoke Hearts and Cucumber

🥜 32.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 15 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Quinoa
  • 1.5 cup Water
  • 2 cup Cooked Chickpeas
  • 0.5 cup Marinated Artichoke Hearts
  • 1 whole Cucumber
  • 1 cup Cherry Tomatoes
  • 0.2 cup Kalamata Olives
  • 0.2 cup Fresh Parsley
  • 2 tbsp Hemp Seeds
  • 1 whole Lemon
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Dried Oregano
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Rinse quinoa thoroughly under cold water. Combine 0.75 cup quinoa and 1.5 cups water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all the water is absorbed. Remove from heat, let stand, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 2. High-Protein Seitan & Lentil Shepherd's Pie with Sweet Potato Topping

🥜 96.5g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 whole Sweet Potatoes
  • 0.5 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 200 g Seitan
  • 1 cup Brown or Green Lentils
  • 1 cup Carrots
  • 0.5 cup Celery
  • 2 whole Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Tomato Paste
  • 1 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 1 tsp Dried Thyme
  • 0.5 tsp Dried Rosemary
  • 2 tbsp Soy Sauce
  • 0.5 cup Frozen Peas
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Prepare the sweet potato topping: Peel and cube the sweet potatoes. Place them in a pot and cover with water. Bring to a boil and cook until they are fork-tender, which usually takes about 15-20 minutes. Drain the sweet potatoes thoroughly, then return them to the empty pot. 2. Mash the cooked sweet potatoes with 0.5 cup vegetable broth, 2 tablespoons nutritional yeast, and 1 teaspoon smoked paprika until the mixture is smooth and creamy. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. Set aside. 3. While the sweet potatoes are cooking, prepare the filling: Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in an oven-safe skillet (or a large pot that can be transferred to an oven-safe baking dish) over medium heat. Add the diced carrots and celery, and cook for about 5 minutes until they begin to soften. Mince the garlic cloves, then add the minced garlic to the skillet and cook for another minute until fragrant. 4. Stir in the chopped seitan and cook for 3-5 minutes, allowing it to brown lightly. Next, add the tomato paste, dried thyme, dried rosemary, and soy sauce (or tamari for a gluten-free option), stirring well to combine all ingredients and coat the seitan and vegetables. 5. Pour in 1 cup of vegetable broth and bring the mixture to a gentle simmer. Add the cooked and drained lentils along with the frozen peas, stirring everything together. Continue to simmer for 5-7 minutes, allowing the sauce to thicken slightly. Season the filling with salt and black pepper to your preference. 6. Carefully spoon the prepared sweet potato topping over the seitan and lentil filling, spreading it evenly to completely cover the surface. You can use a fork to create decorative swirls on the topping if desired. 7. Bake in the preheated oven for 20-25 minutes, or until the sweet potato topping is lightly golden brown and the filling is bubbling hot. Allow the Shepherd's Pie to cool for a few minutes before serving. This dish is perfect for sharing and leftovers can be stored in the refrigerator.

High-Protein Savory Chickpea & Kale Scramble with Toasted Quinoa

🥜 33.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Firm tofu (pressed)
  • 240 g Canned chickpeas (drained and rinsed)
  • 60 g Quinoa (dry)
  • 4 cup Kale (chopped, stems removed)
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper (diced)
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (Kala Namak)
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
First, rinse the quinoa thoroughly under cold water, then combine it with 120ml of water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside. While the quinoa cooks, press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15 minutes to remove excess water, then crumble it into small pieces using your hands or a fork. Drain and rinse the canned chickpeas, then lightly mash about half of them with a fork, leaving the other half whole for texture. Dice the red bell pepper and chop the kale, ensuring stems are removed. Heat the olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the diced red bell pepper and sauté for 5-7 minutes until softened. Add the crumbled tofu and mashed/whole chickpeas to the skillet, breaking up any large tofu clumps with your spoon. Sprinkle in the nutritional yeast, garlic powder, turmeric powder, black salt, salt, and black pepper, stirring well to coat everything. Cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tofu begins to lightly brown and dry out, mimicking a scrambled egg texture. Stir in the chopped kale and cook for another 3-5 minutes, or until the kale has wilted significantly. Finally, fold in the cooked quinoa, ensuring it's evenly distributed throughout the scramble. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed. Serve immediately, divided between two bowls.

High-Protein Crispy Tofu & Black Rice Bowl with Roasted Asparagus and Spicy Almond Butter Sauce

🥜 36g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu (pressed)
  • 1 cup Black rice (dry)
  • 300 g Asparagus (woody ends trimmed)
  • 3 tbsp Soy sauce (or tamari for GF)
  • 2 tbsp Cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Sesame seeds (for garnish)
  • 4 tbsp Creamy almond butter
  • 4 tbsp Water
  • 2 tbsp Lime juice
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1.5 tsp Chili garlic sauce (or sriracha)
  • 1 tsp Grated ginger
  • 1 tsp Salt and black pepper
Instructions
Begin by cooking the black rice. Rinse the black rice thoroughly, then combine 1 cup dry rice with 2 cups (480ml) water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 30-40 minutes until water is absorbed and rice is tender. Once cooked, let it sit covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. Meanwhile, preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). After pressing the extra-firm tofu, cut it into 1-inch cubes. In a medium bowl, gently toss the tofu cubes with 1 tbsp olive oil, 2 tbsp soy sauce, and 2 tbsp cornstarch until evenly coated. This will help them get crispy. Spread the coated tofu in a single layer on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. On a separate section of the baking sheet, arrange the trimmed asparagus spears. Drizzle the asparagus with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp soy sauce, and a pinch of salt and pepper. Roast both the tofu and asparagus for 20-25 minutes, flipping the tofu halfway through, until the tofu is golden brown and crispy, and the asparagus is tender-crisp. While the tofu and asparagus roast, prepare the spicy almond butter sauce. In a small bowl, whisk together the creamy almond butter, water, lime juice, maple syrup, chili garlic sauce, and grated ginger until smooth and creamy. If the sauce is too thick, add a little more water, 1 tsp at a time, until it reaches your desired consistency. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked black rice between two serving bowls. Top each with crispy tofu cubes and roasted asparagus. Drizzle generously with the spicy almond butter sauce and garnish with sesame seeds before serving.

High-Protein Lentil & Mushroom Stroganoff with Farro

🥜 24.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Brown or green lentils (dry)
  • 1 cup Farro (dry)
  • 400 g Cremini mushrooms (sliced)
  • 2 cup Vegetable broth
  • 120 g Vegan sour cream
  • 3 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Dried thyme
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley (chopped, for garnish)
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
First, prepare the farro and lentils. For the farro, rinse 1 cup dry farro and cook it according to package directions, typically simmering in 2 cups of water or vegetable broth for about 20-30 minutes until tender and chewy. Drain any excess liquid. For the lentils, rinse 1 cup dry lentils and combine them with 3 cups (720ml) fresh water in a separate pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 20-25 minutes until tender. Drain and set aside. While the farro and lentils cook, heat the olive oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the sliced cremini mushrooms and a pinch of salt. Sauté the mushrooms for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they have released their liquid and are nicely browned. Next, stir in the smoked paprika and dried thyme, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant. Pour in the vegetable broth and bring to a gentle simmer. Reduce the heat to low, then stir in the cooked lentils, vegan sour cream, nutritional yeast, and Dijon mustard. Mix well to combine everything into a creamy sauce. Season with additional salt and black pepper to taste. Let it gently simmer for 5-7 minutes to allow the flavors to meld and the sauce to thicken slightly. Serve the hot lentil and mushroom stroganoff over the cooked farro, divided into two bowls, and garnish generously with fresh chopped parsley.
