High-Protein Tofu Scramble with Spinach & Bell Peppers

🥜 29g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 15 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 340 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 0.5 cup Nutritional yeast
  • 2 cup Spinach
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (kala namak)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15 minutes to remove excess water. Crumble it into a bowl using your hands or a fork. 2. Dice the red bell pepper into small pieces. Wash and chop the spinach if using fresh. 3. In a large non-stick pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the diced bell pepper and sauté for 3-5 minutes until slightly softened. 4. Add the crumbled tofu to the pan. Sprinkle with garlic powder, turmeric powder, black salt, and black pepper. Stir well to coat the tofu evenly with the spices, mimicking the color of scrambled eggs. 5. Continue to cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tofu is heated through and slightly browned. 6. Stir in the nutritional yeast and spinach. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, until the spinach has wilted. 7. Divide the scramble evenly into two servings and serve warm. This scramble can be prepped in advance and reheated.

Mediterranean Quinoa Power Bowl with Chickpeas & Tahini Dressing

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Quinoa, dry
  • 1.5 cup Water
  • 400 g Chickpeas, canned (drained and rinsed)
  • 1 whole Cucumber
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes
  • 1 whole Green bell pepper
  • 0.2 cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  • 0.2 cup Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 0.2 cup Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 3 tbsp Water (for dressing)
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
  • 0.1 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Rinse the quinoa thoroughly under cold water. Combine quinoa and 1.5 cups of water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed and quinoa is fluffy. Let it sit, covered, for 5 minutes after cooking, then fluff with a fork. 2. While quinoa cooks, prepare the vegetables. Dice the cucumber and green bell pepper. Halve the cherry tomatoes. Chop the fresh parsley. Drain and rinse the canned chickpeas. Pit and slice the Kalamata olives. 3. For the tahini dressing, whisk together tahini, lemon juice, 3 tablespoons of water, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper in a small bowl until smooth and creamy. Add more water if needed to reach desired consistency. 4. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa evenly between two large bowls. Top each with a generous portion of chickpeas, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, green bell pepper, Kalamata olives, and fresh parsley. 5. Drizzle each bowl generously with the tahini dressing. These bowls are excellent for meal prep; store ingredients separately and combine just before serving.

Spicy Seitan & Edamame Stir-fry with Brown Rice

🥜 39g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 250 g Seitan, pre-made (e.g., in strips or chunks)
  • 0.8 cup Brown rice, dry
  • 1.5 cup Water (for rice)
  • 1.5 cup Shelled edamame (frozen)
  • 2 cup Broccoli florets
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 4 tbsp Soy sauce (or tamari for GF)
  • 1 tbsp Sriracha
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tsp Cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp Water (for cornstarch slurry)
Instructions
1. Cook the brown rice: Combine 0.75 cups dry brown rice with 1.5 cups water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 35-40 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Let it sit, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 2. While the rice cooks, prepare the stir-fry sauce: In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, sriracha, rice vinegar, grated ginger, and garlic powder. In another small bowl, create a cornstarch slurry by whisking cornstarch with 1 tablespoon of water. 3. Slice the red bell pepper into thin strips. If using frozen edamame, thaw it by rinsing under hot water. Cut the seitan into bite-sized pieces if not already. 4. Heat the sesame oil in a large wok or a deep skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seitan and stir-fry for 5-7 minutes until lightly browned. 5. Add the broccoli florets and bell pepper strips to the wok. Stir-fry for 5-7 minutes, until vegetables are crisp-tender. Add the shelled edamame and stir-fry for another 2 minutes. 6. Pour the prepared stir-fry sauce over the vegetables and seitan. Bring to a simmer, then stir in the cornstarch slurry. Cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens. 7. Divide the cooked brown rice between two plates, then top with the spicy seitan and edamame stir-fry. Serve immediately.

Spicy Roasted Chickpeas & Pumpkin Seeds

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 800 g Chickpeas, canned (drained and rinsed)
  • 1 cup Pumpkin seeds
  • 1.5 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Cayenne pepper
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. Thoroughly drain and rinse the canned chickpeas. Pat them very dry with a paper towel. The drier they are, the crispier they will get. 3. In a medium bowl, combine the dried chickpeas and pumpkin seeds. Drizzle with olive oil and toss to coat evenly. 4. Add the smoked paprika, garlic powder, cayenne pepper, and salt to the bowl. Toss again until the chickpeas and pumpkin seeds are well-coated with the spices. 5. Spread the seasoned chickpeas and pumpkin seeds in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. 6. Roast for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the chickpeas are golden brown and crispy. Keep an eye on them to prevent burning. 7. Remove from oven and let cool slightly before serving. These can be stored in an airtight container for up to 5 days, maintaining their crunch.

High-Protein Tempeh Breakfast Hash

🥜 25g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 224 g Tempeh
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 2 cup Baby spinach
  • 300 g Baby potatoes
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Hemp seeds
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce (low sodium)
Instructions
1. Dice the baby potatoes into 1-inch cubes. Place them in a pot, cover with water, bring to a boil, and cook for about 10-12 minutes until fork-tender. Drain and set aside. 2. Crumble the tempeh into small pieces using your hands or a fork. Dice the red bell pepper. 3. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet or cast-iron pan over medium-high heat. Add the crumbled tempeh and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned and slightly crispy. 4. Add the diced bell pepper and cooked potatoes to the skillet. Continue to cook for another 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the bell pepper softens and the potatoes start to crisp up. 5. Stir in the nutritional yeast, smoked paprika, garlic powder, black pepper, and soy sauce. Cook for 1-2 minutes, ensuring everything is well coated. 6. Finally, add the baby spinach to the skillet and cook until wilted, about 2-3 minutes. 7. Divide the hash evenly into two servings. Garnish each serving with 1 tablespoon of hemp seeds before serving.

David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients

  • 2 cup Canned black beans, rinsed and drained

  • 1 whole Red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 whole Avocado, diced
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1 whole Lime, juiced
  • 3 tbsp Sunflower seeds
  • 0.5 cup Salsa
Instructions

Divide the mixture into two serving bowls. Sprinkle 1.5 tablespoons of sunflower seeds over each bowl before serving. This bowl can be prepped in advance and stored in airtight containers in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, adding avocado just before serving to prevent browning.

Thai-Inspired Seitan & Vegetable Noodle Bowl

🥜 39g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 224 g Seitan, sliced or cubed
  • 150 g Rice noodles (vermicelli or flat)
  • 4 cup Kale, stems removed, chopped
  • 2 whole Carrots, julienned
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 4 tbsp Peanut butter (creamy, unsweetened)
  • 3 tbsp Low sodium soy sauce
  • 1 whole Lime, juiced
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp Chili garlic sauce (optional, adjust to taste)
  • 4 tbsp Water
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
Instructions
1. Prepare the rice noodles according to package instructions. Usually, this involves soaking in hot water for a few minutes or boiling briefly until tender. Drain well and set aside. 2. In a small bowl, whisk together the peanut butter, soy sauce, lime juice, grated ginger, chili garlic sauce (if using), and water until a smooth sauce forms. If it's too thick, add a little more water, 1 tablespoon at a time, until it reaches your desired consistency. 3. Heat the sesame oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add the sliced seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and slightly crispy. Remove seitan from the pan and set aside. 4. Add the julienned carrots and sliced red bell pepper to the same skillet. Sauté for 3-5 minutes until they start to soften but still retain some crunch. 5. Add the chopped kale to the skillet and cook for another 2-3 minutes, stirring, until the kale is wilted. 6. Return the seitan to the pan with the vegetables. Add the cooked and drained rice noodles. Pour the peanut-lime sauce over everything and toss gently to coat all ingredients evenly. 7. Divide the noodle mixture into two large bowls and serve hot. This meal is also excellent for meal prepping; store components separately and combine just before reheating or serving cold.

Steamed Edamame Pods with Sea Salt

🥜 20g protein/serving 🔪 2 min prep 🔥 5 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2.5 cup Frozen shelled edamame
  • 0.5 tsp Sea salt
Instructions
1. Place the frozen shelled edamame in a microwave-safe bowl. 2. Add about 2 tablespoons of water to the bowl. 3. Cover the bowl with a microwave-safe lid or plate, leaving a small vent. 4. High-Protein Savory Chickpea & Spinach Pancakes

🥜 25g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 15 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 100 g Chickpea Flour
  • 240 ml Water
  • 10 g Nutritional Yeast
  • 3 g Garlic Powder
  • 1.5 g Turmeric Powder
  • 3 g Salt
  • 0.5 g Black Pepper
  • 60 g Fresh Spinach
  • 75 g Bell Pepper (any color)
  • 30 ml Olive Oil
  • 25 g Vegan Feta Crumbles (optional)
Instructions
1. In a large bowl, whisk together chickpea flour, water, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, turmeric powder, salt, and black pepper until a smooth batter forms. Ensure there are no lumps. 2. Finely dice the bell pepper and roughly chop the fresh spinach. Stir these vegetables into the chickpea batter until evenly distributed. 3. Heat 1 tbsp of olive oil in a large non-stick pan over medium heat. Pour half of the batter into the pan, spreading it into a round pancake shape. Cook for 4-5 minutes per side, or until golden brown and cooked through. 4. Remove the first pancake and repeat the process with the remaining batter and 1 tbsp of olive oil. 5. Serve the warm pancakes immediately, topped with vegan feta crumbles if desired. These are delicious on their own or with a side of sugar-free hot sauce.

Hearty Lentil & Walnut Crumble Bowl with Roasted Sweet Potatoes & Kale

🥜 28g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 190 g Cooked Brown Lentils
  • 50 g Walnuts
  • 20 g Panko Breadcrumbs (vegan)
  • 30 ml Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 3 g Smoked Paprika
  • 1 g Dried Thyme
  • 3 g Garlic Powder
  • 10 g Nutritional Yeast
  • 30 ml Olive Oil
  • 300 g Sweet Potato
  • 120 g Kale
  • 185 g Cooked Quinoa
  • 3 g Salt
  • 0.5 g Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Peel and dice the sweet potato into 1-inch cubes. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and lightly caramelized. 2. While sweet potatoes roast, prepare the lentil-walnut crumble. In a food processor, pulse walnuts until finely chopped (do not over-process into butter). Transfer to a bowl. 3. Add cooked lentils, panko breadcrumbs, tamari, smoked paprika, dried thyme, garlic powder, and nutritional yeast to the bowl with walnuts. Mix well until combined and crumbly. 4. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the lentil-walnut mixture and cook, stirring occasionally, for 8-10 minutes until lightly browned and crispy. 5. During the last 5 minutes of sweet potato roasting, add the chopped kale to the baking sheet and toss with the sweet potatoes. Allow the kale to wilt slightly. 6. Assemble bowls: Divide the cooked quinoa, roasted sweet potatoes, wilted kale, and lentil-walnut crumble evenly between two bowls. Serve warm.

Lemon-Caper Seitan Piccata with Creamy Polenta

🥜 35g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Seitan
  • 30 g All-Purpose Flour
  • 240 ml Vegetable Broth (no onion)
  • 60 ml Lemon Juice
  • 30 g Capers
  • 28 g Vegan Butter
  • 8 g Fresh Parsley
  • 3 g Garlic Powder
  • 3 g Salt
  • 0.5 g Black Pepper
  • 15 ml Olive Oil
  • 150 g Instant Polenta
  • 720 ml Water (for polenta)
  • 120 ml Unsweetened Plant Milk
  • 10 g Nutritional Yeast (for polenta)
  • 3 g Salt (for polenta)
Instructions
1. Slice seitan into thin cutlets. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder, then dredge each piece lightly in all-purpose flour, shaking off excess. 2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seitan cutlets and cook for 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown. Remove seitan from the skillet and set aside. 3. In the same skillet, melt 1 tbsp of vegan butter. Add vegetable broth, lemon juice, and capers. Bring to a simmer, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Continue to simmer for 3-5 minutes until the sauce slightly thickens. Stir in the chopped fresh parsley and remaining 1 tbsp vegan butter until melted and emulsified. 4. While the sauce simmers, prepare the polenta. Bring 3 cups of water and the plant milk to a boil in a medium saucepan. Slowly whisk in the instant polenta, stirring constantly to prevent lumps. Reduce heat to low and cook for 5-7 minutes, continuing to stir, until creamy and thick. Stir in nutritional yeast and polenta salt. 5. Return the seitan cutlets to the skillet with the piccata sauce, tossing gently to coat. Serve the seitan piccata immediately over the creamy polenta.

High-Protein Savory Tofu & Black Bean Breakfast Burritos

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 350 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 1 cup Black beans, canned, rinsed
  • 1 whole Bell pepper (any color), diced
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.2 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 4 whole Large whole wheat tortillas
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
  • 1 whole Avocado, sliced (for serving)
Instructions
First, press the extra-firm tofu for at least 30 minutes to remove excess water. After pressing, crumble the tofu into small, scramble-like pieces. High-Protein Red Lentil Dahl with Quinoa

🥜 35g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Red lentils, rinsed
  • 4 cup Vegetable broth
  • 1 cup Crushed tomatoes, canned
  • 0.5 cup Coconut milk, light
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Coriander powder
  • 0.5 tsp Mustard seeds (brown or black)
  • 3 cup Spinach, fresh
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.8 cup Quinoa, uncooked
  • 1.5 cup Water (for quinoa)

Instructions
First, prepare the quinoa: Rinse 0.75 cups of quinoa thoroughly.
