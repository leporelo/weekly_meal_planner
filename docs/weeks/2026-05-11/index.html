High-Protein Black Bean, Tofu & Brown Rice Bowl with Roasted Sweet Potatoes and Kale

🥜 36g protein/serving 🔪 25 min prep 🔥 50 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 1 cup Brown rice, uncooked
  • 2 cup Vegetable broth
  • 400 g Black beans, canned
  • 2 whole Sweet potatoes, medium
  • 4 cup Kale, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
Instructions
1. Press the tofu: Drain the block of tofu, wrap it in a clean kitchen towel, and place something heavy on top (like a cutting board with cans). Let it press for at least 20-30 minutes to remove excess water. Once pressed, crumble the tofu into small pieces. 2. Cook the brown rice: Rinse the brown rice thoroughly. In a medium saucepan, combine the brown rice with vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 40-45 minutes, or until all liquid is absorbed and rice is tender. Fluff with a fork and set aside. 3. Prepare sweet potatoes: Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Peel the sweet potatoes and cut them into 1-inch cubes. Toss with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 0.5 teaspoon smoked paprika, 0.5 teaspoon cumin, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway, until tender and slightly caramelized. 4. Sauté tofu and kale: While sweet potatoes roast, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the crumbled tofu, remaining 0.5 teaspoon smoked paprika, 0.5 teaspoon cumin, 1 teaspoon garlic powder, and nutritional yeast. Sauté for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tofu is golden brown and slightly crispy. Add the chopped kale and cook for another 3-5 minutes, until wilted. 5. Heat black beans: Drain and rinse the canned black beans. Add them to the skillet with the tofu and kale, stirring to combine and heat through for 2-3 minutes. 6. Assemble the bowls: Divide the cooked brown rice between two serving bowls. Top with the black bean and tofu mixture, roasted sweet potato cubes, and any desired additional seasonings.

High-Protein White Bean & Roasted Vegetable Grain Bowl with Lemon-Herb Dressing

🥜 28g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Quinoa, uncooked
  • 2 cup Water
  • 400 g Cannellini beans, canned
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 1 whole Yellow bell pepper
  • 1 whole Zucchini
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 tsp Dried basil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 4 tbsp Water (for dressing)
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Hemp seeds
Instructions
1. Cook the quinoa: Rinse quinoa thoroughly under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine rinsed quinoa and 2 cups of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed and quinoa is fluffy. Let it sit covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 2. Prepare and roast vegetables: Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Wash and chop the bell peppers and zucchini into 1-inch pieces. Halve the cherry tomatoes. On a large baking sheet, toss the chopped bell peppers, zucchini, and cherry tomatoes with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, dried oregano, dried basil, salt, and black pepper. Roast for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway, until vegetables are tender and slightly charred. 3. Prepare the lemon-herb dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together tahini, lemon juice, 4 tablespoons of water, and fresh parsley until smooth and creamy. Adjust consistency by adding more water if needed. 4. Assemble the bowls: Divide the cooked quinoa between two serving bowls. Drain and rinse the cannellini beans and add them to the bowls. Top with the roasted vegetables. Drizzle generously with the lemon-herb dressing and sprinkle with hemp seeds before serving.

Spicy Black Bean & Tempeh Stir-fry with Jasmine Rice

🥜 44g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Tempeh block
  • 1 cup Jasmine rice, uncooked
  • 2 cup Water
  • 400 g Black beans, canned
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 1 whole Green bell pepper
  • 2 cup Broccoli florets
  • 4 tbsp Soy sauce or tamari
  • 2 tbsp Peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp Sriracha
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp Vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
Instructions
1. Cook the jasmine rice: Rinse the jasmine rice thoroughly. In a medium saucepan, combine the rice with 2 cups of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside. 2. Prepare tempeh: Slice the tempeh block into thin strips or crumble it. Steam the tempeh for 10 minutes to remove bitterness and soften it. Drain well and pat dry. 3. Prepare the stir-fry sauce: In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce (or tamari), peanut butter, maple syrup, sriracha, grated ginger, and minced garlic until smooth. 4. Stir-fry vegetables and tempeh: Heat vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tempeh and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown. Add the chopped bell peppers and broccoli florets. Stir-fry for 5-7 minutes, until vegetables are crisp-tender. 5. Combine and serve: Drain and rinse the black beans. Add the black beans and the prepared stir-fry sauce to the wok. Cook for another 3-5 minutes, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens and coats all ingredients. Serve the stir-fry hot over the jasmine rice, garnished with sesame seeds.

High-Protein Tofu & Spinach Scramble with Toasted Quinoa and Hemp Seeds

🥜 37g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 0.5 cup Quinoa, dry
  • 1 cup Water (for quinoa)
  • 4 cup Fresh spinach
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (kala namak)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 4 tbsp Hemp seeds
Instructions
1. Rinse quinoa thoroughly. Combine 0.5 cup dry quinoa with 1 cup water in a small pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside. 2. While quinoa cooks, press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15 minutes to remove excess water. Crumble the pressed tofu into a bowl. 3. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick pan or skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tofu and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it starts to brown slightly. 4. Stir in the nutritional yeast, turmeric powder, black salt, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and black pepper. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, stirring constantly, to coat the tofu evenly and allow the spices to bloom. 5. Add the fresh spinach to the pan. Cook, stirring, until the spinach wilts down completely, about 3-4 minutes. 6. Stir in the cooked quinoa until well combined and heated through. 7. Divide the scramble evenly into two bowls. Sprinkle 2 tbsp of hemp seeds over each serving before enjoying.

Hearty Red Lentil & Vegetable Coconut Curry with Basmati Rice

🥜 45g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1.5 cup Red lentils, dry
  • 1 cup Basmati rice, dry
  • 800 g Canned diced tomatoes
  • 400 ml Full-fat coconut milk
  • 400 ml Vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 4 whole Garlic, minced
  • 3 tbsp Curry powder
  • 1.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1.5 tsp Cumin powder
  • 1.5 tsp Coriander powder
  • 1 whole Green bell pepper, diced
  • 1 whole Carrot, diced
  • 4 cup Fresh spinach
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Rinse the red lentils thoroughly under cold water. Set aside. 2. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add grated ginger and minced garlic, sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant (do not brown). 3. Stir in the curry powder, turmeric, cumin, and coriander. Cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly, allowing the spices to toast. 4. Add the diced bell pepper and carrot to the pot. Sauté for 5-7 minutes until the vegetables begin to soften. 5. Stir in the rinsed red lentils, canned diced tomatoes, full-fat coconut milk, and vegetable broth. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the lentils are tender and have absorbed much of the liquid. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking. 6. While the curry simmers, cook the basmati rice. Combine 1 cup dry basmati rice with 2 cups water in a separate pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 12-15 minutes until water is absorbed and rice is tender. Fluff with a fork. 7. Once lentils are tender, stir in the fresh spinach into the curry until it wilts. Season the curry with salt and black pepper to taste. 8. Divide the cooked basmati rice into two serving bowls. Ladle a generous portion of the lentil curry over the rice. Garnish with fresh chopped cilantro before serving.

Black Bean & Roasted Sweet Potato Bowl with Creamy Tahini-Lime Dressing and Quinoa

🥜 40g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Quinoa, dry
  • 2 cup Water (for quinoa)
  • 800 g Canned black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 whole Sweet potatoes, peeled and diced into 1-inch cubes
  • 4 cup Kale, destemmed and chopped
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 4 tbsp Lime juice
  • 4 tbsp Water (for dressing)
  • 2 tsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin powder (for dressing)
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. In a large bowl, toss the diced sweet potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp chili powder, and half of the salt and black pepper. Spread them in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. Roast for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway, until tender and slightly caramelized. 3. While sweet potatoes roast, cook the quinoa. Combine 1 cup dry quinoa with 2 cups water in a pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside. 4. In a small bowl, whisk together the tahini, lime juice, 4 tbsp water, maple syrup, garlic powder, and 0.5 tsp cumin powder until smooth and creamy. Add more water if needed to reach desired consistency. 5. In a large skillet, heat the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add the chopped kale and sauté for 5-7 minutes until tender and slightly wilted. 6. To assemble the bowls: Divide the cooked quinoa, roasted sweet potatoes, drained black beans, and sautéed kale evenly into two large serving bowls. 7. Drizzle generously with the creamy tahini-lime dressing. Serve warm.

Spicy Toasted Almonds & Pumpkin Seeds

🥜 32.5g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 12 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Raw almonds
  • 1 cup Raw pumpkin seeds (pepitas)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 160°C (325°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. In a medium bowl, combine the raw almonds and pumpkin seeds. Add olive oil, smoked paprika, chili powder, garlic powder, and salt. Toss thoroughly until the nuts and seeds are evenly coated with the spices. 3. Spread the seasoned almonds and pumpkin seeds in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. 4. Toast in the preheated oven for 10-12 minutes, stirring halfway through, until fragrant and lightly golden. Be careful not to burn them. 5. Remove from oven and let cool completely on the baking sheet. They will become crispier as they cool. Divide into two equal portions for snacking.

🥜 40g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 15 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed and crumbled
  • 240 g Cooked black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 whole Bell pepper, diced
  • 2 cup Fresh spinach
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (kala namak), optional for 'egg' flavor
  • 2 tsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper

Cook for another 2-3 minutes, stirring until the spinach wilts. Serve immediately. This scramble is great for meal prep and can be stored in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3-4 days.

Hearty Chickpea & Red Lentil Masala with Brown Rice

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 160 g Brown rice, uncooked
  • 160 g Red lentils, rinsed
  • 400 g Canned chickpeas, rinsed and drained
  • 400 g Diced tomatoes, canned
  • 200 ml Light coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1.5 tsp Garam masala
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Chili powder (mild)
  • 2 cup Fresh spinach
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Start by cooking the brown rice: Combine 160g brown rice with 380ml water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 25-30 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes. While rice cooks, prepare the masala. Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add grated ginger and minced garlic, sauté for 1 minute until fragrant. Stir in garam masala, turmeric, cumin, and chili powder; cook for another minute, stirring constantly, until aromatic. Pour in the diced tomatoes and light coconut milk, stirring to combine. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer. Add the rinsed red lentils and chickpeas, then cover and cook for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the lentils are tender. If the masala becomes too thick, you can add a splash of water or vegetable broth. Stir in the fresh spinach and cook until wilted, about 2 minutes. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. Serve the hearty chickpea and red lentil masala hot over the cooked brown rice, garnished with fresh cilantro. This dish reheats beautifully for meal prep.

High-Protein Peanut Tofu & Red Bean Stir-fry Bowl with Basmati Rice

🥜 50g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 160 g Basmati rice, uncooked
  • 300 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed, cubed, and pan-fried or baked
  • 240 g Cooked red beans, rinsed and drained
  • 2 whole Bell peppers, sliced
  • 2 cup Broccoli florets
  • 1 whole Carrots, thinly sliced
  • 4 tbsp Creamy peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp Tamari or soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp Sriracha (optional, for heat)
  • 2 tsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds, for garnish
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish
Instructions
First, cook the basmati rice: combine 160g basmati rice with 320ml water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes. While rice cooks, prepare the peanut sauce: In a small bowl, whisk together peanut butter, tamari, rice vinegar, maple syrup, grated ginger, minced garlic, and sriracha (if using). Add a splash of water (1-2 tbsp) if needed to achieve a pourable consistency. Heat sesame oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add the sliced bell peppers, broccoli florets, and thinly sliced carrots. Stir-fry for 5-7 minutes until vegetables are tender-crisp. Add the pre-cooked tofu cubes and cooked red beans to the skillet. Pour the peanut sauce over the vegetables, tofu, and beans, stirring gently to coat everything evenly. Cook for 2-3 minutes, allowing the sauce to thicken slightly and warm through. Serve the peanut tofu and red bean stir-fry immediately over the cooked basmati rice. Garnish with sesame seeds and fresh cilantro. This dish is perfect for meal prep, as it stores well in the refrigerator for several days.

High-Protein Tofu & Black Bean Breakfast Burritos

🥜 40g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Firm Tofu
  • 1 cup Black Beans, cooked
  • 2 whole High-Protein Tortillas (large)
  • 1 cup Bell Pepper (any color), diced
  • 2 cup Spinach
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.2 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 cup Salsa
Instructions
Press the tofu for at least 15 minutes to remove excess water, then crumble it into a bowl using your hands or a fork. In the bowl with crumbled tofu, add nutritional yeast, turmeric, cumin, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper; mix well to combine. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick pan over medium heat. Add the diced bell pepper and sauté for 3-5 minutes until slightly softened. Add the seasoned tofu crumble to the pan and cook for 7-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it starts to brown and resemble scrambled eggs. Stir in the cooked black beans and spinach, cooking until the spinach wilts, about 2-3 minutes. Warm the high-protein tortillas according to package instructions (microwave for 20 seconds or heat in a dry pan for 30 seconds per side). Divide the tofu and black bean mixture evenly between the two warmed tortillas. Top with salsa if desired, then fold the sides of the tortilla inward and roll tightly to form burritos. Serve immediately.

High-Protein White Bean Salad with Toasted Walnuts

🥜 28g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 5 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2.5 cup Great Northern or Cannellini Beans, cooked or canned rinsed/drained

  • 0.5 cup Red Bell Pepper, finely diced
  • 0.2 cup Fresh Parsley, chopped

  • 2 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 2 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 1 tsp Dijon Mustard
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper

Pour the dressing over the bean mixture and stir well to combine all ingredients thoroughly. Fold in the toasted walnuts. Serve chilled or at room temperature as a substantial high-protein snack.
