High-Protein Tofu & Black Bean Hash with Bell Peppers

🥜 29g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 350 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 170 g Cooked black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 whole Yellow bell pepper, diced
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 2 tbsp Low-sodium soy sauce (or tamari)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
Instructions
Press the extra-firm tofu for at least 20 minutes to remove excess water; use a tofu press or wrap in paper towels with a heavy object on top. While tofu is pressing, dice both bell peppers into small, uniform pieces and mince the garlic cloves. Once tofu is pressed, crumble it into a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the tofu is browned and crispy, about 8-10 minutes. Set the crispy tofu aside in a separate bowl. In the same skillet, add the olive oil. Sauté the diced bell peppers and minced garlic for 5-7 minutes until they are softened but still have a slight bite. Return the crumbled tofu to the skillet. Stir in the smoked paprika, cumin, turmeric, nutritional yeast, low-sodium soy sauce, salt, and black pepper, ensuring all ingredients are well combined. Add the rinsed and drained black beans to the mixture. Cook for another 3-5 minutes, stirring frequently, to allow the flavors to meld and the beans to heat through. Taste and adjust seasonings as needed. Divide the hash evenly between two serving bowls and garnish generously with fresh chopped cilantro before serving warm.

Spiced Lentil & Kale Power Bowl with Roasted Sweet Potato and Tahini Dressing

🥜 31g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Dry green or brown lentils
  • 0.8 cup Dry quinoa
  • 2 whole Medium sweet potato, peeled and diced (1-inch cubes)
  • 4 cup Kale, tough stems removed and chopped
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 4 tbsp Water (for dressing)
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.5 tsp Red pepper flakes (optional)
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Peel the sweet potatoes and dice them into 1-inch cubes. On a baking sheet, toss the diced sweet potato with 1 tbsp of olive oil, 0.5 tsp salt, and 0.25 tsp black pepper. Spread them in a single layer and roast for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway through, until they are tender and slightly caramelized. Meanwhile, cook the lentils according to package directions (typically 20-25 minutes until tender) and cook the quinoa according to package directions (typically 15 minutes, ensuring it's fluffy). While the grains and sweet potato are cooking, prepare the tahini dressing by whisking together tahini, fresh lemon juice, 4 tbsp water, 0.25 tsp salt, and 0.25 tsp black pepper in a small bowl until smooth and creamy. Adjust the consistency with more water if needed. In a large skillet, heat the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add the chopped kale (stems removed) and sauté for 3-5 minutes until wilted and vibrant green. Stir in the smoked paprika, cumin, garlic powder, and red pepper flakes (if using). To assemble, divide the cooked quinoa, cooked lentils, roasted sweet potato, and sautéed kale evenly between two large bowls. Drizzle generously with the prepared tahini dressing over each bowl. Serve warm or at room temperature.

Hearty Seitan & Broccoli Stir-fry with Brown Rice and Cashews

🥜 41g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 240 g Seitan, sliced or cubed
  • 1 cup Dry brown rice
  • 4 cup Broccoli florets
  • 1 whole Bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1 whole Medium carrot, julienned or thinly sliced
  • 3 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 4 tbsp Low-sodium soy sauce (or tamari)
  • 2 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp Water (for cornstarch slurry)
  • 0.5 cup Toasted cashews, chopped
  • 1 tbsp Toasted sesame oil
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Sesame seeds (for garnish)
Instructions
Begin by cooking the brown rice according to package directions, which typically takes 25-30 minutes; once cooked, keep warm. While the rice is cooking, prepare your stir-fry components: slice the seitan into bite-sized pieces, chop the broccoli into small florets, thinly slice the bell pepper, julienne or thinly slice the carrot, mince the garlic cloves, and grate the fresh ginger. In a small bowl, whisk together the low-sodium soy sauce, rice vinegar, maple syrup, and a cornstarch slurry (cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water) to create your stir-fry sauce. Heat the toasted sesame oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add the sliced or cubed seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes until it's lightly browned and slightly crispy; remove the seitan from the skillet and set aside. Add the broccoli florets, sliced bell pepper, and julienned carrot to the same skillet. Stir-fry for 5-7 minutes until the vegetables are crisp-tender. Add the minced garlic and grated ginger, stir-frying for another minute until fragrant. Return the seitan to the skillet with the vegetables. Pour the prepared stir-fry sauce over the seitan and vegetables, stirring constantly until the sauce thickens and coats all ingredients evenly, about 1-2 minutes. Stir in the chopped toasted cashews. Serve the hearty stir-fry immediately over the cooked brown rice, garnished with sesame seeds.

High-Protein Spicy Roasted Edamame with Hemp Seeds

🥜 23g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Frozen shelled edamame
  • 3 tbsp Hemp seeds
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). If using frozen edamame, thaw it first by rinsing under warm water in a colander for a minute, or by microwaving for a minute, then pat the edamame thoroughly dry with a clean kitchen towel or paper towels to ensure crispness during roasting. In a medium bowl, toss the thawed and dried edamame with olive oil, smoked paprika, chili powder, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper until all the edamame is evenly coated with the spices. Spread the seasoned edamame in a single layer on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Roast for 15-20 minutes, stirring the edamame halfway through, until it is lightly browned, slightly crispy, and heated through. Remove the edamame from the oven and immediately sprinkle the hemp seeds over it, tossing gently to combine. Allow to cool slightly before serving as a satisfying high-protein snack.

High-Protein Chickpea Flour Scramble with Spinach & Cherry Tomatoes

🥜 28.5g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 15 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1.5 cup Chickpea flour
  • 6 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Kala namak (black salt)
  • 0.2 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 1.5 cup Water
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 3 cup Baby spinach
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley (chopped)
Instructions
In a large bowl, whisk together the chickpea flour, nutritional yeast, kala namak, turmeric powder, and black pepper. Gradually add the water, whisking continuously until a smooth, lump-free batter is formed. The consistency should be similar to pancake batter. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the baby spinach and cherry tomatoes, cooking for 3-5 minutes until the spinach wilts and tomatoes soften slightly. Pour the chickpea flour batter into the skillet over the vegetables. Let it cook undisturbed for 2-3 minutes until the edges begin to set. Then, using a spatula, gently scramble the mixture, breaking it into pieces as it cooks, similar to scrambled eggs. Continue cooking for another 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the scramble is cooked through and lightly golden. Remove from heat, stir in fresh parsley, and serve immediately.

High-Protein Mediterranean Tofu & Quinoa Bowl

🥜 39.0g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Dried oregano
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 cup Dry quinoa
  • 2 cup Water (for quinoa)
  • 1 whole Canned chickpeas (drained and rinsed)
  • 0.5 whole Cucumber (diced)
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes (halved)
  • 0.5 cup Kalamata olives (pitted and halved)
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley (chopped)
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 4 tbsp Water (for dressing)
Instructions
First, press the tofu for at least 15 minutes to remove excess water, then cube it into 1-inch pieces. In a medium saucepan, combine the dry quinoa and 2 cups of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork. While quinoa cooks, preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). In a bowl, toss the cubed tofu with 1 tbsp olive oil, smoked paprika, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper. Spread on a baking sheet and bake for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and slightly crispy. Alternatively, pan-fry the tofu in a skillet. For the tahini dressing, whisk together tahini, lemon juice, 1 tbsp olive oil, and 4 tbsp water until smooth and creamy. Add more water if needed to reach desired consistency. In two large bowls, divide the cooked quinoa, baked tofu, drained chickpeas, diced cucumber, halved cherry tomatoes, and Kalamata olives. Drizzle generously with the tahini dressing and garnish with fresh parsley before serving.

High-Protein Black Bean & Corn Seitan Skillet with Roasted Sweet Potato Wedges

🥜 44.5g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 whole Sweet potatoes
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 250 g Seitan (pre-cooked, diced or crumbled)
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper (diced)
  • 1 whole Green bell pepper (diced)
  • 1 whole Canned black beans (drained and rinsed)
  • 1 cup Corn (frozen or fresh)
  • 400 g Diced tomatoes (canned, unsalted)
  • 0.5 cup Vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro (chopped, for garnish)
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Wash and scrub the sweet potatoes, then cut them into wedges. In a large bowl, toss the sweet potato wedges with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 0.5 tsp smoked paprika, 0.25 tsp salt, and a pinch of black pepper. Spread them in a single layer on a baking sheet and roast for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway, until tender and lightly browned. While the sweet potatoes roast, heat the remaining 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it's lightly browned and slightly crispy. Add the diced red and green bell peppers to the skillet and cook for another 5 minutes until they begin to soften. Stir in the drained black beans, corn, canned diced tomatoes, chili powder, cumin, remaining 0.5 tsp smoked paprika, and vegetable broth. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and cook for 10-15 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld and the sauce to thicken. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. Serve the seitan and black bean skillet hot, topped with fresh cilantro, alongside the roasted sweet potato wedges.

Savory Tofu & Kale Breakfast Bowl with Quinoa

🥜 47g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Quinoa, uncooked
  • 1.5 cup Water
  • 300 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 200 g Kale, destemmed and chopped
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (kala namak)
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 0.2 cup Unsweetened plant milk
Instructions
Rinse the quinoa thoroughly under cold water. Combine quinoa and 1.5 cups of water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside. While quinoa cooks, press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15 minutes to remove excess water. Crumble the pressed tofu into a medium bowl. Add nutritional yeast, garlic powder, turmeric, black salt, and black pepper. Mix gently to combine. Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tofu mixture and cook, stirring occasionally, for 8-10 minutes, or until the tofu is slightly browned and heated through. Add the chopped kale and cook for another 3-5 minutes, until wilted. Stir in the plant milk and halved cherry tomatoes, cooking for 1-2 minutes until warmed. Divide the cooked quinoa evenly into two bowls. Top each with the savory tofu scramble and serve immediately.

High-Protein Tempeh & Peanut Noodle Bowl

🥜 46g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 150 g Brown rice noodles
  • 226 g Tempeh
  • 200 g Shelled edamame (frozen)
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1 whole Carrots, julienned
  • 4 tbsp Peanut butter, unsweetened
  • 3 tbsp Tamari or soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp Lime juice
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Sesame seeds, for garnish
Instructions
Cook the brown rice noodles according to package directions. Drain and rinse with cold water to prevent sticking, then set aside. Steam or boil the shelled edamame until tender-crisp, about 3-5 minutes, then drain. While the noodles cook, prepare the peanut sauce. In a small bowl, whisk together peanut butter, tamari, rice vinegar, maple syrup, grated ginger, minced garlic, lime juice, and sesame oil until smooth. Add 1-2 tablespoons of hot water if needed to reach desired consistency. Crumble the tempeh into a large non-stick skillet. Cook over medium-high heat for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned and cooked through. If desired, add a splash of tamari during the last few minutes for extra flavor. In two large serving bowls, divide the cooked noodles, crumbled tempeh, steamed edamame, sliced red bell pepper, and julienned carrots. Drizzle generously with the peanut sauce. Garnish with sesame seeds before serving.

Seitan Gyros with Tahini Yogurt Sauce and Quinoa

🥜 46g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Seitan, pre-made
  • 0.5 cup Quinoa, uncooked
  • 1 cup Water
  • 1 cup Vegan plain unsweetened yogurt
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Dried dill
  • 0.5 cup Cucumber, diced
  • 0.5 cup Tomato, diced
  • 2 whole Whole wheat pita bread
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Dried oregano
  • 0.2 tsp Salt

Fold or roll up the pita to enjoy.

High-Protein Savory Roasted Almonds with Tamari and Nutritional Yeast

🥜 20g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 120 g Raw whole almonds
  • 2 tbsp Tamari or soy sauce
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
Instructions

High-Protein Savory Lentil & Oat Patties with Roasted Tomatoes

🥜 32g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Cooked brown or green lentils
  • 0.5 cup Rolled oats
  • 0.2 cup Nutritional yeast
  • 2 tbsp Ground flaxseed
  • 2 tbsp Tamari or soy sauce
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Dried thyme
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil (for patties)
  • 2 cup Cherry tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil (for tomatoes)
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley or chives (for garnish)
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. In a large bowl, combine the cooked lentils (lightly mashed), rolled oats, nutritional yeast, ground flaxseed, tamari, smoked paprika, dried thyme, salt, and black pepper. Mix thoroughly until well combined and the mixture can be formed into patties. If too dry, add a tablespoon of water; if too wet, add a little more oats. 3. Form the mixture into 4 equal-sized patties. 4. On the prepared baking sheet, toss the cherry tomatoes with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper. Spread them in a single layer. 5. Heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Carefully place the patties in the skillet and cook for 5-7 minutes per side, until golden brown and crispy. 6. While patties are cooking, roast the cherry tomatoes in the preheated oven for 15-20 minutes, until they start to burst and soften. 7. Serve 2 patties per person alongside the roasted cherry tomatoes. Garnish with fresh parsley or chives.

High-Protein Black Bean & Corn Stuffed Bell Peppers with Quinoa

🥜 26g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 whole Large bell peppers, halved and deseeded
  • 1.5 cup Cooked quinoa
  • 2 whole Canned black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 cup Corn kernels (fresh or frozen)
  • 0.5 cup Vegetable broth
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.2 cup Vegan shredded cheese (optional)
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly grease a baking dish large enough to hold the bell pepper halves. 2. In a large bowl, combine the cooked quinoa, rinsed black beans, corn kernels, vegetable broth, chopped cilantro, cumin, chili powder, salt, and black pepper. Mix well to ensure all ingredients are evenly distributed. 3. Place the bell pepper halves cut-side up in the prepared baking dish. Carefully spoon the black bean and quinoa filling into each pepper half, mounding it slightly. 4. If desired, sprinkle the vegan shredded cheese over the top of each stuffed pepper. 5. Pour a small amount of water (about 1/4 cup) into the bottom of the baking dish to create steam, which helps soften the peppers. 6. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until the bell peppers are tender and the filling is heated through and lightly browned on top. Serve warm.

High-Protein White Bean & Herb Dip with Seed Crackers

🥜 22.5g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 whole Canned cannellini beans, rinsed and drained
  • 2 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 0.2 cup Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 0.2 cup Fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 whole Garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 2 tbsp Water
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
  • 0.1 tsp Black pepper
  • 12 whole Large seed crackers
Instructions
1. In a food processor or high-speed blender, combine the rinsed and drained cannellini beans, tahini, lemon juice, chopped fresh parsley, chopped fresh dill, minced garlic, nutritional yeast, water, salt, and black pepper. 2. This dip can be prepared in advance and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3-4 days.

High-Protein Buckwheat Groats with Spiced Lentils & Greens

🥜 22g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook

  • 450 g Tempeh

  • 2 cup Water

  • 2 whole Garlic (minced)
  • 1 tbsp Ginger (grated)
  • 4 cup Kale (stems removed, chopped)
  • 1 whole Red Bell Pepper (diced)
  • 0.5 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 1 whole Lime (for juice)
  • 2 tbsp Soy Sauce or Tamari
  • 1 tsp Brown Sugar or Maple Syrup
  • 1 tbsp Sesame Oil
  • 0.2 cup Fresh Cilantro (chopped)
Instructions
