High-Protein Chickpea Flour Omelette with Spicy Tempeh Crumbles and Spinach

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 120 g Chickpea flour
  • 240 ml Water
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (Kala Namak)
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.2 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 227 g Tempeh
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce (low sodium)
  • 1 tsp Sriracha
  • 100 g Spinach
Instructions
First, prepare the tempeh crumbles. Crumble the tempeh into a bowl. In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce and sriracha. Pour the mixture over the tempeh crumbles and toss to coat. Heat 1 teaspoon of olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the tempeh crumbles and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and slightly crispy. Set aside. Next, prepare the omelette batter. In a large bowl, whisk together the chickpea flour, water, nutritional yeast, black salt, turmeric powder, smoked paprika, and black pepper until smooth and lump-free. Heat the remaining olive oil in the same skillet over medium heat. Pour half of the chickpea batter into the skillet to form one omelette, swirling to evenly coat the bottom. Cook for 3-4 minutes until the edges are set and bubbles appear on the surface. Sprinkle half of the spinach and half of the tempeh crumbles over one side of the omelette. Carefully fold the omelette in half and cook for another 1-2 minutes until heated through. Slide onto a plate. Repeat with the remaining batter, spinach, and tempeh crumbles for the second serving. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Spicy Peanut Tofu & Chickpea Bowl with Quinoa and Steamed Greens

🥜 51g protein/serving 🔪 25 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 400 g Chickpeas (canned, drained and rinsed)
  • 185 g Quinoa
  • 370 ml Water (for quinoa)
  • 300 g Broccoli florets
  • 4 tbsp Peanut butter
  • 3 tbsp Soy sauce (low sodium)
  • 2 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Sriracha
  • 1 tsp Ginger (grated)
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
Instructions
First, press the tofu: wrap the block in paper towels, place a heavy object on top, and press for at least 20 minutes to remove excess water. While tofu is pressing, cook the quinoa: rinse quinoa thoroughly, then combine with 370ml water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside. Steam the broccoli florets until tender-crisp, about 5-7 minutes. Cut the pressed tofu into 1-inch cubes. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tofu cubes and cook for 8-10 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden brown and crispy on all sides. Add the drained chickpeas to the skillet and cook for another 3-5 minutes, stirring occasionally. Meanwhile, prepare the peanut sauce: in a small bowl, whisk together peanut butter, soy sauce, rice vinegar, maple syrup, sriracha, grated ginger, and sesame oil until smooth. If too thick, add a splash of water. Add the sauce to the skillet with the tofu and chickpeas, tossing to coat evenly. Cook for 2-3 minutes until the sauce has slightly thickened and coats everything. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls. Top each with the sauced tofu and chickpeas, and a generous portion of steamed broccoli. Serve warm.

High-Protein Seitan & Black Bean Skillet with Roasted Bell Peppers and Brown Rice

🥜 50g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 50 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 185 g Brown rice
  • 470 ml Water (for rice)
  • 250 g Seitan
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 1 whole Green bell pepper
  • 400 g Black beans (canned, drained and rinsed)
  • 400 g Diced tomatoes (canned)
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 1.5 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 120 ml Vegetable broth
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro (chopped, for garnish)
Instructions
First, cook the brown rice: rinse brown rice thoroughly, then combine with 470ml water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 40-45 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and set aside. While the rice cooks, slice the seitan into bite-sized pieces and cut the bell peppers into strips. Heat olive oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned. Remove seitan from the skillet and set aside. Add the sliced bell peppers to the same skillet and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring until softened. Stir in the tomato paste, cumin, chili powder, and smoked paprika, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant. Pour in the diced tomatoes and vegetable broth, bringing the mixture to a simmer. Return the cooked seitan to the skillet. Add the drained and rinsed black beans. Stir everything together, reduce heat to low, cover, and let it simmer for 10-15 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld. Stir occasionally. Serve the seitan and black bean skillet over the cooked brown rice. Garnish with fresh chopped cilantro before serving.

High-Protein Cajun-Spiced Pecans & Roasted Edamame

🥜 23g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Shelled edamame (frozen)
  • 150 g Pecan halves
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1.5 tbsp Cajun seasoning (salt-free preferred)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper. In a large bowl, combine the frozen shelled edamame, pecan halves, olive oil, Cajun seasoning, and garlic powder. Toss everything together until the edamame and pecans are evenly coated with the spices and oil. Spread the seasoned edamame and pecans in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. High-Protein Savory Quinoa & Black-Eyed Pea Breakfast Bowl with Roasted Asparagus and Cherry Tomatoes

🥜 27g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 100 g Quinoa (uncooked)
  • 200 ml Vegetable broth
  • 240 g Canned black-eyed peas (drained and rinsed)
  • 200 g Asparagus
  • 150 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Dried thyme
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley (chopped)
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. Wash and trim the woody ends off the asparagus, then cut into 2-inch pieces. Halve the cherry tomatoes. 3. On the prepared baking sheet, toss the asparagus and cherry tomatoes with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 0.5 teaspoon of garlic powder, 0.25 teaspoon of dried thyme, 0.25 teaspoon of salt, and a pinch of black pepper. Roast for 15-20 minutes, or until the asparagus is tender-crisp and tomatoes are slightly burst. 4. While vegetables roast, rinse the quinoa thoroughly under cold water. In a small saucepan, combine the rinsed quinoa with vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all liquid is absorbed and quinoa is fluffy. Let stand, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 5. In a separate bowl, gently mix the drained and rinsed black-eyed peas with the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 0.5 teaspoon of garlic powder, 0.25 teaspoon of dried thyme, 0.25 teaspoon of salt, 0.25 teaspoon of black pepper, and 2 tablespoons of nutritional yeast. 6. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two serving bowls. Top with the seasoned black-eyed peas and the roasted asparagus and cherry tomatoes. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley before serving.

High-Protein French Lentil & Roasted Vegetable Bowl with Lemon-Herb Tempeh

🥜 32g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 120 g French green lentils (uncooked)
  • 480 ml Vegetable broth
  • 226 g Tempeh
  • 300 g Mixed bell peppers (e.g., red, yellow)
  • 200 g Zucchini (medium)
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 tsp Dried basil
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley (chopped, for garnish)
Instructions
1. Rinse French green lentils thoroughly. In a medium saucepan, combine lentils with 480ml vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until tender. Drain any excess liquid. 2. While lentils cook, preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Chop the bell peppers and zucchini into bite-sized pieces. 3. Crumble the tempeh into small, irregular pieces. In a medium bowl, combine the tempeh crumbles with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 1 tablespoon of lemon juice, 0.5 teaspoon of dried oregano, 0.5 teaspoon of dried basil, 0.5 teaspoon of garlic powder, 0.25 teaspoon of salt, and 0.25 teaspoon of black pepper. Mix well to coat. 4. On a large baking sheet, toss the chopped bell peppers and zucchini with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 0.25 teaspoon of salt, and 0.25 teaspoon of black pepper. Spread in a single layer. 5. On a separate section of the same baking sheet or another small one, spread the seasoned tempeh crumbles. Roast the vegetables and tempeh for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, until the vegetables are tender and slightly caramelized, and tempeh is golden brown and slightly crispy. 6. In a small bowl, whisk together the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 1 tablespoon of lemon juice, 0.5 teaspoon of dried oregano, 0.5 teaspoon of dried basil, and 0.5 teaspoon of garlic powder. 7. To assemble, divide the cooked lentils into two bowls. Top with roasted vegetables and tempeh. Drizzle with the lemon-herb dressing and garnish with fresh parsley.

High-Protein Red Lentil & Vegetable Fritters with Herbed Tahini Dip and Sautéed Kale

🥜 30g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Red lentils (dry)
  • 0.5 cup Carrot (grated)
  • 0.5 cup Zucchini (grated, squeezed dry)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley (chopped)
  • 4 tbsp Water (for dip)
  • 4 cup Kale (chopped)
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder (for kale)
Instructions
Rinse the red lentils thoroughly and cook them in 3 cups of water until very tender, about 15-20 minutes. Drain any excess water and mash the lentils slightly, leaving some texture. In a large bowl, combine the mashed lentils, grated carrot, squeezed dry zucchini, 1 tsp garlic powder, cumin, smoked paprika, nutritional yeast, salt, and pepper. Mix well to form a cohesive batter. Heat 1 tbsp of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Form the lentil mixture into 6-8 fritters and cook for 4-5 minutes per side, until golden brown and crispy. While fritters cook, prepare the dip: whisk together tahini, lemon juice, chopped parsley, and 4 tbsp water until smooth and creamy. Add more water if needed to reach desired consistency. In a separate pan, heat the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add the chopped kale and 0.5 tsp garlic powder, sautéing for 5-7 minutes until wilted and tender-crisp. Serve the fritters hot with a side of sautéed kale and a generous dollop of the herbed tahini dip.

High-Protein Peanut-Glazed Seitan & Broccoli Stir-Fry with Brown Rice

🥜 50g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 250 g Seitan, store-bought, sliced or diced
  • 1 cup Brown rice, uncooked
  • 2 cup Water
  • 4 cup Broccoli florets
  • 1 whole Bell pepper (any color), sliced
  • 0.5 cup Carrots, thinly sliced
  • 4 tbsp Peanut butter, natural creamy
  • 3 tbsp Soy sauce or tamari
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup or agave nectar
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp Ginger, grated
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 0.2 cup Water or vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp Cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp Toasted sesame seeds, for garnish
Instructions
1. Cook the brown rice: Combine 1 cup brown rice with 2 cups water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 40-45 minutes, or until water is absorbed and rice is tender. Let stand for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 2. Prepare the peanut glaze: In a small bowl, whisk together peanut butter, soy sauce, rice vinegar, maple syrup, minced garlic, grated ginger, and 1/4 cup water or vegetable broth. In a separate tiny bowl, dissolve cornstarch in 1 tbsp cold water to create a slurry. Set both aside. 3. Heat sesame oil in a large wok or deep skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seitan and stir-fry for 5-7 minutes until lightly browned and slightly crispy. Remove seitan from the wok and set aside. 4. Add broccoli florets, sliced bell pepper, and sliced carrots to the wok. Stir-fry for 5-7 minutes, until vegetables are tender-crisp. You can add a splash of water if needed to help steam them. 5. Return the seitan to the wok with the vegetables. Stir the peanut glaze mixture once more, then pour it over the seitan and vegetables. Bring to a gentle simmer. Stir in the cornstarch slurry and cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens and coats everything evenly. 6. Divide the cooked brown rice between two serving bowls. Spoon the peanut-glazed seitan and vegetable stir-fry over the rice. Garnish with toasted sesame seeds and serve hot.

David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Chickpeas, canned (rinsed and drained)
  • 0.5 cup Walnut halves
  • 3 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
  • 0.1 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. Ensure chickpeas are thoroughly dry by patting them with a paper towel. In a medium bowl, combine the dried chickpeas with olive oil, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Toss until well coated. 3. Spread the seasoned chickpeas in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. Roast for 20 minutes. Then, add the walnut halves to the baking sheet with the chickpeas. Continue roasting for another 8-10 minutes, or until chickpeas are crispy and walnuts are lightly toasted. Keep an eye on the walnuts to prevent burning. 4. Remove from oven and immediately transfer the roasted chickpeas and walnuts to a clean bowl. Sprinkle generously with nutritional yeast and toss well to coat. The warmth will help the nutritional yeast adhere. Let cool slightly before serving as a satisfying high-protein snack.

High-Protein Hearty Lentil & Walnut Loaf with Balsamic Glaze

🥜 25.6g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 45 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Brown lentils, dry
  • 2.5 cup Water or vegetable broth
  • 1 cup Walnuts, chopped
  • 0.8 cup Rolled oats
  • 1 whole Carrot, finely grated
  • 1 whole Celery stalk, finely diced
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 2 tbsp Flaxseed meal
  • 6 tbsp Warm water
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.2 cup Balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
Instructions
Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line a small loaf pan (8.5x4.5 inch or similar) with parchment paper, or grease well. Rinse lentils and cook in water or vegetable broth until tender, about 20-25 minutes. Drain well and set aside. In a small bowl, whisk together flaxseed meal and warm water; let sit for 5 minutes to form a 'flax egg'. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add grated carrot, diced celery, and minced garlic. Sauté for 5-7 minutes until vegetables are softened. In a large mixing bowl, combine the cooked lentils, sautéed vegetables, chopped walnuts, rolled oats, tomato paste, tamari, smoked paprika, dried thyme, salt, pepper, and the flax egg. Mix thoroughly with a spoon or your hands until well combined and slightly sticky. Press the mixture firmly into the prepared loaf pan. Bake for 40-45 minutes. While the loaf bakes, prepare the glaze: In a small saucepan, combine balsamic vinegar and maple syrup. Bring to a simmer over medium-low heat and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the glaze thickens slightly. Once the loaf is baked, let it rest in the pan for 10 minutes before carefully inverting it onto a cutting board. Slice into two generous portions and drizzle with the balsamic glaze before serving.
