High-Protein Savory Black Bean & Quinoa Bowl with Spicy Tofu Crumbles

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Quinoa, dry
  • 1.5 cup Water
  • 340 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 400 g Black beans, canned, drained and rinsed
  • 4 cup Baby spinach
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Chili powder
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 2 tbsp Soy sauce or tamari
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Cook Quinoa: Rinse quinoa thoroughly under cold water. Combine quinoa and 1.5 cups water in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Remove from heat and let sit, covered, for 5 minutes before fluffing with a fork. 2. Prepare Tofu Crumbles: Press extra-firm tofu for at least 15-20 minutes to remove excess water. Crumble the tofu into a bowl. In a large non-stick skillet, heat 1 tbsp olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the crumbled tofu, garlic, smoked paprika, cumin, chili powder, salt, and black pepper. Sauté, stirring frequently, for 8-10 minutes until the tofu is browned and crispy. Stir in 1 tbsp soy sauce and nutritional yeast during the last minute of cooking. Set aside. 3. Sauté Vegetables: In the same skillet, add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil. Add diced red bell pepper and sauté for 5-7 minutes until slightly softened. Add the baby spinach and cook until wilted, about 2-3 minutes. Stir in the black beans and remaining 1 tbsp soy sauce, cooking for another 2-3 minutes until heated through. 4. Assemble Bowls: Divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls. Top each with the black bean and vegetable mixture, followed by the spicy tofu crumbles. Serve warm.

High-Protein Roasted Chickpea & Seitan Grain Bowl with Lemon-Herb Tahini Dressing

🥜 42g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.8 cup Quinoa, dry
  • 1.5 cup Water
  • 200 g Seitan, pre-cooked, sliced or cubed
  • 400 g Chickpeas, canned, drained and rinsed
  • 3 cup Broccoli florets
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 0.5 tsp Dried thyme
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 4 tbsp Water (for dressing)
Instructions
1. Cook Quinoa: Rinse quinoa thoroughly under cold water. Combine quinoa and 1.5 cups water in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Remove from heat and let sit, covered, for 5 minutes before fluffing with a fork. 2. Roast Vegetables and Seitan: Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). On a large baking sheet, toss the seitan, chickpeas, and broccoli florets with 1 tbsp olive oil, oregano, thyme, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Spread in a single layer. Roast for 20 minutes, then add the cherry tomatoes to the baking sheet and roast for another 10-15 minutes, until vegetables are tender-crisp and chickpeas are slightly browned. 3. Prepare Lemon-Herb Tahini Dressing: While vegetables are roasting, whisk together tahini, lemon juice, fresh parsley, and 4 tbsp water in a small bowl until smooth and creamy. Add more water if needed to reach desired consistency. Season with a pinch of salt if desired. 4. Assemble Bowls: Divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls. Top each with the roasted seitan, chickpeas, and vegetables. Drizzle generously with the lemon-herb tahini dressing. Serve warm or at room temperature.

High-Protein Peanut Tofu and Broccoli Stir-fry with Brown Rice

🥜 39g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 60 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Brown rice, dry
  • 2 cup Water
  • 340 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 4 cup Broccoli florets
  • 1 cup Carrots, sliced
  • 3 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp Ginger, grated
  • 4 tbsp Peanut butter, natural creamy
  • 3 tbsp Soy sauce or tamari
  • 2 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Sriracha (optional)
  • 4 tbsp Water (for sauce)
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds (for garnish)
Instructions
1. Cook Brown Rice: Rinse brown rice thoroughly. In a saucepan, combine brown rice and 2 cups water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 40-45 minutes, or until water is absorbed and rice is tender. Remove from heat and let sit, covered, for 5 minutes before fluffing. 2. Prepare Tofu: Press extra-firm tofu for 15-20 minutes. Cut the pressed tofu into 1-inch cubes. Heat 1 tbsp sesame oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat. Add tofu cubes and cook for 8-10 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden brown and slightly crispy on all sides. Remove tofu from skillet and set aside. 3. Make Peanut Sauce: In a small bowl, whisk together peanut butter, soy sauce, rice vinegar, maple syrup, Sriracha (if using), and 4 tbsp water until smooth. Adjust consistency with more water if needed. 4. Stir-fry Vegetables: In the same wok/skillet (add a tiny bit more oil if needed), add minced garlic and grated ginger. Sauté for 1 minute until fragrant. Add broccoli florets and sliced carrots. Stir-fry for 5-7 minutes until vegetables are tender-crisp. 5. Combine and Serve: Return the cooked tofu to the skillet with the vegetables. Pour the peanut sauce over the tofu and vegetables, tossing gently to coat everything evenly. Cook for 2-3 minutes, allowing the sauce to thicken slightly. Serve immediately over the cooked brown rice, garnished with sesame seeds.

High-Protein Savory Tofu & White Bean Quinoa Bowl with Sautéed Greens

🥜 47.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 450 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 1 cup Cooked quinoa
  • 255 g Canned cannellini beans, rinsed and drained
  • 120 g Fresh spinach
  • 6 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.2 tsp Black salt (kala namak, optional, for eggy flavor)
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 60 ml Vegetable broth or water
Instructions
First, prepare the quinoa according to package instructions if not already cooked. While quinoa cooks, press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15-20 minutes to remove excess water; this can be done by wrapping it in a clean towel and placing a heavy object on top. Once pressed, crumble the tofu into a large, non-stick pan. Add 1 tablespoon of olive oil to the pan with the crumbled tofu, garlic powder, turmeric, smoked paprika, black salt (if using), salt, and black pepper. Sauté over medium heat for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tofu is lightly golden and heated through. Stir in the rinsed cannellini beans and nutritional yeast, cooking for another 3-5 minutes. In a separate pan, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium heat. Add the spinach and sauté until wilted, about 2-3 minutes, adding a splash of vegetable broth or water to help it wilt and prevent sticking. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two serving bowls. Top each with a generous portion of the savory tofu and white bean scramble, then add the sautéed spinach. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Seitan & Kidney Bean Mediterranean Grain Bowl with Roasted Zucchini and Tahini Dressing

🥜 64.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1.5 cup Cooked quinoa
  • 250 g Seitan, store-bought, sliced or cubed
  • 255 g Canned kidney beans, rinsed and drained
  • 2 whole Medium zucchini, cut into half-moons
  • 1 cup Cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 0.2 cup Kalamata olives, pitted and halved
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 4 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp Water
  • 1 tsp Minced garlic
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss the zucchini half-moons with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, dried oregano, a pinch of salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes, or until tender and slightly caramelized. While the zucchini roasts, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the sliced seitan and pan-sear for 5-7 minutes, turning occasionally, until it's lightly browned and heated through. In a small bowl, whisk together the tahini, lemon juice, water, and minced garlic until smooth and creamy. If the dressing is too thick, add more water, 1 teaspoon at a time, until desired consistency is reached. Season the dressing with salt and pepper to taste. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa between two large serving bowls. Top each with the roasted zucchini, seared seitan, rinsed kidney beans, cherry tomatoes, and kalamata olives. Drizzle generously with the tahini dressing and garnish with fresh chopped parsley before serving.

High-Protein Tempeh & Adzuki Bean Skillet with Roasted Broccoli and Brown Rice

🥜 50g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 cup Cooked brown rice
  • 227 g Block tempeh
  • 1.5 cup Cooked adzuki beans
  • 400 g Broccoli florets
  • 4 tbsp Soy sauce or tamari
  • 2 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp Minced garlic
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 0.5 tsp Red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 120 ml Vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp Cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds, for garnish
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss the broccoli florets with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes, or until tender-crisp and slightly charred. While the broccoli roasts, prepare the tempeh. Cut the tempeh block into 1/2-inch cubes. In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce/tamari, maple syrup, sesame oil, rice vinegar, minced garlic, grated ginger, and red pepper flakes (if using). In a large skillet, heat a splash of oil over medium-high heat. Add the tempeh cubes and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring frequently, until golden brown on all sides. Pour the prepared sauce over the tempeh. In a small cup, dissolve the cornstarch in the vegetable broth, then add this slurry to the skillet. Bring to a simmer, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens. Stir in the cooked adzuki beans and heat through for 2-3 minutes. To serve, divide the cooked brown rice between two bowls. Top with the tempeh and adzuki bean skillet mixture and the roasted broccoli. Garnish with sesame seeds.
