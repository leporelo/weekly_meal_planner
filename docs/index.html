High-Protein Tofu & Black Bean Hash with Roasted Sweet Potatoes and Kale

🥜 29g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 340 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 1 cup Cooked black beans
  • 300 g Sweet potatoes
  • 4 cup Kale, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 whole Avocado
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Peel the sweet potatoes and cut them into 1/2-inch cubes. Toss the sweet potato cubes with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and lightly caramelized, flipping halfway through. While sweet potatoes roast, press the extra-firm tofu to remove excess water, then crumble it into a large skillet. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, cumin, salt, and pepper to the skillet with the tofu. Cook over medium-high heat for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tofu is lightly browned and crispy. Stir in the cooked black beans and nutritional yeast, then add the chopped kale. Sauté for another 3-5 minutes until the kale is wilted. Combine the roasted sweet potatoes with the tofu and black bean mixture. Divide the hash into two bowls and top each with half an avocado, sliced or diced. Serve warm.

High-Protein Balsamic Glazed Seitan & White Bean Farro Bowl with Roasted Asparagus

🥜 40.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Seitan, sliced or cubed
  • 1 cup Cooked farro
  • 1 cup Canned white beans, rinsed and drained
  • 200 g Asparagus spears
  • 3 tbsp Balsamic vinegar
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Trim the woody ends off the asparagus spears. On a baking sheet, toss the asparagus with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast for 10-12 minutes, or until tender-crisp. In a small bowl, whisk together the balsamic vinegar, tamari, maple syrup, and garlic powder to create the glaze. In a separate bowl, toss the seitan pieces with the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil. Heat a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, turning occasionally, until lightly browned. Pour the balsamic glaze over the seitan, stirring to coat. Cook for another 2-3 minutes until the glaze thickens and coats the seitan. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked farro between two bowls. Top each with the glazed seitan, roasted asparagus, and rinsed white beans. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Tempeh & Chickpea Coconut Curry with Brown Rice

🥜 34g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 220 g Tempeh, crumbled or cubed
  • 1 cup Cooked chickpeas
  • 1.5 cup Cooked brown rice
  • 400 ml Full-fat coconut milk
  • 400 g Diced tomatoes, undrained
  • 3 cup Fresh spinach
  • 2 tbsp Curry powder
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 cup Vegetable broth
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the crumbled tempeh and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned. Add the grated ginger and minced garlic, sauté for 1 minute until fragrant. Stir in the curry powder and cook for another minute. Pour in the diced tomatoes (undrained), coconut milk, and vegetable broth. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for 15 minutes to allow the flavors to meld. Stir in the cooked chickpeas and fresh spinach. Continue to cook for 3-5 minutes, or until the spinach is completely wilted. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve the tempeh and chickpea coconut curry hot over bowls of cooked brown rice.

High-Protein Tamari & Chili Roasted Walnuts & Pumpkin Seeds

🥜 21g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 15 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 100 g Walnut halves, raw
  • 100 g Pumpkin seeds, raw
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 1 tsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Olive oil
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 325°F (160°C). In a medium bowl, combine the raw walnut halves and raw pumpkin seeds. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the tamari, chili powder, maple syrup, and olive oil until well combined. Roast in the preheated oven for 12-15 minutes, stirring halfway through, until they are fragrant and lightly toasted. Keep a close eye on them to prevent burning. Remove from the oven and let cool completely on the baking sheet before serving. The mixture will crisp up as it cools. Store any leftovers in an airtight container at room temperature.

High-Protein Savory Farro & Chickpea Breakfast Bowl with Sautéed Greens and Nutritional Yeast

🥜 34g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 120 g Farro
  • 240 g Cooked Chickpeas
  • 160 g Kale, chopped
  • 2 whole Garlic cloves, minced
  • 240 ml Vegetable Broth
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 2 tbsp Hemp Seeds
  • 2 tbsp Sunflower Seeds
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
Rinse the farro under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine the farro with 480ml (2 cups) of water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the farro is tender and the water is absorbed. While the farro cooks, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the chopped kale and 120ml (0.5 cup) of vegetable broth, cover, and steam for 5-7 minutes until the kale is wilted and tender. Stir in the chickpeas, smoked paprika, turmeric, remaining salt, and black pepper, cooking for another 2-3 minutes until heated through. Once the farro is cooked, fluff it with a fork and divide it into two bowls. Top each bowl with the sautéed kale and chickpea mixture. Sprinkle each serving with 2 tablespoons of nutritional yeast, 1 tablespoon of hemp seeds, and 1 tablespoon of sunflower seeds. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Smoky Black Bean & Tempeh Quinoa Bowl with Roasted Plantains and Collard Greens

🥜 43.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 120 g Quinoa
  • 200 g Tempeh
  • 300 g Cooked Black Beans
  • 2 whole Ripe Plantains
  • 200 g Collard Greens, chopped
  • 2 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Chipotle Powder
  • 120 ml Vegetable Broth
  • 1 whole Lime, for juice and wedges
  • 30 g Fresh Cilantro, chopped
  • 3 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Peel the plantains and slice them into 1.5 cm (0.6 inch) thick rounds. Toss them with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden brown and caramelized. Meanwhile, rinse the quinoa thoroughly. In a saucepan, combine quinoa with 240ml (1 cup) of water, bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes or until water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork. Crumble the tempeh into a large skillet. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium-high heat and sauté the tempeh for 5-7 minutes until lightly browned. Add smoked paprika, cumin, garlic powder, and chipotle powder, stirring for 1 minute until fragrant. Stir in the black beans and 120ml (0.5 cup) vegetable broth, bringing it to a gentle simmer. Add the chopped collard greens, cover, and cook for 5-7 minutes until tender. Season with salt and pepper to taste, then stir in the juice of half a lime. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked quinoa into two serving bowls. Top with the smoky tempeh and black bean mixture, roasted plantains, and a generous sprinkle of fresh cilantro. Serve with extra lime wedges.

High-Protein Spicy Peanut Seitan Skillet with Roasted Sweet Potatoes and Collard Greens

🥜 51g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 300 g Seitan, cubed or sliced
  • 400 g Sweet Potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 200 g Collard Greens, chopped
  • 4 tbsp Natural Peanut Butter
  • 2 tbsp Soy Sauce (or Tamari)
  • 1 tbsp Rice Vinegar
  • 1 tsp Sriracha (or chili garlic sauce)
  • 1 tbsp Fresh Ginger, grated
  • 2 whole Garlic cloves, minced
  • 120 ml Vegetable Broth
  • 1 whole Lime, for juice
  • 1 tbsp Sesame Seeds, for garnish
  • 3 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss the cubed sweet potatoes with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway, until tender and lightly caramelized. Heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned on all sides. Add the chopped collard greens to the skillet and sauté for 5 minutes until starting to wilt. Pour the peanut sauce over the seitan and greens, stirring to coat evenly. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 3-5 minutes, allowing the sauce to thicken slightly. Stir in the juice of one lime. Divide the seitan skillet mixture and roasted sweet potatoes between two plates. Garnish with sesame seeds before serving.

High-Protein Savory Amaranth & Tempeh Scramble with Chickpeas and Kale

🥜 38g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 100 g Amaranth, dry
  • 300 ml Water (for amaranth)
  • 200 g Tempeh, organic
  • 250 g Chickpeas, cooked
  • 100 g Kale, chopped
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.2 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 60 ml Vegetable Broth
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
Instructions
1. Rinse amaranth thoroughly under cold water. Combine amaranth and 300ml water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until water is absorbed and amaranth is tender. Fluff with a fork. 2. While amaranth cooks, crumble the tempeh into small pieces using your hands or a fork. Drain and rinse the cooked chickpeas. 3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tempeh and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned and slightly crispy. 4. Add the drained chickpeas, chopped kale, nutritional yeast, tamari, garlic powder, smoked paprika, turmeric powder, black pepper, and salt to the skillet. Pour in the vegetable broth. 5. Stir everything together and cook for another 5-7 minutes, or until the kale has wilted and the liquid has mostly evaporated, stirring frequently to combine all the flavors. 6. To serve, divide the cooked amaranth evenly between two bowls. Top each bowl generously with the tempeh and chickpea scramble. Serve warm.

High-Protein Seitan & Green Lentil Shawarma-Inspired Bowl with Toasted Pearl Couscous and Roasted Eggplant

🥜 49g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Seitan, pre-made
  • 100 g Green Lentils, dry
  • 400 ml Water (for lentils)
  • 100 g Pearl Couscous
  • 200 ml Vegetable broth (for couscous)
  • 1 whole Eggplant, medium
  • 1 whole Lemon, juiced
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tsp Ground Cumin
  • 1.5 tsp Ground Coriander
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 3 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Dice the eggplant into 1-inch cubes. Toss with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, until tender and lightly browned. 2. Rinse green lentils thoroughly. Combine lentils and 400ml water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes until tender. Drain any excess water. 3. In a separate saucepan, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium heat. Add the pearl couscous and toast for 2-3 minutes, stirring, until lightly golden. Pour in 200ml vegetable broth, bring to a simmer, cover, and cook for 10-12 minutes or until liquid is absorbed. Fluff with a fork. 4. Slice the seitan into thin strips or bite-sized pieces. In a bowl, combine the seitan with 1 tablespoon olive oil, 1 teaspoon cumin, 1 teaspoon coriander, 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika, 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper. Mix well to coat. 5. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the marinated seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring frequently, until heated through and slightly browned. 6. Prepare the tahini dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together the tahini, lemon juice, 1/2 teaspoon cumin, 1/2 teaspoon coriander, 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika, 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and enough water (1-3 tbsp) to reach a pourable consistency. 7. To assemble the bowls, divide the pearl couscous, green lentils, roasted eggplant, and seitan evenly between two bowls. Drizzle generously with the tahini dressing and garnish with fresh chopped parsley. Serve warm.

High-Protein Black-Eyed Pea & Seitan Stew with Collard Greens and Quinoa

🥜 52g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 50 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 150 g Black-Eyed Peas, dry
  • 600 ml Water (for peas)
  • 200 g Seitan, pre-made, diced
  • 100 g Quinoa, dry
  • 200 ml Water (for quinoa)
  • 150 g Collard Greens, chopped
  • 400 g Diced Tomatoes, canned
  • 250 ml Vegetable Broth
  • 2 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 1 tsp Ground Cumin
  • 1.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 whole Bay Leaf
  • 1.5 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
1. Rinse black-eyed peas thoroughly. In a large pot, combine the rinsed black-eyed peas with 600ml fresh water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 40-50 minutes, or until tender. Drain any excess water. 2. While peas are cooking, rinse quinoa thoroughly. In a small saucepan, combine quinoa with 200ml water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until water is absorbed. Let stand covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 3. Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven or heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add the diced seitan and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned. 4. Add the smoked paprika, cumin, and garlic powder to the pot with the seitan. Stir and cook for 1 minute until fragrant. 5. Stir in the cooked black-eyed peas, diced tomatoes (undrained), vegetable broth, chopped collard greens, bay leaf, salt, and black pepper. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for 15-20 minutes, or until collard greens are tender and flavors have melded. Stir occasionally. Remove the bay leaf before serving. 6. To serve, divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls. Ladle generous portions of the black-eyed pea and seitan stew over the quinoa. Serve hot.

High-Protein Crispy Sesame Tofu & Chickpea Barley Bowl with Roasted Asparagus

🥜 34g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 450 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 1 cup Cooked chickpeas
  • 0.5 cup Pearl barley
  • 300 g Asparagus
  • 3 tbsp Soy sauce or tamari
  • 1 tbsp Toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Maple syrup
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp Cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp Sesame seeds
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). 2. Cook the pearl barley: Combine 0.5 cup dry pearl barley with 1.5 cups water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 30-40 minutes, or until tender and liquid is absorbed. 3. Prepare the tofu: While barley cooks, press extra-firm tofu for at least 20 minutes to remove excess water. Cut into 1-inch cubes. In a medium bowl, whisk together 2 tbsp soy sauce/tamari, toasted sesame oil, rice vinegar, maple syrup, and garlic powder. Add the cubed tofu and gently toss to coat. Sprinkle with cornstarch and toss again until evenly coated. 4. Roast asparagus: On a large baking sheet, toss the trimmed asparagus with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread in a single layer. 5. Crisp the tofu: On a separate part of the baking sheet (or a second sheet if needed to avoid overcrowding), spread the coated tofu cubes in a single layer. 6. Roast for 20-25 minutes, flipping the tofu halfway through, until tofu is golden brown and crispy, and asparagus is tender-crisp. 7. Assemble bowls: Divide the cooked barley between two bowls. Top each with crispy sesame tofu, roasted asparagus, and chickpeas. Drizzle with the remaining 1 tbsp soy sauce/tamari (or a fresh dressing if preferred). Garnish with sesame seeds.
