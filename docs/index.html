High-Protein Scrambled Tofu with Kidney Beans, Kale, and Toasted Whole-Wheat Bread

🥜 48.5g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 15 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Firm tofu
  • 1 cup Kidney beans (cooked)
  • 4 cup Kale (chopped)
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Black salt (kala namak)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 4 whole Whole-wheat bread
Instructions
1. Press the tofu: Drain the firm tofu and press it for at least 15-20 minutes to remove excess water. This helps with texture. 2. Prepare ingredients: While tofu is pressing, rinse and drain the cooked kidney beans, chop the kale, and gather your spices. 3. Scramble the tofu: Heat olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Crumble the pressed tofu into the skillet using your hands or a fork. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 5-7 minutes until it starts to brown slightly. 4. Season and add beans: Add nutritional yeast, turmeric powder, black salt, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and black pepper to the tofu. Stir well to coat evenly. Add the kidney beans and cook for another 2-3 minutes, stirring gently. 5. Wilt the kale: Add the chopped kale to the skillet, stirring until it wilts, about 3-5 minutes. 6. Toast bread and serve: While the scramble finishes, toast the whole-wheat bread slices. Divide the scrambled tofu mixture between two plates and serve immediately with the toasted bread on the side.

High-Protein Red Lentil & Chickpea Coconut Curry with Brown Rice and Spinach

🥜 40.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 0.5 cup Brown rice (dry)
  • 0.8 cup Red lentils (dry)
  • 1 cup Chickpeas (cooked, drained, rinsed)
  • 400 ml Full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 cup Vegetable broth
  • 4 cup Fresh spinach
  • 400 g Diced tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp Grated ginger
  • 2 whole Minced garlic
  • 2 tbsp Curry powder
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tsp Ground coriander
  • 0.5 tsp Red pepper flakes
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Cook the brown rice: Rinse the brown rice thoroughly. Combine 0.5 cup dry brown rice with 1 cup of water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 25-30 minutes, or until water is absorbed and rice is tender. Let it rest for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 2. Prepare aromatics and spices: While rice cooks, heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add grated ginger and minced garlic, and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant. Stir in curry powder, turmeric, cumin, coriander, and red pepper flakes, cooking for another minute until aromatic. 3. Add liquids and legumes: Pour in the full-fat coconut milk, vegetable broth, and diced tomatoes (undrained). Add the red lentils (rinsed) and chickpeas. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lentils are tender. 4. Finish with spinach: Stir in the fresh spinach until it wilts into the curry, about 2-3 minutes. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. 5. Serve: Divide the cooked brown rice between two bowls and ladle the hot lentil and chickpea curry over the rice. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Seitan and Kidney Bean Chili with Cornbread

🥜 56g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 150 g Seitan (pre-made, chopped)
  • 2 whole Kidney beans (canned, drained, rinsed)
  • 400 g Crushed tomatoes (canned)
  • 400 g Diced tomatoes (canned)
  • 2 cup Vegetable broth
  • 1 whole Bell pepper (any color, diced)
  • 2 whole Celery stalks (diced)
  • 2 whole Minced garlic
  • 2 tbsp Chili powder
  • 1 tbsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Unsweetened cocoa powder (optional)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.8 cup Cornmeal
  • 0.2 cup All-purpose flour
  • 0.8 cup Plant milk
  • 1 tbsp Sugar
  • 1 tbsp Baking powder
  • 0.2 tsp Salt (for cornbread)
  • 2 tbsp Vegan butter (melted)
Instructions
1. Sauté vegetables: Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add diced bell pepper, celery, and minced garlic. Sauté for 5-7 minutes until vegetables soften. 2. Add spices and seitan: Stir in chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, and optional cocoa powder. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the chopped seitan and cook for 3-5 minutes, stirring, to slightly brown it. 3. Simmer chili: Add the drained and rinsed kidney beans, crushed tomatoes, diced tomatoes, and vegetable broth. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and let it cook for at least 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, allowing flavors to meld. Season with salt and pepper to taste. 4. Prepare cornbread: Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). In a mixing bowl, whisk together cornmeal, all-purpose flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. In a separate bowl, combine plant milk and melted vegan butter. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and mix just until combined (do not overmix). 5. Bake cornbread: Pour the cornbread batter into a lightly greased 8x8 inch baking dish (or equivalent). Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. 6. Serve: Ladle the hot seitan and kidney bean chili into bowls. Cut the cornbread into 4 equal pieces (2 per person) and serve alongside the chili.

High-Protein Roasted Edamame and Pumpkin Seed Mix

🥜 35g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 1.0 servings Baska: 0.8 servings
Ingredients
  • 1.5 cup Shelled edamame (frozen, thawed)
  • 0.2 cup Pumpkin seeds
  • 1 tsp Olive oil
  • 0.2 tsp Sea salt
  • 0.2 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.2 tsp Smoked paprika
Instructions
1. Preheat oven: Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper for easy cleanup. 2. Roast: Spread the seasoned edamame and pumpkin seeds in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. Roast for 15-20 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the edamame is slightly browned and the pumpkin seeds are toasted and fragrant. 5. Cool and serve: Allow the roasted mix to cool slightly before serving as a crunchy, high-protein snack.

High-Protein Savory Millet & Adzuki Bean Breakfast Bowl with Smoked Tempeh Crumbles and Spinach

🥜 47g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep 🔥 30 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Dry Millet
  • 2.5 cup Water
  • 1 whole Canned Adzuki Beans, drained and rinsed
  • 224 g Smoked Tempeh
  • 4 cup Fresh Spinach
  • 4 tbsp Nutritional Yeast
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.2 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
1. Rinse the millet thoroughly under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine the millet and water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the water is absorbed and the millet is fluffy. Remove from heat and let stand covered for 5 minutes. 2. While the millet cooks, prepare the tempeh. Crumble the smoked tempeh into small pieces using your hands or a fork. 3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the crumbled tempeh, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper. Sauté for 7-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tempeh is lightly browned and crispy. 4. Add the drained and rinsed adzuki beans, tamari, and nutritional yeast to the tempeh in the skillet. Stir well to combine and cook for another 2-3 minutes. 5. Stir in the fresh spinach and cook just until it wilts, about 2-3 minutes. 6. To assemble, divide the cooked millet evenly between two bowls. Top each bowl generously with the tempeh and adzuki bean mixture. Serve warm.

High-Protein Farro & Black Bean Bowl with Spiced Tofu and Roasted Sweet Potatoes

🥜 41.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Semi-pearled Farro
  • 3 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 198 g Extra-firm Tofu
  • 1 whole Sweet Potato
  • 1 whole Canned Black Beans, drained and rinsed
  • 0.5 whole Avocado
  • 1 whole Lime
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Cilantro, chopped
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Peel and dice the sweet potato into 1.5 cm (0.5 inch) cubes. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, 0.5 tsp cumin, 0.5 tsp chili powder, and 0.25 tsp salt. Spread in a single layer on a baking sheet. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and slightly caramelized, flipping halfway through. 2. While the sweet potatoes roast, press the tofu to remove excess water. Crumble or dice the tofu into small pieces. In a bowl, toss the tofu with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, 0.5 tsp cumin, 0.5 tsp chili powder, 0.5 tsp smoked paprika, 0.25 tsp salt, and 0.25 tsp black pepper. 3. Cook the farro: In a medium saucepan, combine farro and vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 25-30 minutes, or until farro is tender and liquid is absorbed. If using quick-cooking farro, follow package instructions. 4. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seasoned tofu and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and crispy. 5. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked farro between two serving bowls. Top with roasted sweet potatoes, spiced tofu, and drained black beans. Slice the avocado and add half to each bowl. 6. Squeeze fresh lime juice over each bowl and garnish with chopped fresh cilantro. Serve immediately.

High-Protein Lentil & Mushroom Stroganoff with Seitan Strips and Whole Wheat Pasta

🥜 55g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Whole Wheat Pasta
  • 224 g Seitan (in strips or chunks)
  • 1.5 cup Brown or Green Lentils, cooked
  • 2 cup Cremini Mushrooms, sliced
  • 1.5 cup Vegetable Broth
  • 0.5 cup Vegan Sour Cream
  • 1 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tsp Dijon Mustard
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Dried Thyme
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped
Instructions
1. Cook the whole wheat pasta according to package directions until al dente. Drain, reserving 0.5 cup of pasta water, and set aside. 2. While pasta cooks, heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet or pot over medium-high heat. Add the seitan strips and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned. Remove seitan from the skillet and set aside. 3. Add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil to the same skillet. Add the sliced mushrooms and cook for 5-7 minutes until they are softened and have released their liquid. 4. Stir in the smoked paprika, dried thyme, salt, and black pepper. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant. 5. Pour in the vegetable broth, tamari, and Dijon mustard. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to medium-low. Add the cooked lentils and the seitan back into the skillet. Simmer for 5 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld. 6. Stir in the vegan sour cream until well combined and heated through, but do not boil. If the sauce is too thick, add a splash of the reserved pasta water until desired consistency is reached. 7. Serve the stroganoff over the cooked whole wheat pasta. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley.

High-Protein Tempeh & Black Bean Hash with Bell Peppers and Spinach

🥜 32g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 200 g Firm Tempeh
  • 240 g Canned Black Beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 whole Red Bell Pepper
  • 1 whole Green Bell Pepper
  • 100 g Fresh Spinach
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Chili Powder
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
First, pat the tempeh dry and crumble it into small pieces using your hands or a fork. Rinse and drain the black beans thoroughly. Dice both bell peppers into small, uniform pieces. Heat the olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the crumbled tempeh and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it's lightly browned and somewhat crispy. Add the diced bell peppers and minced garlic to the skillet and sauté for another 5-7 minutes until the peppers begin to soften. Stir in the smoked paprika, cumin powder, and chili powder, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the rinsed black beans, salt, and black pepper, and cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring gently to combine. Finally, add the fresh spinach, stirring until it wilts completely, which should take 2-3 minutes. Serve the hash immediately.

High-Protein Baked Tofu & White Bean Skillet with Roasted Bell Peppers and Rosemary

🥜 44g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-Firm Tofu, pressed
  • 400 g Canned Cannellini Beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 whole Red Bell Pepper
  • 1 whole Yellow Bell Pepper
  • 400 g Crushed Tomatoes
  • 100 g Fresh Spinach
  • 3 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp Fresh Rosemary, chopped
  • 1 tsp Dried Oregano
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 0.8 tsp Salt
  • 0.5 tsp Black Pepper
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Press the extra-firm tofu for at least 20 minutes to remove excess water, then cut it into 1-inch cubes. Dice the red and yellow bell peppers into similar-sized pieces. In a large oven-safe skillet or baking dish, combine the tofu cubes, diced bell peppers, rinsed cannellini beans, minced garlic, chopped fresh rosemary, dried oregano, 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and black pepper. Toss everything gently to ensure even coating. Roast in the preheated oven for 20 minutes. After 20 minutes, remove the skillet from the oven, stir in the crushed tomatoes and the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil. Return to the oven and bake for another 15 minutes, or until the sauce is bubbly and slightly thickened. Finally, stir in the fresh spinach until it wilts into the sauce, which will take about 2-3 minutes from residual heat. Serve directly from the skillet.

High-Protein Smoky Tofu & Chickpea Hash with Roasted Bell Peppers and Rosemary

🥜 37.5g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 35 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 340 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 240 g Cooked chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 whole Red bell pepper
  • 1 whole Green bell pepper
  • 300 g Potato, peeled and diced
  • 2 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Dried rosemary
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 2 tbsp Tamari (or soy sauce)
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish
Instructions
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper. Crumble the pressed tofu into bite-sized pieces and set aside. Dice the red and green bell peppers and the peeled potato into roughly 1.5 cm (0.6 inch) cubes. In a large bowl, combine the diced potatoes, bell peppers, chickpeas, smoked paprika, dried rosemary, garlic powder, half of the olive oil, salt, and black pepper. Toss well to ensure all vegetables are evenly coated. Spread the seasoned vegetables in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet and roast for 20 minutes.
