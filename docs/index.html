Spicy Tofu & Bell Pepper Scramble with Black Beans and Quinoa

🥜 34g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 340 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 425 g Canned black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 0.5 cup Quinoa
  • 1 whole Bell pepper (any color), chopped
  • 2 whole Garlic, minced
  • 2 cup Spinach, packed
  • 2 tbsp Nutritional yeast
  • 0.5 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 0.2 tsp Red pepper flakes
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)
Instructions
1. Rinse 0.5 cup of quinoa thoroughly under cold water. Combine quinoa with 1 cup of water in a small pot, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, or until all water is absorbed. Let it rest for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. 2. While quinoa cooks, press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15 minutes to remove excess water. Once pressed, crumble the tofu into small, scramble-like pieces using your hands or a fork. 3. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and chopped bell pepper, and sauté for 5-7 minutes until the bell pepper softens. 4. Add the crumbled tofu to the skillet. Sprinkle with nutritional yeast, turmeric, cumin, smoked paprika, red pepper flakes, salt, and black pepper. Cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tofu is lightly golden and heated through. 5. Stir in the drained and rinsed black beans and fresh spinach. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, until the spinach wilts. 6. Divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls. Top each bowl with the spicy tofu scramble. Garnish with fresh cilantro if desired, and serve warm.

Spicy Black Bean & Tofu Burrito Bowl with Brown Rice

🥜 34g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 340 g Extra-firm tofu
  • 0.5 cup Brown rice
  • 425 g Canned black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup Corn (frozen or canned)
  • 1 whole Bell pepper (any color), diced
  • 1 whole Avocado, diced
  • 0.5 cup Salsa
  • 1 whole Lime, juiced
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)
Instructions
1. Cook 0.5 cup of brown rice according to package instructions (typically 1 part rice to 2 parts water, simmer for 30-40 minutes). Fluff with a fork once cooked. 2. While rice cooks, press the extra-firm tofu for 15-20 minutes. Once pressed, cut the tofu into 1/2-inch cubes. 3. In a medium bowl, toss the tofu cubes with 0.5 tablespoon of olive oil, chili powder, cumin powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper until evenly coated. 4. Heat the remaining 0.5 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seasoned tofu and cook for 10-12 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden brown and slightly crispy on all sides. 5. In a separate bowl, combine the drained and rinsed black beans, corn, diced bell pepper, and the juice of half a lime. Mix well. 6. Assemble the bowls: Divide the cooked brown rice between two serving bowls. Top with the seasoned tofu, black bean and corn mixture, diced avocado, and a dollop of salsa. Squeeze extra lime juice over the top and garnish with fresh cilantro if desired.

Hearty Beluga Lentil & Spinach Curry with Basmati Rice and Toasted Cashews

🥜 27g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 40 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 cup Beluga lentils (black lentils), dry
  • 1 cup Basmati rice, dry
  • 400 g Canned diced tomatoes
  • 400 ml Full-fat coconut milk
  • 4 cup Fresh spinach, packed
  • 3 whole Garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp Ginger, grated
  • 50 g Raw unsalted cashews
  • 2 tsp Curry powder
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 1 tsp Coriander powder
  • 0.5 tsp Red pepper flakes
  • 2 cup Vegetable broth
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 2 tbsp Fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)
Instructions
1. Rinse 1 cup of beluga lentils thoroughly under cold water. In a medium pot, combine the rinsed lentils with 3 cups of water, bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 20-25 minutes or until tender. Drain any excess water. 2. In a separate pot, rinse 1 cup of basmati rice thoroughly. Combine with 1.5 cups of water, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes. Let rest for 5 minutes, then fluff. 3. While lentils and rice cook, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the raw cashews and toast for 3-5 minutes until lightly golden. Remove cashews and set aside. 4. In the same pot, add the minced garlic and grated ginger, and sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant. Be careful not to burn the garlic. 5. Stir in the curry powder, turmeric, cumin, coriander, and red pepper flakes. Cook for 30 seconds, stirring constantly, until aromatic. 6. Add the canned diced tomatoes (undrained), full-fat coconut milk, and vegetable broth. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer. 7. Stir in the cooked beluga lentils and salt. Simmer for 10-15 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld and the curry to thicken slightly. 8. Stir in the fresh spinach, cooking until just wilted, about 2-3 minutes. 9. Divide the basmati rice between two serving bowls. Ladle the hearty lentil and spinach curry over the rice. Garnish each serving with the toasted cashews and fresh cilantro if desired.

Spicy Roasted Chickpeas & Pepitas

🥜 26g protein/serving 🔪 5 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 850 g Canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 75 g Pepitas (pumpkin seeds)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Cayenne pepper
Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. Thoroughly drain and rinse the two cans of chickpeas. Pat them very dry with a clean kitchen towel or paper towels. This step is crucial for crispy chickpeas. 3. In a large bowl, combine the dried chickpeas, pepitas, olive oil, smoked paprika, chili powder, cumin powder, garlic powder, salt, and cayenne pepper. Toss everything together until the chickpeas and pepitas are evenly coated with the spices. 4. Spread the seasoned chickpeas and pepitas in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. Ensure they are not overcrowded to allow for even roasting. 5. Roast for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the chickpeas are golden brown and crispy. Keep a close eye on them during the last few minutes to prevent burning. 6. Remove from the oven and let cool completely on the baking sheet; they will crisp up further as they cool. Store any leftovers in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3-4 days. Divide into two equal portions for serving.

High-Protein White Bean & Artichoke Heart Pasta with Toasted Walnuts

🥜 35g protein/serving 🔪 15 min prep 🔥 20 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 180 g High-protein pasta (chickpea/lentil based)
  • 1.5 cup Canned cannellini beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 cup Marinated artichoke hearts, drained and chopped
  • 0.5 cup Walnuts, chopped
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 cup Vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 0.2 cup Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.5 tsp Red pepper flakes (optional)
Instructions
Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the high-protein pasta according to package directions until al dente. While the pasta cooks, toast the chopped walnuts in a dry skillet over medium heat for 3-5 minutes until fragrant, stirring frequently to prevent burning. Remove from heat and set aside. In a separate large pan or skillet, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium heat. Add the garlic powder and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant. Stir in the rinsed cannellini beans, chopped artichoke hearts, and vegetable broth. Simmer for 5-7 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld and the sauce to thicken slightly. Once the pasta is cooked, drain it and add it directly to the pan with the bean and artichoke mixture. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil, lemon juice, fresh parsley, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes (if using). Toss everything together thoroughly until the pasta is well coated. Divide into two bowls and sprinkle with the toasted walnuts before serving.

High-Protein Mexican-Inspired Black Bean & Tofu Fajita Bowl with Basmati Rice

🥜 32g protein/serving 🔪 20 min prep 🔥 25 min cook
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 400 g Extra-firm tofu, pressed and sliced into strips
  • 2 cup Cooked basmati rice
  • 1.5 cup Canned black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 2 whole Bell peppers (red and green), sliced
  • 2 tbsp Fajita seasoning (ensure no onion)
  • 2 tbsp Lime juice
  • 0.2 cup Fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1 whole Avocado, diced (optional)
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
Instructions
If not already cooked, prepare 2 cups of basmati rice according to package directions. Press the extra-firm tofu for at least 15-20 minutes, then slice it into 1/2-inch strips. Slice the bell peppers into similar strips. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet or cast-iron pan over medium-high heat. Add the tofu strips and cook for 8-10 minutes, flipping occasionally, until golden brown and slightly crispy. Remove the tofu from the pan and set aside. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil to the same pan. Add the sliced bell peppers and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring frequently, until they are tender-crisp. Return the tofu to the pan with the bell peppers. Sprinkle the fajita seasoning over the tofu and vegetables, tossing to coat evenly. Cook for another 2-3 minutes. To assemble the bowls, divide the cooked basmati rice among two serving bowls. Top with the seasoned tofu and bell pepper mixture, rinsed black beans, a generous squeeze of fresh lime juice, and chopped fresh cilantro. Add diced avocado if desired. Serve immediately.

High-Protein White Bean Dip with Cucumber & Bell Pepper Sticks

🥜 22g protein/serving 🔪 10 min prep
David: 2.0 servings Baska: 1.6 servings
Ingredients
  • 2.5 cup Canned great northern beans, rinsed and drained
  • 4 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice
  • 0.5 tsp Garlic powder
  • 0.5 tsp Cumin
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 30 ml Water (to adjust consistency)
  • 0.5 tsp Salt
  • 0.2 tsp Black pepper
